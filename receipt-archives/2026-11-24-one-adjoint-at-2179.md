# One adjoint supplies the trace bound at 2179

**24 November 2026.** For $\psi=\chi_{-2179}$ the [strict Selmer group](2026-11-24-two-classes-of-order-twenty-five.md) has length two. There is a genuine characteristic-zero cuspidal rank-three representation whose nonzero trace differences from $1+\Psi+\Psi^{-1}$ are all divisible by $5^3$. Its difference at the split prime 23 has valuation **exactly three**. Counting this representation once gives six on the right side of the released inequality. The two weight-two eigensystems that produce it give the same adjoint. ## A congruence on all Hecke operators

Let $\eta=\chi_{-7}$, $D=7\cdot2179=15253$, and $\chi=\eta\psi$. Use the primitive Eisenstein series $E_2^{1,\chi}$ with Fourier coefficient $1+\chi(p)p$ at good primes and first coefficient one. Its constant term at infinity is $-B_{2,\chi}/4=-41500$. Direct summation gives $B_{2,\chi}=D^{-1}\sum_{a=1}^{D}\chi(a)a^2=166000=125\cdot1328$. Billerey–Menares, Proposition 4 with characters $1,\chi$ and dilation one, gives zero at the other cusps unless the lower-left entry is divisible by $D$. In that case the same constant is multiplied by a unit. The primitive conductor leaves no missing Euler factor. [Constant terms at every cusp](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.01173v2#page=7) · [Integer sum](selmer-order-25/check_two_sums.py)

The integral $q$-expansion defines a form over $\mathbb Z_5$ by the $q$-expansion principle at tame level. The constant terms show that its reduction modulo 125 is cuspidal. More explicitly, on the smooth proper tame modular curve a weight-two cusp form is a regular differential. Global regular differentials commute with this base change by relative duality and connected smooth fibers. The character component is a direct summand since $5\nmid\varphi(D)$, so this mod-125 form comes from the integral cuspidal lattice. Its first Fourier coefficient is a unit. All Hecke relations on that lattice therefore give relations on the Eisenstein system modulo 125. A weak eigensystem over a finite ring can in general combine distinct characteristic-zero points. We check the precise local component before assigning the congruence to one form.

## Isolate the characteristic-zero points

The signed weight-two Manin symbols start with the $8\cdot2180=17440$ elements of $\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_7)\times\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_{2179})$. Rescaling a representative multiplies it by the quadratic character. In either sign we impose the two-term and three-term relations with unit pivots. This gives 1452 relative coordinates. The endpoint functions at the four cusps span two signed boundary coordinates in each sign. Quotienting the dual symbol space by those coordinates gives 1450 cuspidal coordinates. PARI/GP independently gives the same dimension with `mfdim([15253,2,znchar(15253)],1)`. The good-prime operators use the integral Heilbronn–Merel representatives. At the residual eigenvalue $T_2=-1$, the **whole** primary space has dimension two. The coordinate certificate gives a $1450\times2$ matrix $U$ with first two rows the identity. We verify $T_2U=1999U\bmod15625$ on all rows. On the quotient the matrix of $T_2+1$ reduces to $A_{JJ}-U_J A_{SJ}$ where $A=T_2+1$, $S=\{0,1\}$ and $J$ is the complement. All 1448 pivots exist modulo five. This checks exhaustiveness without trusting the method that found the coordinates. Hensel factorization for the characteristic polynomial splits the integral 5-adic lattice into the two primary summands. On the rank-two summand $T_3$ has two simple roots, congruent to 4 and 1 modulo five. At precision $5^6$ these are 14879 and 746. Each lifts to a distinct rank-one direct summand over $\mathbb Z_5$. The completed cuspidal Hecke algebra at the first residual system is therefore exactly $\mathbb Z_5$, acting on a single characteristic-zero eigenline. We checked both signs separately. The Eisenstein system chooses the line with $T_3=4\bmod5$. It follows that the characteristic-zero newform $f$ on that line has $a_p(f)\equiv1+\chi(p)p\bmod125$ for **all** good $p$. Primitivity of $\chi$ forces the full level. The unit eigenvalue at 5 gives ordinarity. In arithmetic normalization put $W=W_f$, $\det W=\chi\varepsilon$. By Chebotarev and continuity,
$$\operatorname{tr}W(g)\equiv1+\chi(g)\varepsilon(g)\pmod{125}\quad(g\in G_\mathbb Q).$$
Twisting by $\eta$ keeps the primitive level $D$. At 7 its inertia has one unramified and one quadratic character, and the twist interchanges them. At the other ramified prime it is unramified. Since $\eta(2)=1$ and $\eta(3)=-1$, the twist stays in the two-dimensional $T_2$ component and interchanges the two $T_3$ lines. Twisting changes neither the adjoint nor its restriction to $G_K$.

## The representation at the required level

Let $\rho=\operatorname{Ad}^0(W)|_{G_K}$, where $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$. The eigensystem is non-dihedral. A dihedral induction has trace zero on the nontrivial coset of a quadratic kernel. Its residual trace $1+\chi\omega$ would vanish there too. A character constant $-1$ on that coset would be trivial on the kernel and of order at most two, whereas $\chi\omega$ has order four on inertia at 5. The projective image is infinite by the distinct Hodge–Tate weights. In characteristic zero the remaining proper infinite algebraic subgroups of $\mathrm{PGL}_2$ either fix a line or preserve a pair of lines. The cuspidal, non-dihedral representation therefore has Zariski-dense projective image, also after the index-two restriction. Its adjoint is irreducible. The Gelbart–Jacquet adjoint transfer is cuspidal. Quadratic base change to $K$ remains cuspidal, since a quadratic self-twist of the rational transfer contradicts its trivial central character in odd dimension. It is regular algebraic and conjugate self-dual. With the release's Tate normalization $\rho$ itself reads the unitary Satake parameters. [Adjoint transfer and its cuspidality](https://math.uchicago.edu/~fcale/papers/Ramakrishnan.pdf) · [Cyclic base change and its fibers](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02513)

At $q$ the full inertia of the adjoint is $1,\psi,\psi$, with no monodromy. At 7 restriction to $K$ kills the quadratic inertia. At other places away from 5 it is unramified. At 5 it is crystalline ordinary with successive inertia characters $\varepsilon,1,\varepsilon^{-1}$. The Hecke computation gives $a_5=4621\bmod15625$. Since $\chi(5)=-1$, the unit root of $X^2-a_5X-5$ is $\nu(\mathrm{Fr}_5)=3376\bmod15625$. Its fourth power minus one is $13500\bmod15625$, of valuation exactly three. Over the unramified quadratic completion the unramified factor between adjacent flag characters is the inverse of this fourth power. It cannot disappear after any positive-degree restriction. Indeed $v_5(\nu^{4k}-1)=3+v_5(k)$ by the binomial expansion. This verifies the stronger potential unlinked condition directly on this eigensystem. For the additional `WitA` condition, the local normalized inducing characters have complex absolute value one at every finite place. At the ramified primes the two-dimensional local representation is principal series with one quadratic inertia character, and its normalized Frobenius eigenvalues have equal absolute value. At 5 use the pure crystalline roots. All supports of the transferred representation are therefore toral and unlinked, with real exponents zero. Any other attached cuspidal representation has the same good Satake parameters, hence is the same by strong multiplicity one. These verify the level, unlinked flag and automorphic witness required of this candidate. ## The prime 23 and the universal pointwise reading

On $G_K$ we have $\det W=\Psi$ since $\eta=1$. The trace identity on traceless endomorphisms gives
$$\operatorname{tr}\rho-(1+\Psi+\Psi^{-1})
 = \frac{(\operatorname{tr} W-(1+\Psi))(\operatorname{tr} W+(1+\Psi))}{\Psi}.$$
The denominator is a 5-adic unit and the first factor is divisible by 125. This gives a valuation of at least three whenever the difference is nonzero, for every group element in the release's pointwise test. For good split primes the two directions agree on this adjoint. Inverting Frobenius gives the same rank-three trace and the same target.

At 23 use $15524\equiv-101\bmod15625$. Subtraction gives $-125$ and addition gives $-77$, a unit. At 43 the displayed finite precision does not determine the exact valuation. The characteristic-zero difference is nonzero at every such prime $p\ge7$ since $|a_p|\le2\sqrt p<p-1$. The inequality in full has a noncoincidence premise covering **all** admissible representations. Whenever it applies, this singleton alone supplies a right side of at least six for the measured length two. The conclusion here is about this particular input.
