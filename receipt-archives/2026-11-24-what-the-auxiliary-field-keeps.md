# What survives the auxiliary field?

**24 November 2026.** The auxiliary field in the published propagation argument is abelian over $\mathbb Q$ and contains a cyclic field of degree 577 which removes the auxiliary character. Restriction–corestriction with an invertible degree is unavailable on that step. The live order-577 class nevertheless survives. The source also needs two *different* nonzero classes on this field. We checked all three on the actual coefficient characters. ## One class survives every abelian restriction

The [Jacobi witness](2026-11-24-the-577-class-on-paper.md) gives translation 333 at the split detecting prime 2363393 for the coefficient $z^{-1}$. At inertia $h$ at 2 with $\psi(h)=510\bmod577$, the coefficient scalar is 155. The commutator of $h$ with the detecting Frobenius therefore gives
$$(155-1)\,333=506\bmod577.$$
This element belongs to every abelian absolute subgroup. The full 577-adic coefficient character is one on it because it is a commutator. Hence no finite- or divisible-coefficient coboundary can change its value. In particular it cannot become zero simply because the field degree in the frame is divisible by 577.

## Check the two other signs at the same auxiliary prime

The source asks for classes of coefficients $\kappa_1=\eta z^{-1}$ and $\kappa_2=\eta z^{-2}$, split at 577 and allowed controlled inertia at an auxiliary $w$. Both are odd at complex conjugation. Over $\mathbb Q$ their unramified class-group components at the original level vanish, as witnessed by the nonzero multipliers **537 and 556**. These have an independent modular check: they equal $-4$ times the earlier Eisenstein constants 10 and 438, respectively, modulo 577. The vanishing about class groups follows from the divisor argument. [Tangent and multiplier calculation](2026-11-24-three-deformation-directions-at-577.md)

Now take any source witness prime $w$. It splits in the fields $M_i$ cut out by $\kappa_i$ and satisfies $w\equiv1\bmod577$. Each $M_i$ is cyclic CM of degree prime to 577. In the ray-class sequence for modulus $w$ over $M_i$, the 577-primary local residue groups form the regular module
$$(\mathbb Z/577^{s})[\operatorname{Gal}(M_i/\mathbb Q)],\qquad s=v_{577}(w-1).$$
This is because $w$ splits completely and the residue field at every prime is $\mathbb F_w$. On the $\kappa_i$ component global units contribute zero: its odd unit part is torsion and no 577-power root has the prescribed inertia action at 2. The ordinary class group also contributes zero by the table. The $\kappa_i$-component of the ray group therefore has **exact order $577^s$** and is cyclic. Its mod-577 character gives a nonzero cocycle over $\mathbb Q$, by prime-to-577 descent. The class is unramified at 577, and the prime classes there project to zero because the decomposition stabilizer contains nontrivial $\kappa_i$ inertia. Hence its local restriction there is zero. Away from $w$ it satisfies the source's unramified-class condition. Its inertia reading at $w$ must be nonzero, since an everywhere-unramified class in this sector was already excluded. [Exact cocycle required by the frame](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/FrobeniusSplitPrimeDatum.lean#L91-L95)

Normalize each of these two cocycles to read 1 on the same generator $t$ of the tame 577-part at $w$. This is possible since $\kappa_i$ is trivial on decomposition there. Its kernel on that inertia quotient is the same as that of the order-577 character $\theta$ used by the seed. Thus on the field killing $\theta$ its $w$ inertia disappears. The class itself survives: at $g=[c,t]$ both readings are $-2\bmod577$. At $hgh^{-1}$ they are $-2\cdot155$ and $-2\cdot368$, since $\kappa_1(h)=155$, $\kappa_2(h)=155^2=368$. The determinant of the two readings is $4(368-155)=275\bmod577$, a unit. The original even class is zero on both these words, being unramified at $w$, where its residual coefficient is trivial. Adding its separate detecting commutator gives a three-by-three determinant $506\cdot275=93\bmod577$, up to the order of rows and columns. This proves independence after passing to any further abelian compositum trivializing the three residual coefficients. The original two are precisely the different signs needed in the frame. None is supplied by counting another class twice. [Integer checks](marked-directions-577/residual_directions.py)

The cyclotomic layer used in `Upl` removes a principal-unit quotient at 577. The auxiliary prime factor is unramified there and the real quadratic factor agrees locally with $K$ at the chosen places. The separately allowed abelian 577-power factor has inertia at 577 trivial by its defining hypothesis. Thus the tame cyclotomic inertia used in the [local weight calculation](2026-11-24-the-companion-with-the-same-full-trace.md#check-the-width-locally) remains visible through this construction. This explains why introducing large local degrees here does not manufacture the two classes by collapsing their distinct residual characters. ## How large the frame must already be

The upper bound `C₀` chosen by the release measures the mod-577 unramified class-character rank after adjoining $K$, the field of $z$, and $\zeta_{577}$ to the real auxiliary field. With `XDual 1` it requires unramifiedness at all finite places and has no additional split-prime condition. In this example the measured field contains
$$F_m=K\,\mathbb Q(\zeta_{256})^+\,\mathbb Q(\zeta_{577^{m+1}})$$
and the degree-577 field $T_w$ cut out by $\theta$. This follows by intersecting the displayed coefficient kernels with `Hlay`. The base $F_0$ has degree $2\cdot64\cdot576=73728$ by the disjoint conductors, and $w$ splits completely there. For $s=v_{577}(w-1)$, the order of $w$ on the principal cyclotomic layer is $577^{\max(0,m+1-s)}$. Hence there are exactly $73728\cdot577^{\min(m,s-1)}$ primes of $F_m$ over $w$. Complex conjugation pairs them because $w$ splits in $K$. [What XDual tests](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/KummerTheory/Mk0IdMapCongr.lean#L90-L93) · [Measured rank](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisCochains/GLinkQuot.lean#L26-L33) · [Quantifier order](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/RankLeOfCardLe.lean#L137-L139) · [Actual cyclotomic layers](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/RankLeOfCardLe.lean#L171-L187)

In the ray sequence over $F_m$ the minus part of the local residue groups at $w$ has one cyclic 577-primary factor per conjugate pair. The minus part of the global units comes from roots of unity, hence removes at most one generator. The resulting subgroup of the minus ray group has mod-577 generator rank at least the number of pairs minus one. An ambient finite abelian 577-group has rank at least that of any subgroup, so at least that many independent mod-577 minus characters exist. Adjoining $T_w$ kills their ramification at $w$: $F_m$ is unramified there and the tame inertia quotient of order 577 is unique. Restriction through any further abelian extension over $\mathbb Q$ stays injective on this minus space. A character which died would factor through an abelian Galois quotient on which conjugation acts trivially, while on the character it acts by $-1$. These classes are unramified at all other finite places from their construction.

It follows that the actual rank is at least $36864\cdot577^{\min(m,s-1)}-1$. Since the source bounds every $m$, its constant must satisfy
$$C_0\ge36864\cdot577^{s-1}-1.$$

For a fixed $w$, this lower bound stops growing with $m$ at $s-1$. No bound uniform in the choice of $w$ is possible. The release chooses $w$ first, then $C_0$, then $m=3C_0+7$. Already at $s=1$ its selected $m$ is at least 110596. This verifies the dependence on the choice of auxiliary prime. An upper estimate on the later class ranks requires the separate tower argument. ## Unresolved

These computations verify the three indicated class directions and the effect of the auxiliary level at the fixed datum. They do not decide which components of the global ordinary determinant ring contain the non-self-dual point. That question is visible in the [two invariant equations](2026-11-24-two-equations-at-the-marked-point.md), and the source distinguishes it from the production of the [initial rational adjoint](2026-11-24-one-ramified-digit-at-the-auxiliary-prime.md).
