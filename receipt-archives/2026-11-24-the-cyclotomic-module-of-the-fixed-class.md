# The split and relaxed modules in the cyclotomic tower

**24 November 2026.** The two local conditions at the coefficient prime give different answers on the entire tower behind the order-577 class. In its fixed real character sector the everywhere-unramified module with the primes above 577 split is just **$\mathbb F_{577}$**. The module allowing ramification at 577 is **$\mathbb Z_{577}$** with a nontrivial cyclotomic action. Its finite-twist lengths are unbounded as the positive integers vary. The strict lengths are all one. This distinction is obtained from the [actual order-squared lifts and their local failures](2026-11-24-the-last-577th-power-digit.md).

Let $O=\mathbb Z_p$, $k=\mathbb F_p$, $p=577$, $z=\bar\psi\omega^{296}$, and $M$ the real degree-576 field cut out by $z$. Put $M_\infty=M\mathbb Q_\infty$ for the cyclotomic $\mathbb Z_p$-extension of $\mathbb Q$. Its group over $\mathbb Q$ is $\Delta\times\Gamma$, with $|\Delta|=576$ and $\Gamma\simeq\mathbb Z_p$. Write $\xi=\widetilde z^{-1}$ for the Teichmüller character of $\Delta$. Choose the generator of $\Gamma$ with principal cyclotomic value $1+p$, and write $\Lambda=O[[T]]$ with generator acting by $1+T$.

## Control on the same character

Let $X_R$ be the $\xi$ component of the maximal abelian pro-$p$ Galois group over $M_\infty$ unramified away from $p$. Let $X_S$ be its quotient also split at the primes over $p$. For a finite coefficient module with $\Delta$ acting by $\xi$ and $\Gamma$ by a specified principal unit, the relaxed or strict classes over $\mathbb Q$ are precisely the continuous $\Lambda$-homomorphisms from the corresponding $X$. Indeed restriction to $G_{M_\infty}$ trivializes the coefficients. The quotient has a central scalar whose difference from one is a unit, so positive-degree inflation and descent obstructions vanish. At $p$ the local quotient has such a scalar from the inertia image of order 72. Away from $p$ the finite inertia quotient in the tower has order prime to $p$, so the inertia condition descends. This proves the control assertion for both conditions.

The residual groups each have dimension one. A compact-module Nakayama argument makes both $X$ cyclic over $\Lambda$. In detail, a nonzero compact quotient would have a nonzero finite stable quotient, whose reduction has nonzero coinvariants for the pro-$p$ group. Thus a lift of the residual generator spans densely and its compact cyclic span is closed. Write $X_R=\Lambda/I_R$ and $X_S=\Lambda/I_S$ with $I_R\subset I_S\subset(p,T)$.

At $j=296+576u$ the specialization is
$$T=T_j=(1+p)^{-j}-1\equiv-jp\pmod{p^2}.$$
The order-$p^2$ lift exists if and only if all the relation forms in $I\bmod(p,T)^2$ vanish on $(1,-j)$ over $k$. For the relaxed condition, the divisor multiplier excludes all residues except $u=460\bmod p$, where a nonzero Jacobi lift realizes it. For the strict condition it exists at none. It follows that the linear part of $I_R$ is a line with a nonzero $T$ coefficient. The linear part of $I_S$ contains that line. A single such line always vanishes at one finite slope $(1,-j)$, and the integer twists cover all these slopes. Hence $I_S$ spans the whole cotangent space $(p,T)/(p,T)^2$ and Nakayama gives **$I_S=(p,T)$**. Equal lengths at integer twists alone would not give this conclusion. The extra relaxed lift rules out an ideal with only the linear form $p$ and a hidden nilpotent $T$ direction.

## An exact divisor test at every coefficient order

For $j\equiv296\bmod576$ let $F_n=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{256p^n})$ and define
$$K_n(j)=\sum_{\substack{128p^n<a<256p^n\\(a,256p)=1}}\psi(a)^{-1}a^{-(j+1)}\pmod{p^n}.$$
A relaxed class of order $p^n$ exists **if and only if $K_n(j)=0$**. We can prove both directions directly with ideals. The witness prime in the existence proof may depend on $n$.

For necessity, restrict the class to $F_n$ and use Kummer theory. Its radicand has valuations divisible by $p^n$ away from $p$ by the unramified condition. The same holds at $p$ because some element of tame inertia fixes the valuation and acts by a scalar whose difference from one is a unit. Dividing its principal divisor by $p^n$ preserves the order in the required odd character. The only possible odd unit contribution comes from roots of unity, and inertia at 2 fixes the $p$-power roots while acting nontrivially on this character. The upper-half ideal operator annihilates the ideal class group and acts on this divided divisor by $K_n(j)$. Its vanishing follows. These arguments work in the full cyclotomic field even when the wild character order drops. [Jacobi divisor identity](2026-11-24-why-the-classes-stop-at-twenty-five.md#a-divisor-identity-at-a-splitting-prime)

For sufficiency, take the already measured prime $P_0$ over 1920257 in $F_1$ with its specified cyclotomic embedding. At this prime the first-level Jacobi radicand is nonzero modulo $p$th powers. Its divided divisor has nonzero ideal class. For any split prime $P$ of $F_1$ the first-level divided divisor is obtained by applying the **same integral ideal operator** $WT_1/p$ to $P$. Here $T_1$ is the upper-half ideal operator and $W$ is the sum of conjugations weighted by the chosen lifts of the inverse radicand character. Modulo $p$, $W$ is a character eigenvector in the group ring and $WT_1=K_1(j)W=0$. Thus every coefficient of $WT_1$ is divisible by $p$. Primes in the ideal class of $P_0$ give that same nonzero divided-divisor class. [Original radicand, local test and independent detecting prime](2026-11-24-the-577-class-on-paper.md)

Let $H$ be the Hilbert class field of $F_1$. The extension $F_n/F_1$ is totally ramified at each place over $p$, while $H/F_1$ is unramified. Their intersection is $F_1$. In $\operatorname{Gal}(HF_n/F_1)$ choose the Artin element of $[P_0]$ on $H$ and the identity on $F_n$. Both fields are Galois over $\mathbb Q$. Chebotarev supplies a rational prime $r$ and a choice of prime above it with that Frobenius. Thus $r$ splits completely in $F_n$ and its chosen prime in $F_1$ is in precisely the required ideal class. Use a compatible finite-field primitive root to define the Jacobi sums at levels 1 and $n$. [Hilbert class field and Chebotarev, Milne’s Class Field Theory](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf)

Now form the level-$n$ Jacobi radicand with weights $(\psi\varepsilon^{j+1})^{-1}$ modulo $p^n$. The carries at $r$ give $K_n(j)$ and its unit multiples at the conjugate primes. All valuations are divisible by $p^n$ if $K_n(j)=0$. Away from $p$ the only extra inertia in the cyclotomic field is at 2 and has order prime to $p$, so the relaxed condition descends. To reduce the radicand modulo $p$th powers, take the norm of the Jacobi sum to $F_1$. The $p^{n-1}$ carries over each old residue sum to the old carry plus $(p^{n-1}-1)/2$. The norm therefore agrees with the first-level Jacobi sum times $r^{(p^{n-1}-1)/2}$ up to a root of unity, by equality of divisors and of all complex absolute values. The projected weights kill the rational factor and the possible root-of-unity class by the same inertia-at-2 argument. The residual class is the nonzero one just selected. It stays nonzero on restriction to $F_n$ by the central residual scalar. Multiplication by $p^{n-1}$ on the coefficient module is reduction followed by the residual inclusion, which is injective on global $H^1$ because the coefficient quotient has no invariants. The resulting relaxed class has order exactly $p^n$.

## Locate the relaxed center by finite sums

Write $j=296+576u$. Stacking the upper-half carries above an old residue also shows $K_{n+1}(j)\equiv K_n(j)\bmod p^n$. The added constant multiplies a complete character sum, which is zero by the nontrivial 256-part. For $n\ge2$, shifting $u$ by $d p^{n-2}$ changes $K_n$ modulo $p^n$ by
$$540\,d\,p^{n-1}.$$
Indeed put $b(a)=(a^{-576}-1)/p\bmod p$. The binomial theorem gives the difference divided by $p^{n-1}$ as $d$ times the upper-half sum of $\bar\psi(a)^{-1}a^{-297}b(a)$ modulo $p$. This weight depends only on $a\bmod256p^2$. Grouping the carries down to level two leaves its old upper-half sum, since the sum on all units again vanishes by the 256-part. The level-two value is 540. [Independent second-level sum](fixed-character-577/bounds.py)

There is consequently one zero residue for $u\bmod p^{n-1}$ at every level, and they are compatible. They determine $u_*\in\mathbb Z_p$ and $j_*=296+576u_*$. At every positive integer twist in the family with $j\ne j_*$ the relaxed length is $1+v_p(j-j_*)$. Equality gives a divisible relaxed group. These conclusions follow from the divisor test and the residual rank one.

## Recover the relaxed cyclotomic module

Set $\alpha=(1+p)^{-j_*}-1$. For every $n$, take a positive integer $j_n$ at the level-$n$ zero. The existence of a relaxed class of order $p^n$ gives a surjection $X_R\to O/p^n$ with $T$ acting as $T_{j_n}$. Every $f\in I_R$ therefore satisfies $f(\alpha)=0\bmod p^n$, since $T_{j_n}\equiv\alpha\bmod p^n$. It follows that $I_R\subseteq(T-\alpha)$. The order-squared test already gave an element of $I_R$ with unit linear $T$ coefficient. Dividing that element by $T-\alpha$ gives a unit of $O[[T]]$. Hence **$I_R=(T-\alpha)$**. This derivation uses the existence of a prime in a specified ideal class at each finite level.

The first digit is $\alpha\equiv164p\bmod p^2$ because $j_*\equiv413\bmod577$. Thus $X_R$ is free of rank one over $O$ with $\Gamma$ acting by $1+\alpha$. Its quotient $X_S=k$ shows that killing the local decomposition subgroup kills exactly $pO$. The tame inertia stabilizer acts nontrivially on $\xi$ and fixes the residue-field quotient, so this kernel is generated by the images of local inertia.

## What persists and what capitulates

At finite $M_n=M\mathbb Q_{\ell,n}$, where the rational layer has degree $p^n$, the same argument works with $\Gamma^{p^n}$ in place of $\Gamma$. In the $\xi$-sector unramified class-group characters automatically vanish on local Frobenius above $p$ because its prime classes have nontrivial tame stabilizer. The unramified $p$-class group in this sector is therefore the coinvariants of $X_S=k$, again $C_p$. The norm maps between these class sectors are isomorphisms. Their duals are restriction of characters, which takes the generator to the same surviving class. Extension of ideals in the opposite direction followed by norm is multiplication by $p$, hence **each such ideal class becomes principal in the next layer** while the class-field character remains detectable there. The [complete class-group computation](2026-11-24-the-affine-resolvent-and-its-class-groups.md) checks the other character sectors as well.
