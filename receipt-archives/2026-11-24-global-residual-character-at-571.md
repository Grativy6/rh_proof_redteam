# Identifying the global residue of the four lifts

**24 November 2026.** For the [four ordinary cuspidal systems](2026-11-24-matching-the-unitary-level.md) at $q=571$, the semisimplified residual Galois representation over $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$ is
$$\bar r^{\,ss}=1\oplus\psi\omega\oplus\psi\omega^{-1},\qquad
  \psi=\chi_{-571},\quad\omega=\varepsilon_5\bmod5.$$
This identification needs more than agreement at the ten split primes tested. Below we exclude a rank-two constituent, then the possible irreducible three-dimensional competitors. The latter require different arguments for odd and even projective extensions. Together with the [exact local coefficient fields](2026-11-24-two-ramified-hecke-fields.md), this independently accounts for the pointwise characteristic-zero trace budget on the order-five input.

## Information available before identifying the representation

Use the normalized characteristic-zero representation $\rho$ of $G_K$ whose geometric Frobenius eigenvalues are the unitary Satake parameters. It is absolutely irreducible, conjugate self-dual and has determinant one by the [local-type and transfer check](2026-11-24-matching-the-unitary-level.md). At 5 it is crystalline ordinary. Over the unramified quadratic completion $K_5$ its residual flag on inertia has characters $\omega,1,\omega^{-1}$ in order from submodule to quotient. Intersecting a characteristic-zero ordinary flag with a stable lattice and passing to its saturated successive quotients gives the same order on a reduction. Any Jordan–Hölder subquotient inherits the corresponding ordered subfiltration on this local group. At the two primes over 571 inertia has order two and its fixed line reads $+1$ on Frobenius. It is unramified at all other primes of $K$ away from 5.

All good Hecke operations preserve the common residual eigenline. They act on it through $\mathbb F_5$. The adjoint preserves the localized integral algebra and induces the identity on this residue field. Therefore the two trace coefficients $a_w,b_w$ of the normalized cubic are equal modulo 5 for **every** good split rational prime. At those primes the polynomial is self-reciprocal and defined over $\mathbb F_5$. Such places have density one among primes of $K$. Chebotarev and Brauer–Nesbitt give
$$\bar r^{\,ss}\simeq(\bar r^{\,ss})^\vee
                    \simeq(\bar r^{\,ss})^c.$$
These implications require the same eigensystem throughout. Also, a polynomial with a root 1 at every Frobenius does not by itself supply a global fixed vector. An irreducible orthogonal three-dimensional representation illustrates exactly that danger.

## Reducible possibilities

If all three constituents are characters, their exponents on inertia at 5 are $j=1,0,-1$. After dividing by $\omega^j$, each is unramified there. At the two primes above 571 it can only be trivial or quadratic. The two quadratic choices must occur together for a global character since their individual signs on the unit $-1$ are negative since $571\equiv3\bmod4$, and all other finite units contribute trivially after removing cyclotomic. The field has class number one. Hence each constituent is $\omega^j\psi^\delta$, $\delta=0,1$, on $G_K$. Self-duality pairs the two outside exponents with equal $\delta$. Determinant one forces the middle character to be trivial. The two quadratic inertia eigenvalues at 571 then force both remaining $\delta$ to be one.

Suppose instead there is an irreducible two-dimensional constituent $B$. The single linear constituent is self-dual and has exponent zero at 5, since the other exponents have order four there. The possibility $\psi$ is excluded by the eigenvalues $1,2,3$ at 2 and $\psi(2)=-1$. Thus $\det B=1$. It has its usual alternating determinant pairing. It is invariant under $c$, so an intertwiner for complex conjugation has scalar square by Schur’s lemma. Scaling it to have square one extends the representation to $G_{\mathbb Q}$. This extension is **odd**. The characteristic-zero conjugate-self-dual representation has positive polarization sign because it is irreducible of odd dimension for an involutive complex conjugation. Its reduction has multiplicity-free constituents in the case under consideration. Bellaïche–Chenevier’s sign specialization, Proposition 2.4, gives a positive conjugate-duality sign on $B$. If the extending involution acted scalarly, its conjugate-duality form would be a scalar multiple of the alternating determinant form and would have negative sign. A nonscalar involution in dimension two has determinant $-1$. [Bellaïche–Chenevier, sign under specialization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0804.2860v1#page=7)

Twist that extension by $\psi\omega$. The twist removes the scalar quadratic inertia at 571. At 7 its inertia factors through the quadratic extension and has opposite signs there, since the determinant of the untwisted extension is $\eta=\chi_{-7}$. At 5 the inherited ordinary flag has the ramified subcharacter of exponent two and unramified quotient after the twist. The distinct residual inertia characters make the ordered line descend across the unramified quadratic local extension. In a nonsplit extension it is the unique line, and in the split case Frobenius preserves the two distinct characters. The resulting Serre weight is 3, with no adjacent-weight exception. The refined Serre modularity theorem gives a weight-three mod-five cusp form of level 7. That space has dimension one also in characteristic five: the tame genus-zero curve $X_1(7)$ has the same six regular cusps and degree-two Hodge bundle, and the integral eta product remains nonzero. At split 11 the twist has required trace 2, while the only cusp form has $a_{11}=-6\equiv4$. This excludes $B$. [Khare–Wintenberger, Serre’s modularity theorem](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~shekhar/papers/results.pdf) · [The level-seven calculation](2026-11-24-the-rank-two-alternative-at-571.md)

## An absolutely irreducible residue would extend projectively to $\mathbb Q$

An absolutely irreducible $\bar r$ can be defined over $\mathbb F_5$ because its characteristic polynomials are rational over that field and the Brauer group of a finite field is zero. Self-duality in odd dimension gives a nondegenerate symmetric form, which can be taken over $\mathbb F_5$. Thus the image is in $\mathrm{SO}_3(\mathbb F_5)\simeq\mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_5)\simeq S_5$. Since $\bar r^c\simeq\bar r$, solve for an intertwiner over $\mathbb F_5$. It is an orthogonal similitude by Schur’s lemma. In dimension three its multiplier is a square by taking determinants. There is a unique choice of scalar multiple in $\mathrm{SO}_3$. Its square centralizes the image and is therefore the identity. This extends $\bar r$ to $R$ on $G_{\mathbb Q}$ with values in that same group.

The image on $G_K$ contains an element of order four at 2. An intransitive subgroup of $S_5$ containing a four-cycle fixes the fifth letter and lies in $S_4$. Its possibilities of order at most eight have no absolutely irreducible three-dimensional representation, and the index-two $A_4$ has no four-cycle. A transitive subgroup has order divisible by 20. At orders 20 or 40 its Sylow 5-subgroup is normal. A normal 5-group has nonzero invariants in characteristic five, which by irreducibility would be the whole space, contradicting faithfulness. The index-two $A_5$ has no four-cycle. Hence the image is $S_4$ or $S_5$. The image on $G_{\mathbb Q}$ is the same group because the point stabilizer $S_4$ is its own normalizer in $S_5$.

View $R$ as a projective two-dimensional representation. Tate’s vanishing of $H^2(G_{\mathbb Q},\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z)$ gives it a finite linear lift $U$ over $\overline{\mathbb F}_5$, since $\overline{\mathbb F}_5^\times$ is the prime-to-five direct summand with trivial central action. We fix its scalar inertia by one Dirichlet twist as follows. At primes outside $5,7,571$ inertia is scalar. At 571 the two inertia eigencharacters have quadratic quotient. Frobenius preserves them because swapping the two lines in dimension two acts by $-1$ on the fixed axis of the adjoint, while the [parahoric measurement](2026-11-24-matching-the-unitary-level.md#frobenius-on-the-unramified-line-at-571) gave $+1$. At 7 projective inertia has order at most two because its intersection with $G_K$ is trivial. If nontrivial, its eigenlines cannot be interchanged. An interchanging Frobenius would have projective order two, whereas a Frobenius lift inside $G_K$ has order four there. Thus at each of these two places we can remove one inertia character. A Frobenius-invariant inertia character extends to the local Weil group, for example by prescribing the value on a Frobenius lift. Local class field theory and the Chinese remainder theorem realize the inverse finite unit characters in a single global twist. [Serre–Tate projective lifting and local choices, Theorem 4.2.2](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/~mascotn/research/Companion.pdf)

At 5 the projective tame inertia quotient has order four. With trivial wild image, its two eigenlines are individually invariant under Frobenius and can be ordered with ratio $\omega$ on inertia. With nontrivial wild image, the wild group has order five and fixes a unique line in dimension two. Its invariant subspace in the traceless adjoint is exactly the upper nilpotent line. The first line of the ordinary flag in $\bar r$ has inertia character $\omega$ and is fixed by wild inertia. So the ratio of subcharacter to quotient on inertia in $U$ is precisely $\omega$. In both cases we can twist to make the quotient unramified, with subcharacter of inertia exponent one. The globally normalized determinant is now
$$\det U=\omega\psi\eta^t,\qquad t=0\ \text{or}\ 1,$$
where $t=1$ exactly when the extension $R$ has nontrivial inertia at 7. This follows by matching the local unit characters everywhere since $\mathbb Q$ has no nontrivial character unramified at every finite prime. Both $\omega$ and $\psi$ are odd.

## The odd alternative fails a two-prime modular test

When $t=1$, $U$ is odd and absolutely irreducible with prime-to-five conductor $3997=7\cdot571$ and primitive residual nebentypus $\chi_{3997}=\eta\psi$. Its Serre weight is 2. The potential weight-six exception for an upper triangular adjacent-weight ordinary representation is the très ramifiée Kummer case. After removing a common unramified character this would have a Kummer class with nonzero valuation modulo five. In the wild affine group of order 20 one may then raise the Kummer class to a power prime to five and remove a fifth power to obtain a representative of valuation one. The Eisenstein quintic $x^5-a$ has different exponent 9 from its derivative, hence the lower break is $9-4=5$. The same calculation can be made after an unramified extension to remove scalar factors. But the upper break on this projective image is at most $1/2$ by the crystalline bound for the adjoint of weight width two. This excludes the exception. The tame or unramified-scalar alternatives have the ordinary weight two directly. [Abrashkin’s crystalline ramification theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.3861#page=7) · [Khare–Wintenberger](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~shekhar/papers/results.pdf)

For a rational split prime $p$ in our Hecke table, Serre modularity would give
$$a_p^2=(1+p\psi(p))^2\pmod5.$$
Indeed the adjoint trace is $a_p^2/\det U(\mathrm{Frob}_p)-1$, the determinant is $p\psi(p)$ at these primes, and the measured normalized trace is $1+\psi(p)(p+p^{-1})$. Already **$a_2^2=1$ and $a_{29}=0$ are incompatible** in the cuspidal space.

The primitive-character newspace has dimension 378. PARI/GP 2.15.4 gave dimensions $4,4,4,0$ on imposing the squared trace conditions successively at $2,11,23,29$. All four full Hecke matrices had denominator $10344335408$, a unit modulo 5. There is no old subspace because the nebentypus already has full conductor. For the use of characteristic-zero cusp forms at this tame level, the 5-Sylow diamond subgroup acts freely on $X_1(3997)$ because elliptic stabilizers in characteristic 5 have order prime to 5 and cusp widths are prime to 5. The mod-five character is trivial on that subgroup. Weight-two cusp forms are regular differentials, which descend on this étale quotient and commute with base change on the resulting smooth proper modular curve. The remaining diamond group has order prime to five, so its character projector lifts. Thus the specified mod-five Hecke system would also occur as the reduction of a characteristic-zero system in this character space.

There is a quick independent check using modular symbols. The 4,576 Manin symbols use bottom rows in $\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_7)\times\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_{571})$, with the product of the two Legendre symbols for the rescaling. Impose the relations $1+S$ and $1+U+U^2$. The dual relative space has dimension 760. The four cusps are indexed by whether their denominator vanishes at each of $7,571$. For a primitive column $(a,c)$ the cusp sign at $p$ is the Legendre symbol of $c$ when $p\nmid c$ and of $a$ otherwise. This gives four independent boundary columns. Quotienting the dual relative symbols by their span gives a space dual to cuspidal homology. The Heilbronn–Merel formula evaluates $T_p$ by the integral matrices of determinant $p$ with $a>b\ge0,\ d>c\ge0$. On each putative symbol require $(T_2^2-I)f$ and $T_{29}f$ to lie in the boundary span, allowing four independent nuisance coefficients for each condition. The resulting system on 4,584 coordinates has rank 4,580. Its four-dimensional solution projects precisely onto the cusp boundary span and gives **zero** in the cuspidal quotient. This independent calculation works directly in the residue field. In characteristic zero the cuspidal modular symbols contain the holomorphic forms, and reduction of the integral Hecke lattice preserves occurrence of their residual systems. When $t=0$, complex conjugation in $U$ has determinant one. A two-dimensional involution in odd characteristic with determinant one is scalar, so the projective extension is totally real, unramified outside 5 and 571. The [complete field search](2026-11-24-quintic-check-for-the-even-alternative.md) rules out both $S_4$ and $S_5$ in this case. Thus there is no absolutely irreducible residual competitor.

## Consequence for the exact source inequality

All other cases excluded, the three global residual characters are as stated. On every $\sigma\in G_K$ the characteristic-zero trace of each lift is congruent to $t_\sigma=\Psi(\sigma)+1+\Psi(\sigma)^{-1}$ with $\Psi=\psi\varepsilon_5$. The two computed ramified quadratic coefficient completions contain the traces on every group element by continuity and density of good Frobenius conjugacy classes. The target lies in $\mathbb Z_5$. Whenever the actual noncoincidence premise requires the difference to be nonzero, **each** of the four lifts gives
$$v_5(\operatorname{tr}\rho(\sigma)-t_\sigma)\ge\tfrac12.$$
The source needs $1\le2\sum v_5(\operatorname{tr}\rho(\sigma)-t_\sigma)$ on this input. Even one of these lifts meets that numerical requirement. Equality for a single lift occurs at 29. At 11 every lift gives valuation 1. This verifies a genuine protection from outside the disputed proof, with the same global character, field, twist and trace normalization.
