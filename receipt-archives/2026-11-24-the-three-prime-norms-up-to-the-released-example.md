## A prime with an elementary certificate

Write $A=413718278401$, $B=603339156001$. The released prime $T$ satisfies
$$T-1=2^{12}3^{10}571^2 599^3 A^4 B^4.$$
The smaller certificates use bases 34 and 17 respectively and the factorizations
$$A-1=2^8 3^3 5^2 7\cdot571\cdot599,\qquad
B-1=2^5 3^2 5^3 7^2\cdot571\cdot599.$$
For the large certificate the base is 5. Repeated squaring gives $5^{T-1}=1\bmod T$. For each of the six distinct prime divisors $r$ of $T-1$ the calculation gives $5^{(T-1)/r}\ne1\bmod T$. The same checks with the stated smaller bases prove $A$ and $B$ after trial division proves the leaves. Thus the multiplicative order of 5 modulo $T$ is $T-1$. Those powers already account for all $T-1$ nonzero residue classes. A composite modulus would have a nonzero nonunit, so $T$ is prime. ## A finite result for the same construction

Set $M=16\cdot571\cdot599\,(AB)^2$. In $K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-599})$, put $\alpha_k=1+kM\sqrt{-599}$. Its quadratic norm is
$$T_k=(1+kM\sqrt{-599})(1-kM\sqrt{-599})=1+599(kM)^2.$$
The source chooses $k=972$. This gives the displayed prime and supplies an algebraic integer congruent to one modulo $M$. [The released constant and modulus](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/SpanPowerMembership/ExampleLayerExistsGenerators.lean#L264-L286) · [Norm and congruence](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/SpanPowerMembership/ExampleLayerExistsGenerators.lean#L300-L325)

**Finite classification.** For integers $1\le k\le972$, the prime values of this particular polynomial occur exactly at **363, 663 and 972**.

**Proof by reproducible integer calculation.** For 969 of the 972 candidates, $2^{T_k-1}\bmod T_k$ differs from one, proving compositeness. The other three pass the full Lucas order test with base 5. In their predecessor factorizations the common part is $571^2 599^3 A^4 B^4$. The remaining factors are respectively $2^8 3^2 11^4$, $2^8 3^2 13^2 17^2$, and $2^{12}3^{10}$. The [standalone checker](check_release_prime_large.py) multiplies these factors and executes all the order tests. The range is a reconstruction by this audit. The repository records only the choice 972 for this example.

The norm has a concrete geometric interpretation. The source host is $E=\mathbf Q(\zeta_{16})^+(\sqrt{-571},\sqrt{-599})$ of degree 16. The real cyclotomic quartic is disjoint from the biquadratic field: its possible quadratic overlap is $\mathbf Q(\sqrt2)$, whereas the positive quadratic radicand of the latter is $571\cdot599$. Since $T\equiv1\bmod16\cdot571\cdot599$, it splits completely in $E$. In $K$ the principal ideal $(\alpha_{972})$ selects just one of the two primes over $T$, since its norm is prime. Extending this ideal to $E$ selects exactly the eight primes over that choice. The absolute norm of $\alpha_{972}$ from $E$ is $T^8$. The source uses its quadratic identity $\alpha_{972}\bar\alpha_{972}=T$. ## Which information reaches the RH argument

The example host lists the level primes $751,991,A,B,T$. Its pair $(571,599)$ would fail the moving-window test $q\mid d+1$, since $600\bmod571=29$. Both statements concern actual objects in the release. Following the variables explains why this arithmetic does not falsify the window argument. [Example level list](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/SpanPowerMembership/ExampleLayerExistsGenerators.lean#L223-L242) · [Moving pair required in the window](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L27-L46)

The example uses $T$ and its norm generator to inhabit the record called `D7`. To build this record the constructor also receives a package $G$ consisting of a local theory, a family of period-ring objects and comparison data for every prime. All three fields of $G$ are independent of the example host. The constructor sets its three corresponding fields equal to those of $G$. Projecting back to these fields returns $G$ exactly. One can verify this identity by reading the constructor assignments. The supplier constructs $G$ before combining it with the numerical example. [Local package and its projections](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/SpanPowerMembership/ExampleClassGroupCardPos.lean#L21-L36) · [Three assignments](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/SpanPowerMembership/ExampleClassGroupCardPos.lean#L173-L181) · [Supplier independent of $T$](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/ExistsLocTheoryTied.lean#L8-L11)

In the released dependency path the prime theorem supplies `examplePrime_prime`, then an instance for `exampleRecord`, then existence of a `D7`. The global Wach-lattice argument chooses that record. Its only projections from the chosen record are precisely the three local fields. It applies them at the **input** coefficient prime $\ell$ and on the **input** number field, with its own embeddings and Galois representations. Consequently this dependency carries a valid existence witness. The value of $T$, the eight primes selected by $\alpha_{972}$, and the pair $(571,599)$ supply no numerical bound there. The remaining obligations belong to the local comparison on the actual input. ## Reproduction

Run `python -B check_release_prime_large.py` from the receipt archive for the arithmetic. Pass `--source PATH_TO_UPSTREAM_GIT_REPO` to repeat the source census at the pinned revision. It inspects 175,187 Lean files, compares same-line decimal `Nat.Prime n` assertions, verifies the named winner in its file, and searches for the separate decimal tokens 577 and 2663. Neither token occurs in these release sources. Symbolically defined values and the subsequent community edits are outside that last search.

## Unresolved
