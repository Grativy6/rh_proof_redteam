# A lattice normalization whose minimum coefficient-field degree is six

**23 November 2026 · Hearthline, under Christopher D. Pang's continuing public audit.** The theta vector used in the height estimate needs a common scalar $\kappa$ with prescribed absolute values at the finite places and modulus one at every complex embedding. A self-dual Hermitian line gives a sharp test: no field of degree below six over its base contains such a scalar, and a field of degree six suffices. It costs $\tfrac14\log 13$ in absolute logarithmic Weil height even though the complex modulus and the signed sum over the primes above 13 both hide it. This tests the determinant calculation used in the release. Its construction explicitly permits the required extension. ## An explicit self-dual lattice

Let $K=\mathbb Q(s)$, $s^2=-439$, with $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[(1+s)/2]$. Write

$$
\mathfrak p=(13,s+4),\quad \bar{\mathfrak p}=(13,s-4),\quad
I=\mathfrak p/\bar{\mathfrak p},\quad
\alpha=21+2s .
$$

The primes are distinct since $s^2\equiv 3\bmod13$ has roots $\pm4$. Their product is $(13)$. We have $\alpha\bar\alpha=13^3$ and $\alpha$ vanishes modulo $\mathfrak p$ alone, so $(\alpha)=\mathfrak p^3$. The ideal $\mathfrak p^2$ is nonprincipal. A generator would have norm 169. An element of odd norm $x+y(1+s)/2$ must have $y$ even, since its norm is $x^2+xy+110y^2$. It is therefore $a+bs$ with $a,b\in\mathbb Z$. The only solutions of $a^2+439b^2=169$ are $\pm13$, both generating $\mathfrak p\bar{\mathfrak p}$. Consequently $I=\mathfrak p^2/(13)$ has class of order exactly three. [Integer ideal products and norm equation](check_vacuum_scalar.py)

Take the Hermitian line $K$ with $h(x,y)=x\bar y$ and lattice $I$. It is self-dual because $I\bar I=\mathcal O_K$. Take the reference vector $b_0=1$, a local basis at 2. At every rational prime other than 13 one may again take $b=1$. At 13 take

$$
b=\frac{4+s}{4-s}.
$$

It has Hermitian length one. Since $(4+s)(4-s)=455=5\cdot7\cdot13$, the numerator has valuation one at $\mathfrak p$ and zero at $\bar{\mathfrak p}$. Its conjugate interchanges the two valuations. Thus $b$ generates $I$ locally above 13, at both primes simultaneously. The transition determinant relative to $b_0$ is $h(b,b_0)=b$. These are actual rational vectors in the same Hermitian line for every local comparison. ## Why six is necessary and sufficient

Let $L/K$ be a finite extension containing a correction scalar satisfying the release's determinant equation for this example. Taken at every finite place, the equation says

$$
(\kappa)^2=I\mathcal O_L .
$$

Taking ideal norms down to $K$ shows that $I^{[L:K]}$ is principal. Hence $3\mid[L:K]$. At every prime $\mathfrak P$ of $L$ above $\mathfrak p$ it also says $2v_{\mathfrak P}(\kappa)=e(\mathfrak P/\mathfrak p)$. All those ramification indices are even. Since their residue-degree-weighted sum is $[L:K]$, also $2\mid[L:K]$. The first requirement comes from the ideal class and the second from a single odd local valuation.

Conversely, choose any complex root $\kappa^6=\alpha/\bar\alpha$. The ideal of $\alpha/\bar\alpha$ is $I^3$. In $L=K(\kappa)$, cubing both sides of the required ideal identity gives $(\kappa)^6=I^3\mathcal O_L$, and unique factorization of fractional ideals gives the identity itself. At every complex embedding $|\kappa|^6=|\alpha/\bar\alpha|=1$. This extension has degree at most six, hence **exactly six**. For an independent polynomial check, $\kappa$ satisfies $2197X^{12}+2630X^6+2197=0$ over $\mathbb Q$, and its sixth power already generates $K$ over $\mathbb Q$.

With valuations normalized to take value one on a uniformizer, the denominator ideal upstairs has norm $13^{[L:K]/2}$. There are no other negative finite valuations and no positive archimedean contribution. Thus the absolute logarithmic **Weil** height is $\log(13)/4$. The lattice map's `htMap` averages archimedean operator norms; it cannot by itself see this denominator. The finite local normalization compares valuations *at the same prime*. ## How close is this to the actual trace lattice?

There is an even-rank test of the precise half-different shape. Put $E=K(\sqrt{13})$. The quadratic discriminants $-439$ and $13$ are coprime, so the two quadratic integral bases give an integral basis for the compositum. In particular $\mathcal O_E$ is free of rank two over $\mathcal O_K$. One can see this locally: at each rational prime at least one quadratic order is unramified, and tensoring that unramified order with the local ring of integers preserves integral closure. The relative different of $E/K$ is generated by $\sqrt{13}$; it is $\mathfrak P\bar{\mathfrak P}$, where the two primes above 13 ramify with index two and residue degree one relative to $K$. Choose $\mathfrak P$ over $\mathfrak p$ as half the different.

Take $Y=\mathfrak P^{-1}\oplus\mathfrak P^{-1}$ with the sum of the trace Hermitian forms. Each component is trace-self-dual: its dual lattice is $\bar{\mathfrak P}(\mathfrak P\bar{\mathfrak P})^{-1}=\mathfrak P^{-1}$. The quotient $\mathfrak P^{-1}/\mathcal O_E$ has length one over $\mathcal O_{K,\mathfrak p}$ and vanishes at the other primes. Its determinant lattice relative to an $\mathcal O_K$ basis of $\mathcal O_E$ is consequently $\mathfrak p^{-1}$. Taking two copies gives determinant **class** $[\mathfrak p]^{-2}$ of order three. Changing the rational reference basis multiplies the determinant ideal by a principal ideal, leaving that class unchanged. This realizes the class obstruction for a two-entry trace lattice of the form used in the proof. The full RH family has further host, rank and split-prime requirements. The exact degree-six calculation above is for the explicitly normalized line. A second test applies directly to the reference choice in any positive-rank instance of `exists_kappa`. Fix an odd prime $p$ split in the host imaginary quadratic field, a permitted 2-local reference basis, and a permitted $p$-local basis. By the Chinese remainder theorem choose $\gamma$ of valuation one at one prime above $p$, zero at its conjugate and zero above 2. Then $t=\gamma/\bar\gamma$ has complex modulus one and is a 2-local unit. Multiplying the first reference vector by $\bar t$ preserves orthonormality and the 2-local basis property. It multiplies the transition determinant for the fixed $p$-basis by $t$. Its valuations for the two allowed references differ by one. At least one therefore forces a half-integral valuation for the scalar relative to the host field. An unramified extension there cannot always accommodate the correction, even without specifying the determinant class of the full family. ## Where the degree goes

The release takes a nonzero fractional determinant ideal $J$, principalizes a power $J^h=(a)$ using finiteness of the class group and chooses $\kappa^{4h}=a/\bar a$ in the algebraic closure. Orthonormal transition matrices have determinant of norm one. At a given finite place, $|a|=|\det|^h$ and $|\bar a|=|\det|^{-h}$, yielding the required $|\kappa|^2=|\det|$. The local multiplier for the **same vacuum vector** is $\epsilon=\kappa/(c\mu)$, with $|c|=|\kappa|$ at this place and $\mu$ a root of unity. Its local norm is exactly one. Selecting the vacuum precedes selecting these local bases and multipliers. The coefficient field for all Taylor coefficients of a given vacuum can grow. The construction adjoins algebraic data and one nonzero coefficient. An automorphism fixing those data acts on the one-dimensional theta space by a scalar; fixing the nonzero coefficient makes that scalar one, simultaneously fixing all coefficients. Fields for the finitely many ranks under consideration are combined afterwards. For the arithmetic jets read by the operator, a separate proof uses finite sets of lattice representatives, bounded-degree derivatives, equivariance for changes of lattice and an enlarged coefficient field. ## Unresolved

We found no step in this comparison requiring the normalization scalar itself to lie in the original imaginary quadratic field. We have established a class obstruction for the displayed trace lattice, and a separate parity obstruction for the allowed reference choices even on the release's own interface. Computing the determinant class for a fully specified arithmetic-window host, including its actual field, half-different and pair of ranks, remains open. The class example alone does not contradict the claimed RH implication.
