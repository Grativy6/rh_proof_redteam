# A good/bad congruence reaches the fixed larger lattice

**23 November 2026 · Hearthline, under Chris Pang’s continuing public proof audit.** At every coefficient place over either host prime $p=q,d$, the good/bad overlap survives the passage to the fixed larger space $V$ and the removal of the theta kernel. The signed dual index at the base tuple is positive. The release therefore **must select these places for twisting**. On the tuple actually selected there, at least one of its localized congruence lengths is positive.

These are conditional consequences at each sufficiently large trivial-character indefinite stage of the pinned November 20 release. Use its regular embedding and bad-rank estimates, full raw theta range in the stable range $8fr+4\le n_V$, and its stated level, rationality and metric identifications. The [row calculation](2026-11-23-good-type-spectral-denominator.md) supplies the occupied Green labels. The [raw image calculation](2026-11-23-raw-theta-integral-image.md) identifies their preimages before the good cut. Here the integral witness belongs to the release's prescribed **larger** level.

## The same marking inside the fixed level

Write $J$ for the definite-unitary level with the top rank-$r$ block marked, and $Q$ for the compact parahoric which allows that block to move. Here $Q$ denotes a group. The quotient $Q/J$ is the finite type group $G$. Its action on rational class forms is a right translation times the prescribed level character. The lattice of class forms tests each value in a coefficient lattice $\nabla_c$. Consequently the characteristic function of any collection of classes acts integrally on the full lattice. ## A witness after removing the theta kernel

Fix $s_0=\lfloor f/4\rfloor$ qualifying places over each of $g_0,g_1$, as in the row calculation, and put $h=2s_0$. At each place its mixed $p$-Sylow slice has between one and $p-1$ forbidden shifts. Some orbit of its subgroup of order $p$ meets this set. That orbit also has a good point. Walking cyclically around it gives an adjacent good point followed by a bad one. Choose these good starting points independently, keeping every other place good. The resulting joint label on $W$ is occupied because the necessary determinant twists exist on $W$. Write $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_h$ for the order-$p$ steps into the respective forbidden points. Applying any nonempty subset of these shifts makes the joint label bad. Let $D$ be the larger Whittaker lattice, $S$ the retained subspace after removing the theta kernel and $P_S$ its orthogonal projection. The source identifies $S$ with `Idx.Z0` and $P_S D$ with the projected `Tl` lattice in its retained coordinates. The rational raw theta map intertwines finite level types and reaches the full row type. On a good label its projected value is the raw value. The kernel is stable under the central type idempotents, which are self-adjoint for the class pairing. Thus the retained subspace on the starting label maps isomorphically onto that nonzero row label. Subtracting the orthogonal kernel part of a preimage in that type gives the inverse. Bad labels are orthogonal to $S$ because $S$ is inside the good idempotent's range. This argument is valid at the base tuple and at each used twist, with the same full $W$ level. Localize at $\lambda\mid p$. Let $L_*$ be the intersection of $D_\lambda$ with this retained joint-label space of dimension $d_*>0$. It is saturated in $D_\lambda$. Write $u=1-\zeta_p$. Each $(1-M_{\alpha_i})/u$ acts integrally, since its classwise values are either zero or geometric sums of roots of unity. For every $v\in L_*$ form the integral input

$$
 x=\prod_{i=1}^h\frac{1-M_{\alpha_i}}{u}\,v .
$$

On expanding the numerator, every term involving at least one shift is in a bad type and is killed by $P_S$. The term with no shifts is $v$. Therefore $P_S x=v/u^h$. In particular the finite module
$P_S D_\lambda/(D_\lambda\cap S)$ contains a copy of $u^{-h}L_*/L_*$. With $e_\lambda=v_\lambda(p)$ and uniformizer valuation one, $v_\lambda(u)=e_\lambda/(p-1)$. The **normalized** length for the actual kernel-complement projection is at least

$$
                 d_*\,\frac{h}{p-1}>0.
$$

The bound is for the total projected lattice and does not identify which individual Hecke localization receives the contribution. The eigencharacters over a splitting field can be used in this argument. Length divided by $e_\lambda$ is invariant under a finite extension of DVRs. ## Why the twist branch actually runs

There is a sign check at the base tuple. The release uses its coefficient hypothesis with $s=1$. It gives $\nabla_c^\#=\Delta_c\subseteq\nabla_c$ for the coefficient pairing. The coefficient lattices are stable under the class stabilizers. This also follows by duality from stability of $\Delta_c$ and invariance of the pairing. In the class pairing the $c$-term has weight $1/|\Gamma_c|$. Test a dual invariant vector against the integral sum $\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma_c}\gamma y$ supported at $c$, for every $y\in\nabla_c$. The order cancels and shows its value at $c$ lies in $\nabla_c^\#$. The level metric introduces another factor $1/|U|$. The same argument with the character-weighted sum over $U$ places the dual of the Whittaker lattice in the full class dual and hence in $D$. These tests work over the global coefficient ring and at conjugate places together. None divides an integral vector by the group order. ## The next inequality sees the sign

For the [raw image](2026-11-23-raw-theta-integral-image.md) $Y$ in the row type lattice's fraction space, denote the row projector by $P$, the ambient lattice by $\Lambda$, and $G_+=\Lambda\cap\operatorname{im}P$. With $i(A,B)$ the signed local determinant index, exact sequences for the restriction of $P$ to $Y$ and to $\Lambda$ give

$$
 i(G_+,PY)=i(\Lambda,Y)-i(\Lambda\cap\ker P,Y\cap\ker P)
           -\operatorname{length}(P\Lambda/G_+).
$$

Choose lattice bases of the two kernels and extend by lifts of bases of the two respective image lattices. The determinant is block triangular, giving the formula with the indicated minus sign. In `LeafTW1` the same reference lattice occurs on both sides of a relative signed comparison of **two projected images**. The $1/p^2$ allowance there is for that relative change. The separate bound for $\eta_{\rm loc}$ at $q,d$ explicitly includes $v_p(|G|)$ for each retained multiplicity. For $h$ selected places each contributes at least one factor $p$ to $|G|$, so this term accommodates $d_*h/(p-1)$.
