# The real class field behind the obstruction

**24 November 2026.** Let $M$ be the real cyclic degree-576 field cut out by $z=\bar\psi\omega^{296}$ for the [live in-window input](2026-11-24-a-class-inside-the-zeta-windows.md). The entire 577-primary class group of $M$ is **$C_{577}$**. Its Hilbert 577-class field $L_y$ has trivial 577-class group. The same conclusions hold after adjoining each finite rational cyclotomic 577-layer. This identifies the actual field cut out by the nonsplit lower class and rules out using a further totally real unramified tower there to account for the mixed obstruction.

## Check all 576 real sectors

Put $p=577$ and $\Delta=\operatorname{Gal}(M/\mathbb Q)=C_{576}$. Every character of $\Delta$ is defined over $\mathbb Z_p$. For $a=1,\ldots,575$ the mirror Kummer character of the coefficient $z^a$ is $\lambda_a=\omega z^{-a}$. It is odd and nontrivial on $p$-inertia because $1-296a$ is never zero modulo 576. Consequently all radicand valuations are zero modulo $p$, even in the relaxed problem. Odd units contribute no power class of this character because $\lambda_a\ne\omega$. Unramified class-field characters in the $z^a$ sector of $M$ give classes in this relaxed group by prime-to-$p$ descent. If $z^a$ is nontrivial on inertia at $p$, they automatically satisfy the strict condition as well.

The sole surviving real character is $a=575$, namely $z^{-1}$. It has residual dimension one. Its class group for the **finite Teichmüller character** can be tested for order $p^2$ by taking $j=296\cdot577$, congruent to 296 modulo 576 and zero modulo 577. Equivalently specialize the [strict tower module](2026-11-24-the-cyclotomic-module-of-the-fixed-class.md) at $T=0$. Thus the full 577-primary group at $M$ has order 577.

## All finite layers and the degree-577 field

For every nontrivial $z^a$ whose relaxed residual group is zero, compact Nakayama on the cyclotomic tower gives zero even with ramification allowed at 577. In particular no unramified class at a finite layer can survive in that sector because restriction is injective there. Indeed the prime-to-577 $\Delta$-character is nontrivial and the cyclotomic quotient is abelian. For the three nonlocal mirror lines the strict tower module is zero instead. Their inertia characters at 577 are nontrivial, so an unramified class at any layer would again have to be strict. The exceptional surviving real line has group $C_{577}$ in each layer by the previous receipt. The $\Delta$-trivial component is the 577-part of the class group of the rational cyclotomic layer. It vanishes because the maximal pro-577 extension of $\mathbb Q$ unramified away from 577 has just one generator by abelian class field theory and Frattini generation, so it is the cyclotomic $\mathbb Z_{577}$-extension. An unramified 577-extension of a rational layer would give an additional pro-577 extension after taking conjugates.

The affine Galois action attached to the original nonzero cocycle is $x\mapsto z(g)^{-1}x+y(g)$ on $\mathbb F_{577}$. Its image is $C_{577}\rtimes C_{576}$. Its splitting field $L_y$ is totally real, since every complex conjugation has $z(c)=1$ and $y(c)=0$. It is unramified cyclic of degree 577 over $M$ and split there at the coefficient prime. A pro-577 group with cyclic abelianization is procyclic. The unramified tower group over $M$ must therefore already equal its abelianization $C_{577}$. The class number of $L_y$ is prime to 577. The same reasoning works over every $M_n=M\mathbb Q_{\ell,n}$ because the base-changed cyclic extension remains nontrivial, and because the full 577-class group has just been counted.

Let $H_y$ fix the origin of the affine line. The resulting real field $E_y$ of degree 577 is a subfield of $L_y$ of index 576. Its 577-class group and those of $E_y\mathbb Q_{\ell,n}$ vanish by extension and norm across prime-to-577 degree. Its discriminant is exactly
$$\operatorname{disc}(E_y)=2^{4023}577^{568}.$$
At 2 its inertia permutation consists of one fixed point and nine regular copies of the order-64 cyclotomic inertia. The conductor exponent is $9\sum_{s=1}^6(s+2)2^{s-1}=4023$. At 577 its tame order-72 inertia fixes one point and has eight other orbits, giving conductor exponent $577-9=568$. These are the only ramified primes. At 577 the local algebra of $E_y$ has one degree-one factor and eight totally ramified degree-72 factors. The local decomposition group gives these same orbits because the residual cocycle is strict there and the residue-field character value lies in the order-72 inertia image.

## What changes on adjoining the imaginary quadratic field

For $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$, the odd characters of $MK/\mathbb Q$ are exactly $\eta z^a$, $0\le a<576$. The relative class number has 577-adic valuation four. The contributing labels $a=276,336,407,447$ have first Bernoulli quotients modulo 577 of $149,392,324,222$. At $r=12407809$ we formed the Jacobi radicand for each. At the independent split prime 19645697 the respective power-residue exponents are $12,234,48,215$. Thus there are four different nonzero divided-divisor classes, one in each sector. All four are of exact order 577 and exhaust the minus 577-primary group. This uses neither a projected circular-unit index nor an assumed numerical class number for the real degree-576 field. In the unramified cyclic extension $L_yK/MK$ with group $P=C_{577}$, the $P$-coinvariants of the upper minus class group equal this four-dimensional lower minus group. To see this, let $G$ be the unramified tower group over $MK$ and $H$ its index-577 subgroup. Every unramified pro-577 extension above the upper field stays in this tower after taking normal closure. Since $G/H$ is cyclic, the closed commutator subgroups $[G,G]$ and $[G,H]$ coincide. Hence the coinvariants of $H^{\rm ab}$ inject into $G^{\rm ab}$ with image the kernel of $G^{\rm ab}\to P$. The latter quotient is even under complex conjugation, proving the assertion on the minus part. Over the affine group algebra the minus class group modulo 577 is consequently generated by classes at those four weights. Generators may be chosen of definite complement weight because 576 is invertible. None of these weights belongs to the range $-2,\ldots,2$ of the [nonsplit odd tangent module](2026-11-24-the-obstruction-at-the-nonsplit-point.md). Thus a map from this minus class group to that module must vanish. This is a statement about its generating characters. The whole minus class group upstairs has not been computed.
