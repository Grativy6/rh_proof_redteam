# When a moving coefficient prime can hide a linked ordinary flag

**24 November 2026.** In the zeta branch take the coefficient prime $\ell$ to be one of the two moving field primes $q,d$. A specific part of the released rank-three argument must rule out two ordinary characters whose ratio is exactly the $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character. At late scales, independent local arithmetic reduces this test to twist $j=1$ and **five or six character/prime pairs in total**, however large the real dyadic field becomes. At the moving prime $d$ the remaining condition is the value of a single unramified character. Local examples agreeing to any prescribed finite precision give opposite answers there. ## Use the flag on the field the proof selects

Let $F_m=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{2^{m+2}})^+$. A host character is $\theta\chi_q^u\chi_d^v$, with $\theta$ a character of $F_m$, and $\chi_r(a)=(a/r)$. Its parity is $(-1)^{u+v}=(-1)^j$. The auxiliary field used in Case I for the trivial outer character is $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$. The size condition $q,d>2t+1$ puts $\ell=q,d$ outside the exceptional set and gives $0<2j<\ell-1$ for $1\le j<t$. The numerical conclusions below concern the release’s Type I ordinary points with this datum and its global strong-irreducibility test. Fix $w\mid\ell$ in $K$, of residue degree $f_\ell=1$ or $2$. This extension is unramified. Let $\epsilon$ denote the $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character and $\Psi=\psi\,\epsilon^j$. In the ordinary integral basis the three characters on inertia are $\Psi,1,\Psi^{-1}$. Their reductions on the **whole** decomposition group over $K_w$ are $\bar\Psi,1,\bar\Psi^{-1}$. Indeed the source’s upper-triangular change of basis is congruent to the identity, and the reduced matrices on $G_K$ are the diagonal matrices of the base representation. The finite factors away from $\ell$ retain their usual unramified Frobenius values. ## Five or six labels on every late input

At $j=1$ the two possible families of labels are $\theta\chi_q$ and $\theta\chi_d$. For a label unramified at $\ell$, a collision requires $\bar\psi(\ell)^{f_\ell}=1$ by reducing the ratio on Frobenius at $w$. All these finite values have two-power order, so the equivalent test is $\psi(\ell)^{f_\ell}=1$ in characteristic zero. Using the rational Frobenius exponent 1 at an inert place would incorrectly eliminate real possibilities.

The host has $q\equiv3\bmod8$, $d\equiv7\bmod16$, and $d\equiv-1\bmod q$. Quadratic reciprocity gives $\chi_d(q)=1$ and $\chi_q(d)=-1$. At all sufficiently late scales the small-prime divisibility condition includes 7, so $d\equiv-1\bmod7$. It follows that $d$ is inert in $K$ since $(-7/d)=(d/7)=-1$. [Prime conditions](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L27-L38) · [Earlier reciprocity calculation](2026-11-24-prime-pair-readouts.md#where-the-forced-small-prime-bias-goes)

| Coefficient prime and splitting in $K$ | Mixed label that could contain a linked pair | Permitted dyadic characters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $\ell=q$, split | $\theta\chi_d$ | Only $\theta=1$ |
| $\ell=q$, inert | $\theta\chi_d$ | $\theta=1$ or the unique even quadratic character of conductor 8 |
| $\ell=d$, necessarily inert | $\theta\chi_q$ | All four even characters of order dividing 4, with conductors $1,8,16,16$ |

Indeed in $(\mathbb Z/2^{m+2})^\times/\{\pm1\}$, generated by 5, the exponent of $q$ is odd since $-q=5\bmod8$. The exponent of $d$ is twice an odd integer since $-d=9\bmod16$. Thus $\theta(q)$ has the full order of $\theta$, and $\theta(d)$ has that order divided by two whenever it exceeds one. This proves the table. The count includes labels for which a collision is possible from the inertia and residual data. Whether any given strict class forces a globally admissible point is a separate question.

## The last local value

At an inert prime the polarization further identifies the two outer characters. Conjugating by a lift of the nontrivial local quadratic automorphism sends the representation on $G_{K_w}$ to its dual up to isomorphism, by the transpose-inverse action in the source. Each graded character is invariant under this conjugation because its finite and cyclotomic parts extend from $\mathbb Q_\ell$ and conjugation acts trivially on the unramified quotient. Matching the three distinct integral inertia weights in the semisimplification gives $\kappa_-=\kappa_+^{-1}$ and $\kappa_0^2=1$. The middle character reduces to 1 and $\ell$ is odd, hence $\kappa_0=1$. This reasoning uses the action on characters and does not require an upper-triangular matrix for an element outside $G_K$. [Polarization](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/UnitaryThreeGroups/UnitaryThreeReflectionRight.lean#L72-L108)

For any surviving inert label, $\psi$ restricts trivially to $G_{K_w}$. The characters are therefore $(\epsilon U,1,(\epsilon U)^{-1})$, with $U$ unramified and congruent to 1. The exact condition is
$$U(\operatorname{Frob}_w)\ne1.$$
At a surviving split label, writing the three unramified factors as $U_+,U_0,U_-$, the corresponding tests are $U_+\ne U_0$ and $U_0\ne U_-$. A different choice of triangular basis cannot remove a cyclotomic ratio from the multiset of composition factors.

For a local control use the diagonal base representation and then replace $\Psi$ in it by $\Psi T_n$, where the unramified $\ell$-adic character of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ has $T_n(\operatorname{Frob}_\ell)=1+\ell^n$. These are continuous characters for every $n\ge1$. They preserve the given inertia, integral reduction, weights and polarization. Both restrictions on the surviving local field are crystalline. The first is linked and the second is unlinked. Over $K_w$ the first differing digit is given by
$$(1+\ell^n)^{f_\ell}=1+f_\ell\ell^n\bmod\ell^{n+1}.$$
The examples agree modulo $\ell^n$ for arbitrarily large $n$. These diagonal representations are reducible. The released implication demands irreducibility on every open subgroup inside the polarization kernel. For finite checks the script uses $(q,d)=(67,619079),(83,209159)$, satisfying the indicated congruences and opposite splitting at $q$. It tests all dyadic labels through $m=12$. These two numerical pairs test local arithmetic only. [Exact global hypothesis](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/LinkedUnramifiedChains/LogarithmicNormBounds/LinkedUnlEOf.lean#L20-L30)

The two controls also agree on an actual local cohomology count through depth $n$. For $O=\mathbb Z_\ell$, put $u=U(\operatorname{Frob}_w)$ and $v_\ell(0)=\infty$. Finite local Tate duality identifies the dual of $H^2(K_w,(O/\ell^k)(\epsilon U))$ with the invariants of $(O/\ell^k)U^{-1}$. They are killed by $u-1$, so the cardinality is exactly $\ell^{\min(k,v_\ell(u-1))}$. At $k=n+1$ the linked and perturbed examples first differ by a factor $\ell$. ## Follow the first downstream distinction

For a linked adjacent pair in an upper-triangular matrix representation, divide the off-diagonal entry by the lower diagonal character. The result is a cocycle $c\in Z^1(K_w,O(1))$. If $u$ is the additive unramified character taking arithmetic Frobenius to 1, the release’s `UnitStep` equation asks for a cochain with coboundary $c\smile u$, up to the coboundary sign convention. Local reciprocity evaluates this cup product on a Kummer class $\alpha$ by its valuation, up to sign. A uniformizer has nonzero pairing. A unit has zero pairing. Potential crystallinity of the adjacent subquotient forces the Kummer valuation to vanish. Crystalline Kummer extensions come from units, and passage to a finite extension multiplies valuation by its ramification index. The same zero then holds integrally because the local invariant group in degree two for $O(1)$ is torsion-free. Thus a linked crystalline extension can be nonsplit and still supplies the local cochain the proof asks for. [Local equation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/LinkedUnramifiedChains/CrystallineCardinalities/UnitStep.lean#L48-L55) · [Local reciprocity and the cup product](https://pagine.dm.unipi.it/tamas/CFT.pdf) · [Crystalline Kummer classes](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/BSDseminar/refs/BKintro.pdf)

There is an independent check once a characteristic-zero point is identified with the Galois representation of a regular algebraic essentially conjugate-self-dual **cuspidal** representation of $\mathrm{GL}_3/K$. A linked point satisfying `LevelAt` must be potentially crystalline, so its Weil–Deligne monodromy is zero. The exact ratio of graded characters gives two singleton Weil–Deligne constituents differing by the norm character or its inverse. Full local-global compatibility at $\ell$ applies in odd rank by Theorem A of Barnet-Lamb, Gee, Geraghty and Taylor. The common normalization twist preserves ratios. Zelevinsky’s criterion then makes this local representation nongeneric, while a cuspidal $\mathrm{GL}_3$ representation has generic local factors. This check requires the cuspidal identification for that same point. [Local-global compatibility, Theorem A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.2242#page=2) · [Zelevinsky, theorem 9.7](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0.pdf) · [Global Whittaker expansion](https://indico.ictp.it/event/a02086/contribution/1/material/0/0.pdf)

The conversion from counts at every depth to characteristic-zero rank can itself be checked. For an augmented noetherian $O$-algebra $(R,\xi)$ over a discrete valuation ring, the module representing derivations fixing the chosen entries is a quotient of $\ker\xi/(\ker\xi)^2$. It is finite over $O$ since $\ker\xi$ is a finitely generated ideal. Write its decomposition as $O^r\oplus\bigoplus_i O/\pi^{a_i}$. For $Q=|O/\pi|$ and ramification index $e_O$ the number of derivations into $O/\ell^k$ is
$$Q^{r e_O k+\sum_i\min(a_i,e_O k)}.$$
A lower bound $Q^{2e_Ok}/C$ for **every** $k$ with one fixed $C$ forces $r\ge2$. A free rank-one module with an additional torsion summand of length $n$ reproduces the free rank-two count through $e_Ok\le n$. The source explicitly assumes a noetherian ring and one lower bound for all depths. This puts the burden on obtaining that whole lower bound for the actual fixed-weight deformation conditions. [Precise rank comparison](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/LinkedUnramifiedChains/CrystallineCardinalities/RankTwo.lean#L177-L190)

There is a distinct test on the upper bound. Take $\Lambda=O[[W]]$, $B=\Lambda[X]/(X(X-W))$, $M=B$, and the identity projector. At $W=X=0$ the fixed-weight derivation $\delta W=0,\ \delta X=1$ respects $X^2=WX$. At a characteristic-zero fiber $W=a\ne0$ there are two distinct eigencharacters of multiplicity one. For a weight-character coordinate centered at a fixed algebraic weight, one can take $a=\zeta_{\ell^k}-1$ and keep the algebraic exponent fixed while changing the finite part. At $W=0$ multiplication by $X$ on the basis $1,X$ is a single nilpotent Jordan block of size two. Its ordinary eigenspace has dimension one, while its generalized eigenspace has dimension two. The release measures the simultaneous **generalized** eigenspace after the selected projector. It requires the arithmetic-point multiplicity to be no greater than that at every eligible good irreducible point of the same weight. For these two control fibers the required multiplicity inequality fails as $2>1$. Counting ordinary eigenvectors would miss the extra tangent. The remaining issue is the producer of that multiplicity inequality on the actual projected Hida module. ## Unresolved

The source also asserts unlinking for a strongly irreducible marked point under its frame hypotheses. Its proof transports a proposed collision to an auxiliary field. From **one** linked adjacent pair and its local unit condition it constructs two independent derivations of the global deformation ring fixing the inertia weights. It invokes an upper bound on their rank at the same point. The two derivations cannot simply be identified with the two outer local characters, which at an inert place have just been shown to be reciprocal. The outstanding test is the global passage from the unit cocycle to two independent derivations, together with the rank bound for that specialization. No counterexample meeting the global strong-irreducibility and frame hypotheses was found here.
