# Three global quadratic readings at the order-577 point

**24 November 2026.** The two normalized ordinary tangents give three global Galois words whose second-order lower entries are
$$167a^2,\qquad 247ab,\qquad 163b^2\pmod{577}.$$
Here $a,b$ are the amplitudes on the two [specified local units](2026-11-24-four-local-units-at-577.md). The words use genuine local inertia and its conjugate at another place. They provide a global check on any proposed second-order computation. The ordinary condition itself measures two different elements of the decomposition group at 577.

## Read the quadratic residue without knowing the global primitive

Use the residual representation $\exp(yL)\operatorname{diag}(z,1,z^{-1})$, $L=E_{21}-E_{32}$, and fix the real affine field and $F=L_yK$ as in the [first-order construction](2026-11-24-the-unramified-character-that-local-data-miss.md). Choose the lift with odd first-order and even second-order logarithmic terms by the duality involution $\Theta$, acting simultaneously with $\delta\mapsto-\delta$. Equivalently lift over a small extension, average the two lifts in its additive torsor, and normalize the frame. The even global correction is unique up to coboundary since the relaxed even $H^1$ vanishes.

Write $B_0,\ldots,B_4$ for the odd polynomial matrix basis and $A_0,A_1,A_2$ for the even one. We use $B_4=E_{13}$, $B_3=-(E_{12}+E_{23})/4$, $A_2=E_{12}-E_{23}$, $A_1=(E_{11}-E_{33})/2$, $A_0=-L/2$. Let $g_1,g_2$ be local reciprocity lifts of $u_1,u_2$ in $G_{F_v}$. Their first-order matrices are $35aB_3$ and $387bB_4$. For any two group elements acting trivially in the residual representation, their commutator modulo $\delta^3$ is the identity plus $\delta^2$ times the bracket of their first-order matrices. Every unknown second-order term in the two elements cancels.

With commutator convention $[s,t]=sts^{-1}t^{-1}$ take
$$W_{ij}=[\gamma,[\gamma,[g_i,\gamma g_j\gamma^{-1}]]].$$
Inside the innermost bracket one of the two polynomials is translated by one. Its $A_2$ coefficients for the pairs $33,34,44$ of bare basis vectors are $3/16,-3/8,-1/2$. ## A smaller field where the two remaining corrections are abelian

Choose the real degree-577 point field $E$ whose origin is fixed at the chosen local place. On $G_E$ the residual action is diagonal. For a tangent $C=aC_U+bC_V$ write its scalar first-order coordinates as $\chi_j$ in degrees $j=0,\ldots,4$. Their characters are $\eta z^{j-2}$, and their local data and four global normalizations were [identified separately](2026-11-24-the-unramified-character-that-local-data-miss.md#four-actual-global-tests). Both $\chi_0$ and $\chi_1$ are locally coboundaries at the degree-one place over 577. Choose their representatives to vanish there. This is a permissible constant first-order change of frame.

Let $J$ be the fixed field of their common zero subgroup in $G_E$. The action on the two affine coordinates has all translations over $F$, since the image on cubics or quartics projects onto both coefficients. Thus $[J:E]=577^2$ for every nonzero direction, and $[J:\mathbb Q]=577^3$. The chosen decomposition group fixes the zero pair. The resulting degree-one completion of $J$ is the original $\mathbb Q_{577}$. The local splitting field $F_v$ used in the unit tests can be adjoined with prime-to-577 degree. In particular both tested local elements remain available by restriction.

On $G_J$, the first-order polynomial is in the span of $B_2,B_3,B_4$. Every bracket in this subspace has zero $A_0$ and $A_1$ coordinates, by the exact bracket table. Since the residual action here preserves degrees, the lower $A_0$ and diagonal $A_1$ coordinates of the second-order logarithm obey ordinary **scalar crossed-homomorphism laws**. Their coefficients are $z^{-1}$ and $1$. After adjoining $F$ these are two abelian characters with allowed ramification only above 577. On the chosen local splitting field the lower character on $u_-$ and the diagonal character on $u_0$ decide ordinarity. Scaling $A_0$ or $A_1$ to the literal lower or top diagonal matrix entry multiplies the reading by a unit.

There is a lower bound on this particular reduction. Over $G_F$, the alternating form given by the $A_0$ part of the bracket pairs $B_0$ with $B_3$ and $B_1$ with $B_2$, with respective nonzero coefficients $-1/8$ and $1/16$. Its rank is four on both the four- and five-dimensional first-order images. A subspace on which it vanishes has codimension at least two. The chosen subgroup attains that bound and makes both tested forms vanish. Thus a restriction which abelianizes these corrections by making their pointwise brackets zero costs at least $577^2$ in the first-order image over $F$.

The field $J$ also distinguishes a mixed direction locally. At a nonzero affine orbit $t$, the two rows of the lower scalar coordinates $\chi_0,\chi_1$ on the two local unit inputs form the matrix

## What still determines the two characters

On $G_F$, write $H$ for the even second-order logarithm. For $s\in G_F$ and $h\in G_{FJ}$, the two lower projected characters of $H$ must satisfy
$$H(shs^{-1})-H(h)=[C(s),C(h)]\quad\text{in the } A_0,A_1\text{ coordinates}.$$
For two lifts $s_0,s_1$ of generators of $\operatorname{Gal}(FJ/F)$, their value on $[s_0,s_1]$ is the projected bracket, and their value on each $s_i^{577}$ is zero. These identities follow by multiplying the three truncated matrix coefficients. They give explicit compatibility requirements on an abelian ray-character computation over $FJ$. The characters must also extend from $G_F$ with the original affine action and local conditions. Choosing arbitrary ray characters on $FJ$ would leave that extension condition untested.

There is a separate global control on any **change** of the diagonal primitive after restriction to $E$. The difference of two primitives with the same cup is an abelian character. If it is unramified away from $p$ and cyclotomic on inertia at each of the nine places, the nine cyclotomic multipliers must agree. ## Two specific comparison fields

There is a number-field version of the zero test. Let $N/F$ denote the above canonical relaxed second-order extension for a fixed nonzero tangent, and put $T=F\mathbb Q_{p,1}$ for the first rational cyclotomic $p$-layer. The abelianization of $\operatorname{Gal}(N/F)$ is exactly the odd space of first-order polynomials. Its entire even central subgroup is made of commutators. The cyclotomic extension $T/F$ is even under complex conjugation, hence disjoint from $N/F$. In particular $NT/N$ has degree $p$.

At the chosen place the restriction of $N/F$ is abelian of exponent $p$ because the two first-order local upper matrices commute. On inertia its even diagonal character is a scalar multiple of the principal cyclotomic character. No other local matrix coordinate can read that trivial tame character and unramified-quadratic sign on units. The lower and upper even characters are distinct on inertia, and both first-order characters have odd sign. Adjoining the cyclotomic $p$-layer to $N$ removes its local ramification precisely when that cyclotomic inertia character already occurs in the local character span for $N/F$. All places above $p$ are conjugate in the global Galois field, and the comparison is already unramified elsewhere. It follows that **$NT/N$ is everywhere unramified exactly when the missing diagonal reading is nonzero**. When it is zero the comparison remains ramified above $p$.

For the lower coordinate take the [explicit relaxed order-$p^2$ lower class at twist 265256](2026-11-24-the-last-577th-power-digit.md). Over $T$ its diagonal coefficient action is trivial modulo $p^2$ and its residual translation is killed. Its remaining translation defines an even cyclic degree-$p$ extension $T_-/T$. This translation is nonzero locally at the coefficient prime by the previously measured 177. More explicitly $u_-$ has norm one to $\mathbb Q_p$ by its nontrivial tame logarithm character, so its local Artin lift fixes the cyclotomic layer. The nonzero lower reading remains there. A local change of the lower residual frame can add a cyclotomic coboundary before restriction to $T$, where that change vanishes.

The abelianization of $\operatorname{Gal}(NT/T)$ is again odd, so $NT_-/NT$ has degree $p$. Over the local cyclotomic layer the lower inertia character of $T_-$ lies in the span of the local characters of $N$ exactly when the lower reading is nonzero. The lower character on inertia before adjoining the cyclotomic layer is $z^{-1}$, distinct from the upper $z$ and from the first-order odd characters. Adding the cyclotomic character and restricting to its kernel preserves this distinction by the nontrivial tame action and the available principal unit $u_-$. Hence **$NT_-/NT$ is everywhere unramified exactly when the missing lower reading is nonzero**. The global conjugates give the same conclusion at all primes above $p$. The two tests specify particular composita, so a large class number on its own would not determine either answer.
