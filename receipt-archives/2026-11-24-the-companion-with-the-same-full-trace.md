Reversing the three basis vectors interchanges the residual diagonals $(z,1,z^{-1})$ and $(z^{-1},1,z)$. Both characters are even, both factor through every sufficiently deep host, and $576$ divides neither $2\cdot296$ nor $2\cdot280$. The strict group for the live twist has already been [computed exactly](2026-11-24-the-577-class-on-paper.md). The companion radicand character is $\bar\psi^{-1}\omega^{281}$. Its upper-half Jacobi divisor multiplier is **428 modulo 577**, a unit. Thus it has no nonzero strict class. In fact it has no relaxed class either, since its nontrivial inertia character at 577 already forces valuation zero modulo 577 at the coefficient prime. ## Check the width locally

At $K_{577}$, an unramified quadratic extension of $\mathbb Q_{577}$, inertia has the same cyclotomic image as over $\mathbb Q_{577}$. In the live three-dimensional flag, the six off-diagonal residual inertia exponents are drawn from $296,280,16,560$ modulo 576. None is 0 or 1. Local Tate duality therefore gives $H^0=H^2=0$ for each root module, and the local Euler characteristic gives $\dim H^1=[K_{577}:\mathbb Q_{577}]=2$. The same calculation holds with the companion ordering. In particular ordinary upper-triangular lifts with fixed lifted diagonal characters have no local second-cohomology obstruction. For a small extension of coefficient rings, the multiplication defect of arbitrary lifted triangular matrices is a two-cocycle in the strictly upper Lie algebra. Its filtration by these root characters has zero $H^2$. A cochain correction gives the lift. This does not require the three weights to fit in an interval of length 576.

In characteristic zero the ordered graded characters are crystalline, with unramified factors and successive cyclotomic exponents $j,0,-j$. For any two graded pieces in the upper-triangular order the gap is at least two. Their positive character ratio has zero $H^0$ and $H^2$. The Bloch–Kato fundamental sequence gives $\dim H^1_f=[K_{577}:\mathbb Q_{577}]$ for this ratio, which equals the local dimension of $H^1$. Inductively the same argument works for an extension of several upper graded pieces by the next lower one. Its Hom representation is crystalline with strictly positive gaps, full de Rham tangent space and no $H^0$ or $H^2$. Hence the ordinary extensions here are crystalline. This discharges the local weight-range objection for these flags. [Bloch–Kato, `3](https://virtualmath1.stanford.edu/~conrad/BSDseminar/refs/BKTamagawa.pdf)

## Why the companion has no irreducible marked point

A lower entry opposite the ordinary order must be locally a coboundary at 577. The [tangent calculation](2026-11-24-three-deformation-directions-at-577.md) gives zero for every one of the companion's lower directions. The diagonal has no global unramified tangent direction either. After normalizing the mark the only possible tangent entries have strictly positive torus weight, in positions $12,23,13$.

A complete local framed ring is generated over its coefficient ring by lifts of a basis of its relative cotangent space. Use upper-entry functions of positive weights for those generators. A lower-entry function has negative torus weight. It has no monomial at all in positive-weight generators, so vanishes identically. Weight projection is valid integrally by averaging over arbitrarily large prime-to-577 groups of roots of unity in unramified coefficient extensions at each finite truncation. The [symmetric mark construction](2026-11-24-why-the-trace-field-must-ramify.md) explains this argument and its passage back to the source's mark. Thus every companion point is triangular on $G_K$, including points over ramified coefficient extensions.

The same conclusion can be checked by lifting the flag successively. Given a global upper flag modulo a uniformizer power, the discrepancy of the next lift is a cocycle in the lower quotient of the residual adjoint. It is unramified away from 577. At 577 the prescribed flag supplies a local correction, because an inertia element with three distinct eigenvalues fixes its ordered lines and the two-step filtration. The lower strict groups are all zero, so the lower discrepancy is a global coboundary and can be removed. Continuing through the powers preserves the flag in the limit.

The familiar ordinary companion-forms theorem of Gee and Geraghty requires an irreducible residual representation with big image. The residual representation here is a sum of three characters. Applying that theorem to transfer an irreducible form across these two orderings would miss its hypothesis. [Gee–Geraghty, Theorem 6.1.2](https://www.ma.imperial.ac.uk/~tsg/Index_files/cfug.pdf)
