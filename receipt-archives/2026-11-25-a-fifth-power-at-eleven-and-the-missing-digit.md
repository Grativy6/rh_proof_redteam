# A fifth power at 11 and the missing digit modulo 25

**25 November 2026.** Christopher Daniel Pang asked what happens to the residue of a reading when a field changes. His shadow-lattice paper supplied that question, and he suggested 2663 as a prime to try. Hearthline derived the ray-class example, the local fifth-power calculation and the obstruction to lifting the same class. The audited release supplies the coefficient characters, its auxiliary field and the precision of its component argument. [Pang’s May paper](../corpus/Shadow_Lattice_and_Dispersal_Lattice.pdf) · [PAL atlas](../corpus/PAL_v2.4-M_Mathematical_Realization_Atlas.docx) · [The original 2663 question](2026-11-24-what-prime-2663-does-in-the-real-dyadic-tower.md)

The two operations here have different arithmetic effects. A degree-five ramified field kills the inertia reading of a class with values modulo 5. An unramified field of degree five then kills its Frobenius reading. For a lift of the same original class to values modulo 25, the first operation leaves a visible multiple of five on inertia. The second operation cannot erase it.

## The character in the release

In the trivial outer-character zeta family take $\ell=5$, the odd inner character $\psi=\chi_{-d}$ of the second moving prime, and twist $j=1$. This occurs at every sufficiently late admissible window. The exceptional primes for this outer character are 3 and 7, and $4\nmid2j$. Put $\eta=\chi_{-7}$ and $\bar\Psi=\psi\omega$, with $\omega$ the residual cyclotomic character. The second of the two coefficient characters is
$$
 \kappa_2=\eta\bar\Psi^{-2}=\chi_{-7}\omega^{-2}=\chi_{-35}.
$$
Both moving primes have disappeared from this coefficient. Its quadratic field is $K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-35})$. The common character field in the [uniform rank calculation](2026-11-25-why-the-auxiliary-prime-loses-both-classes.md) is $\mathbf Q(\mu_5,\sqrt{-7},\sqrt{-d})$ of degree 16, since 5, 7 and $d$ are distinct ramified primes in the indicated factors. In particular $C_0\ge 8\cdot5^{r-1}-1$ when $r=v_5(w-1)$. [Exceptional set](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/StmtFields.lean#L57-L67) · [Case I](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HeightAndVanishing/DirichletPrimeToLPartMulLPowerPart.lean#L204-L210) · [Window primes](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L27-L37) · [Actual coefficient](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/Strict.lean#L81-L95)

For a witness $w$, the two primes of $K$ above $w$ have residue field $\mathbf F_w$. The reduced positive forms of discriminant $-35$ are $(1,1,9)$ and $(3,1,3)$, as follows from $a\le\sqrt{35/3}$ and $|b|\le a\le c$. Thus the class number is 2. The units are $\{\pm1\}$. The ray class sequence modulo both primes now says that the entire minus 5-part is cyclic of order $5^r$. On local units choose the character $\log_g$ at one prime and $-\log_g$ at its conjugate, for a generator $g$ of $\mathbf F_w^\times$, reducing the logs modulo $5^r$. This determines the full 5-primary character. Modulo 5 the corresponding extension of $K$ is cyclic of degree five and Galois over $\mathbf Q$ with conjugation acting by inversion. Its group over $\mathbf Q$ is dihedral of order ten. Its only possible ramification over $K$ is at $w$. At 5 the unique prime of $K$ is fixed by conjugation, so its unramified Frobenius has value zero. These are the local conditions of the long class. ## Two ways to see the residue at 11

The prime 11 meets the split-prime clause. In a late window $11\mid d+1$, so $-d$ is 1 modulo 11. Also $-7$ is 4 modulo 11, and $11\equiv1\bmod5$. The full seed in this family chooses a further modulus divisible by 7, since $\eta$ ramifies there and factors through the roots of unity of that modulus. Thus 11 illustrates the split-prime step. The all-witness theorem and the lifting argument below include primes satisfying the additional seed congruence. [Split-prime and seed conditions](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/FrobeniusSplitPrimeDatum.lean#L65-L70) · [Source of the modulus](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/SeedCl.lean#L59-L66) · [Modulus passed to the frame](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/SeedCl.lean#L98-L101)

Take $\alpha=(3+\sqrt{-35})/2$ of norm 11. At $\mathfrak w=(\alpha)$ the conjugate $\bar\alpha$ reduces to 3. Use $\log_2$ for the unit character there. Global reciprocity on the principal idele $\alpha$ gives the character value on the rational local uniformizer 11. Indeed $\alpha/11=1/\bar\alpha$ at $\mathfrak w$, and $\alpha$ reduces to 3 at the conjugate prime. All other local contributions are zero. With these signs the value is $2\log_2(3)=16\equiv1\bmod5$. The opposite prime gives $-1$. In the degree-five cyclotomic field $C_{11}=\mathbf Q(\mu_{11})^+$ the inertia value dies. A uniformizer upstairs has norm 11, obtained by taking the relative norm of $1-\zeta_{11}$. Local reciprocity therefore leaves a nonzero **unramified** reading upstairs.

There is a check using two integer polynomials. A quintic subfield of the dihedral extension above has defining polynomial $F$, and $P$ defines $C_{11}$.
$$
 F(X)=X^5-2X^4+17X^3-9X^2+26X+23,\qquad
 P(X)=X^5+X^4-4X^3-3X^2+3X+1 .
$$
The exact factorization identifying the dihedral field is in the [calculation file](check_auxiliary_prime_residue.gp). Over a root $a$ of $F$, the remaining four roots form two quadratic pairs with discriminants $-35$ times squares. Its splitting field is $\mathbf Q(a,\sqrt{-35})$ of degree ten. The file supplies an integral lattice of discriminant $5^2 7^2 11^4$. At 5 and 7 an inertia group containing a rotation would act transitively on the five roots, forcing discriminant exponent at least four. Thus the extension over $K$ is unramified there, as well as at all primes away from 11. Tame ramification at 11 and uniqueness of the minus ray quotient identify the extension.

Put $t=a+4$ and $u=b-2$ for a root $b$ of $P$. Both $F(X-4)$ and $P(X+2)$ are Eisenstein at 11. Their constant terms are $-2849$ and $11$. All other nonleading coefficients are divisible by 11. In the residue field of their local compositum,
$$
 \overline{t^5/11}=259\equiv6,\qquad
 \overline{u^5/11}=-1,\qquad
 \overline{(t/u)^5}=5 \pmod {11}.
$$
The only nonzero fifth powers in $\mathbf F_{11}$ are $1$ and $-1$. The field already contains the fifth roots of unity in its residue field, so adjoining a root of $X^5-5$ there gives degree five. Equivalently that root has multiplicative order 25 and 11 has order five modulo 25. Thus after adjoining $C_{11}$ the quintic contributes residue degree five, with no further ramification. Over the first real layer at 5, the residue degree at 11 is itself five, so the relative unramified character becomes zero at this place.

Pang’s prime 2663 independently sees the global class one layer earlier. It splits in $K$ and in $C_{11}$, and $F$ is irreducible modulo 2663. Its Frobenius therefore has order five. Since $v_5(2663^4-1)=1$, the first real layer at 5 also erases its local reading. At 307 the same polynomial is irreducible, both base fields split, and $v_5(307^4-1)=3$. That prime continues to detect the class at layers 1 and 2, and loses it at layer 3. The file checks irreducibility by modular exponentiation. It also certifies that $\mathrm{Cl}(KC_{11})$ is cyclic of order 110. Its nonzero unramified mod-5 character has no unramified lift to $\mathbf Z/25$ on this particular field, since its 5-primary class group has exponent five. ## What fails at the next digit

Fix any sufficiently late host above and its actual seed modulus $Q$. The field $K$ and the conductor of the common character field are fixed. Dirichlet’s theorem gives infinitely many permissible witness primes $w\equiv1$ modulo the least common multiple of $Q$, 25 and that conductor. Discard the finitely many primes dividing the input modulus or ramifying in the already fixed auxiliary fields $H_0,H_b$ (here these letters denote their fixed fields). This satisfies the full witness and seed congruences and gives $r\ge2$. Thus the following obstruction can occur for witnesses allowed by the full construction. [Order and guards on the choices](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/FrameEx.lean#L126-L136) · [Final seed congruence](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/FrobeniusSplitPrimeDatum.lean#L104-L107)

Normalize the mod-5 minus ray character so a tame inertia generator $\tau$ at $w$ has value 1. Its minus ray lift to $\mathbf Z/25$ exists because $r\ge2$. Over the same frame field, the 5-part of the ramification index at $w$ is exactly five. The layer $T_m$ is unramified there and $C_w$ is totally ramified of degree five. With the preceding exclusions the other auxiliary fields are unramified there. In the pro-5 tame inertia quotient, the upstairs subgroup is generated by $\tau^5$. Its residual value is $5=0$ in $\mathbf F_5$. The value of **any** lift of this same downstairs cocycle to $\mathbf Z/25$ with coefficient action trivial on inertia is
$$
 \widetilde y(\tau^5)=5\widetilde y(\tau)=5\bmod25 .
$$
A coboundary is zero on this inertia. Increasing the unramified degree changes the Frobenius measurement and leaves this inertia value in place. The ray lift uses the quadratic lift $\chi_{-35}$ of the coefficient. The local obstruction applies also to any other globally defined lift with the stated inertia action. An upstairs class chosen independently would be a different lifting problem.

For small arithmetic with two digits already present take $w=701$. Here $v_5(700)=2$, the element $(33+7\sqrt{-35})/2$ has norm 701, and $2$ is primitive modulo 701. The same norm calculation gives $2\log_2(33)=304\equiv4\bmod25$. The ray inertia has order 25 before the degree-five ramified extension and order five afterwards. The number 701 illustrates the arithmetic without prescribing a moving host’s seed modulus.

A separate earlier calculation at 577 measures the congruence of a seed modular form twisted by an auxiliary character. It concerns a different object at the auxiliary prime. [Earlier seed calculation](2026-11-24-one-ramified-digit-at-the-auxiliary-prime.md)

The component proof requests exactly the residual classes and constructs a path over a field of characteristic 5. Raising the precision of the same downstairs witness would be an invalid inference. The displayed component step uses the residual result. An obstruction at that stronger precision becomes an objection to the released claim only if a later indispensable step requires that lift.
