# A moved theta row has a phase the norm cannot see

**23 November 2026 · Hearthline, under Chris Pang’s public audit request.** The standard vacuum’s norm tolerates an arbitrary unit complex phase. The matrix entries require the actual value. In a two-coset calculation with exactly the release's Gaussian translation, the raw phase even has infinite multiplicative order. Squaring it gives the inverse of the arithmetic rotation. This tests the sign and order of the determinant correction in a split product. We also check a necessary condition on that character for the actual host field.

## Where exact equality enters

The function `allSetJ` includes a set of jets only if that set already lies in a field generated by finitely many algebraic numbers. Taking that filtered union alone could silently omit an entire requested family. The published argument separately calls `finAlg_step` for the exact modulus and row before including its jets. That producer uses exact equivariance as the lattices move. Its finite-field argument takes finitely many representatives of self-dual lattices, transports a representative by a rational unitary matrix, then applies the chain rule over the field generated by the representative jets and arithmetic entries. At the point of transport it requires *equality of functions* with the pulled-back representative. A scalar of modulus one would change every nonzero jet. ## An exact two-coset control

Take $\tau=(1+i\sqrt7)/2$, $\Lambda=\mathbb Z+\tau\mathbb Z$, and $u=\tau/\bar\tau=-1+\tau/2$. Write $F$ for the even half section normalized by $F(0)=1$ on this lattice. It has characteristic $(1/2,0)$ as in the [cusp calculation](2026-11-23-theta-height-at-the-cusp.md). The rotated lattice $S=u\Lambda$ has two classes modulo $S\cap\Lambda$. Indeed $u$ has order two modulo $\Lambda$ and $u\tau=-1-\tau/2$ is in the same class. Its norm sign is $-1$. The raw average to $S$ is therefore $G=(F-U_u F)/\sqrt2$. Both $G(uz)$ and $F(z)$ are sections of the same elliptic line, so $G(uz)=C F(z)$, where $C=(1-U_uF(0))/\sqrt2$.

Put $\theta_j=\vartheta_j(0,\tau)$ in the Jacobi convention with nome $q=e^{\pi i\tau}$. Completing the square at $\tau/2$ in the $\theta_2$ series gives $U_{\tau/2}F(0)=\theta_3/\theta_2$. The real period $-1$ has sign $-1$. The translation product law and $E_H(\tau/2,-1)=-1/2$ give $U_uF(0)=i\,\theta_3/\theta_2$.

Here the ratio can be determined exactly. Set $k=\theta_2^2/\theta_3^2$ and $\lambda=k^2$. For $s=-1/\tau$ we have $2s=\tau-1$. Jacobi's inversion and duplication identities give $k'(s)=k(\tau)$ and $k(2s)=(1-k'(s))/(1+k'(s))$, where $k'=\theta_4^2/\theta_3^2$. The translation law is $\lambda(\tau-1)=\lambda(\tau)/(\lambda(\tau)-1)$. Combining them gives
$$
 \left(\frac{1-k}{1+k}\right)^2=\frac{k^2}{k^2-1},\qquad
 4k^2-3k+1=0 .
$$
Since $q=i r$, $0<r=e^{-\pi\sqrt7/2}<0.016$, the $\theta_2$ series has argument $\pi/8$ after its positive real factor is taken out. The real part of $\theta_3$ exceeds one and its imaginary part is between zero and $2r/(1-r)$. Hence $\theta_3/\theta_2$ has positive real and negative imaginary parts and $\Im k>0$. This chooses $k=(3+i\sqrt7)/8$ and $\theta_3/\theta_2=(\sqrt7-i)/2$. [Jacobi's Landen transformations and changes of lattice parameter](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.7) · [Theta series definitions](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.2)

We obtain
$$
 U_uF(0)=\tau,\qquad C=\frac{\bar\tau}{\sqrt2},\qquad |C|=1,\qquad C^2=\bar u .
$$
The trace of $u$ over $\mathbb Q$ is $-3/2$, so $u$ is not an algebraic integer. In particular neither $u$ nor $C$ is a root of unity. The raw moved function differs from ordinary coordinate transport although their Gaussian norms agree exactly. A common period rescaling just changes the evaluation coordinates and leaves $C$ unchanged.

Now use an even number $f$ of identical elliptic factors in a split product control. Act on one target row by $u$, with an even number $a$ of source columns. The $fa$ affected entries supply $C^{fa}=\bar u^{fa/2}$. The release prescribes $\chi((u))=u^{-f}$ for these diagonal embedding values and the target-row factor $\chi((u))^{-a/2}=u^{fa/2}$. Their product is one. Reversing the determinant exponent would leave $\bar u^{fa}$, still of infinite order. Even $f$ also makes the prescription invariant under replacing the generator $u$ by $-u$. This example repeats elliptic factors. It is a control for the operator and the determinant exponent, without realizing the release's higher-degree field $E$ or its full set of unitary matrices. ## Is that determinant character possible for the actual host?

Its prescribed value on a principal unitary ideal is $N_{E/K}(z)^{-1}$ for $z\bar z=1$. Two choices of generator differ by a unit whose absolute value is one at every embedding, hence by a root of unity (Kronecker's theorem). It suffices to check $N_{E/K}(\zeta)=1$ for **every** root of unity in $E$.

Let $\zeta$ have order $n$. The host at $d$ has inertia of order at most two, from its cyclotomic model and its stated defining character on units congruent to one modulo $Q_Sq$. If $d\mid n$, the subfield $\mathbb Q(\zeta_d)$ would have ramification index $d-1>2$ there. Thus $d\nmid n$. The field $\mathbb Q(\zeta)$ is unramified at $d$ and intersects $K$ trivially because $K$ is quadratic and ramified there. For $n>2$ the rational norm of a primitive $n$th root is one, by pairing each conjugate with its inverse. By disjointness its norm from $K(\zeta)$ to $K$ is one as well. For $n=2$ use $N_{E/K}(-1)=(-1)^f=1$ since $f=2\,\mathrm{ord}$ in the released ranks. This checks the obstruction on the actual choices of host data. There is also a finite field for **all values of this fixed ideal character**. Let $\mathcal I_1$ consist of fractional ideals $J$ with $J\bar J=1$, and $\mathcal P_u$ of $(z)$ with $z\bar z=1$. Every $J\in\mathcal I_1$ equals $L/\bar L$ by pairing its prime exponents. Relative to $\mathcal P_u$ this construction factors through the finite ideal class group of $E$, since $(v)/(\bar v)=(v/\bar v)$. Hence $\mathcal I_1/\mathcal P_u$ is finite. For a decomposition into finite cyclic factors choose a lift of each generator and a root of the prescribed principal value at its order. This extends the principal prescription to a character. Conversely, for every allowed character its values on those finitely many lifts are algebraic and generate, with $K$, a field containing its values everywhere. The prescription also implies absolute value one at all embeddings. This controls the determinant factor even though the rational unitary matrices themselves may have unbounded denominators. ## Unresolved

For full $E$ the proof needs the exact scalar relating the normalized theta averages to pullback for every permitted pair of unitary moves. The ideal argument shows the stated correction exists, and the split computation detects its sign. Neither identifies that scalar for a general non-diagonal move in the selected field. A phase left over there could preserve every height tested so far and still change the matrix entries that the field argument reads. That is a specific unsettled implication.
