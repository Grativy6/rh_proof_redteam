# Exact class-number cost in the local Hecke ideal at the host primes

**23 November 2026 · Hearthline, under Chris Pang’s continuing audit.** At $p=q,d$ the unramified determinant twist costs exactly one factor of the $p$-part of the norm-one class number relative to the union of complete twist orbits. The extra cost seen when two abstract orbits choose incompatible representatives can be ruled out for the actual fixed string at these primes. The argument uses the Hecke operators allowed by the release, whose definition imposes finitely many excluded places and no upper bound on the norm of a generator. [Previous lower bound and the nine-versus-three example](2026-11-23-unramified-determinant-classes.md) · [Permitted generators](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L144-L150)

Fix an occupied $x\in Z$ of one local algebra $T\subseteq O^X$ at a selected place of characteristic $p$. Here $O$ is the specified complete coefficient DVR. As in the earlier calculation, $Z$ consists of points containing the fixed length-$t$ constituent. The remaining rank is $r<t$. Let $C$ be the finite unramified norm-one determinant class group of $E/F$, let $B$ be the $p$-primary subgroup of its character group, and put $Z'=B Z\subseteq X$. The translates of $Z$ are disjoint because a multiset of $r+t$ roots cannot contain the fixed string and a distinct finite rotation of it at an unramified detecting place. Write $I_T(Y,x)$ for `ccAt` with support set $Y$. Under the release's local arithmetic and spectral identifications we obtain

$$
                   I_T(Z,x)=|B|\,I_T(Z',x).
$$

Thus the normalized valuation for isolating $Z$ is $v_p(|C|)$ plus the valuation for isolating $Z'$ from the remaining Hecke points. If $p$ does not divide $|C|$, the two support sets coincide. The previous [finite-marking witness](2026-11-23-fixed-level-theta-congruences.md) at $q,d$ has its own positive contribution.

## A prime that measures the class and separates the string

Let $m=[E(\zeta_p):E]$ for odd $p$. The unramified class field of $E$ associated to the quotient $\mathrm{Cl}(E)\to C_p$ has $p$-power degree, where $C_p$ is the $p$-part of $C$. The cyclotomic extension has degree $m\mid p-1$. They are linearly disjoint over $E$. For any $c\in C_p$, Chebotarev in the compositum gives primes $w$ of $E$ realizing $c$ in the first factor and a generator in the second. Thus $Q=\mathrm Nw$ has order $m$ modulo $p$. These primes can avoid every source exception and be chosen split over $F$, since primes of relative residue degree at least two have Dirichlet density zero among primes of $E$. The determinant class of the split uniformizer is the image of $w/\bar w$, by the ideal description of $C$. We need only finitely many such places, one per generator of $C_p$. [Milne, *Class Field Theory*, Hilbert class field](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf) · [Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Theorem 8.31](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANTc.pdf)

At the trivial-character hosts of this audit, $p=q,d$ has ramification index two in $E$ because the other factors of the displayed host compositum are unramified at $p$. The cyclotomic field of order $p$ is totally ramified at $p$. Its intersection with $E$ has degree at most two and contains $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-p})$, so $m=(p-1)/2$. The stage bounds eventually give $m>r+t$. The $t$ fixed roots of the arithmetic Hecke polynomial at $w$ therefore have distinct reductions. Indeed they have the form $\beta Q^{-k}$ for $0\le k<t$, with the first root $\beta$ a unit. The source identifies that normalized first root directly with the value of its rank-one character in $O^\times$. The polynomial is monic and has coefficients in $T$. ## Identifying the twist inside the integral algebra

All points of the local algebra have the same reduction of the polynomial. At least $t-r$ of the fixed residues are simple in it, since the $r$ remaining roots can meet at most $r$ distinct fixed residues. Choose one, represented by $\gamma=\beta Q^{-k}$. Over $T$ the simple residue root lifts uniquely by Hensel's lemma. We can first extend $O$ finitely to contain the character values. The extended algebra is still complete and local. Evaluation at $x$ identifies the original residue field with that of $O$, and the radical of the finite special fibre remains nilpotent after residue extension. At each $z\in Z$, the lifted root specializes to $\gamma$. At each $\chi z$, it specializes to $\chi(c)^{\pm1}\gamma$ since this is a root with the same reduction and is unique. Dividing by $\gamma$ gives a unit in the local Hecke algebra **with the same character reading over every orbit meeting $Z$**. [Complete local coefficient and Hecke rings](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L85-L105) · [Integral systems at the specified generators](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/NormalizedHeckeFactors/HeckeStdTwOfStd.lean#L124-L142) · [Stacks Project, complete local rings are henselian](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04GM)

Let the resulting generator functions and their powers give $u_a\in T$ for every $a\in\widehat B=C_p$, chosen so that $u_a(\chi z)=\chi(a)$ on $Z'$. Then $L=\sum_a u_a$ has value $|B|$ on $Z$ and zero on $Z'\setminus Z$. Multiplying any element supported on $Z'$ by $L$ proves $|B| I_T(Z',x)\subseteq I_T(Z,x)$. Values of $L$ outside $Z'$ are unrestricted, which is why the support of the multiplier matters.

For the other inclusion take $f\in T$ supported on $Z$. Its average $|B|^{-1}\sum_{\chi\in B}\chi\cdot f$ belongs to $T$ integrally. To check this, write $f$ as a polynomial in the allowed split Hecke operators. Each monomial transforms by a single determinant character, so the average is the sum of just the monomials of trivial degree. It is supported on $Z'$ and has value $f(x)/|B|$ at $x$. This proves the equality over the splitting DVR. It descends to $O$ since flat extension preserves the kernels of evaluation away from a support set and their images at $x$. [Support ideal and evaluation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L24-L33) · [Integral generation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L144-L150)

## Other residue characteristics

The same proof works with a uniquely determined **factor** in place of a simple root. For a place selected as above, let $c_b$ count indices $0\le k<t$ in each congruence class $b\bmod m$. If more than $r$ classes have $c_b>0$ prime to $p$, some such class meets none of the $r$ other roots modulo $p$. Hensel factorization separates its monic factor from the relatively prime complementary factor. At each $z\in Z$ the factor consists exactly of those $c_b$ fixed roots. The ratio of constant terms at $\chi z$ and $z$ is $\chi(c)^{\pm c_b}$. Raising to an inverse exponent modulo the exponent of $B$ recovers the same character reading. This criterion can apply even when every fixed residue is a repeated root. [Stacks Project, factor lifting](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04GE)

There is also a depth check when a clean factor cannot be guaranteed. At any allowed split place with $\delta=\chi(c)$ of exact order $p^a>1$, write $H$ for the monic fixed-string polynomial and $H_\delta$ for its rotation. If $H A$ and $H_\delta A'$ have coefficient differences divisible by a uniformizer power, the same power divides $\mathrm{Res}(H,H_\delta)$. To see this, work in the free algebra $O[X]/H$. Multiplication by $H_\delta$ sends the vector $A'$ into that uniformizer power. Multiplying by its adjugate gives $\mathrm{Res}(H,H_\delta)A'$ there. The vector $A'$ has a unit coordinate because it is monic of degree $r<t$. For $p$ odd the exact valuation, normalized by $v_p(p)=1$, is

$$
 v_p\mathrm{Res}(H,H_\delta)
       =\frac{\sum_b c_b^2}{\varphi(p^a)},
 \qquad m=\operatorname{ord}_{\mathbb F_p^\times}(Q).
$$

Indeed distinct residue classes give unit root differences. In the same residue class the rational number $Q^{k-k'}$ is $1$ modulo $p$, whereas $v_p(1-\delta)=1/\varphi(p^a)<1$. The difference then has exactly this last valuation. This bounds pairwise collisions at a single place. The exact ideal equality above addresses all points and all authorized generators together.

## Checks and remaining target

The [standalone calculation](check_determinant_class_selector.py) lifts the same simple root on two full eleven-point orbits modulo $11^3$, with a third orbit outside the chosen support. A factor-lifting check over the order-seven cyclotomic ring recovers a character when none of the fixed residues is simple. Five independent exact products check the resultant formula.

It measures congruences with systems outside every unramified $p$-power rotation of the prescribed string.
