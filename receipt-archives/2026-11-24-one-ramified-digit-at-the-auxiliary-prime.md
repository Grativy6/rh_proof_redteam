# One ramified digit at the auxiliary prime

**24 November 2026.** The rank-three seed in the release is built over an auxiliary abelian CM field $L$. Before base change it starts with a rational modular form having an additional character $\theta$ of exact order 577 at a prime $w$. This changes the Eisenstein obstruction. At $w=2363393$ the constant that read **10** at the minimal tame level becomes **$187\pi\bmod\pi^2$**, where $\pi=\zeta_{577}-1$. Its valuation is exactly $1/576$. The seed needs agreement modulo the maximal ideal. We checked how much that weaker congruence buys for the actual live character. ## The change of level

Put $\phi=\eta\psi$ of conductor $D=1792$, $k_0=297$, $\ell=577$. Use a prime $w$ split in both characters and congruent to one modulo 577, away from their conductors. Choose $\theta$ by sending the least primitive root of $\mathbb F_w^\times$ to $\zeta_{577}$. The product $\phi\theta$ is primitive of conductor $Dw$. Modulo $\pi$, $\theta$ is one on the units at $w$ and zero on its nonunits as a Dirichlet character. The Euler factor in the generalized Bernoulli sum gives
$$B_{k_0,\phi\theta}\equiv(1-\phi(w)w^{k_0-1})\,B_{k_0,\phi}=0\pmod\pi.$$
This reduction follows also from the distribution identity for Bernoulli polynomials. All denominators at this reduced weight are 577-adic units. The primitive Eisenstein series $E_{k_0}^{1,\phi\theta}$ therefore has zero constant modulo $\pi$ at every cusp. At a cusp where the lower-left entry is not divisible by its full primitive conductor its constant is zero in characteristic zero, and at the other cusps it is a unit multiple of the displayed constant. Its first positive coefficient is one. [Billerey–Menares, Proposition 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.01173v2#page=7)

The exact character needs care because $577\mid\varphi(Dw)$. Here one can lift the mod-$\pi$ cusp form with this **specific** characteristic-zero character. Keep the fine level and first quotient $X_1(Dw)$ by the 577-Sylow of the diamond group. This action is free, also on the special fiber. In the interior an elliptic automorphism has order prime to 577, and at a cusp the automorphism stabilizers of the generalized elliptic polygon have orders with prime divisors dividing $2Dw$, again prime to 577. The quotient is a finite étale torsor for this constant subgroup. The Hodge bundle descends and an exact character of the subgroup defines an integral line bundle by descent. Its fiber degrees are zero because it is torsion. For weight $k\ge3$ the cusp sheaf on the quotient has zero $H^1$: by Kodaira–Spencer and Serre duality its dual sections would have Hodge weight $2-k$ with a degree-zero twist. This has negative degree. Base change therefore lifts the cuspidal section in that exact character. The remaining prime-to-577 diamonds have the usual integral idempotent. The diamond action on weight $k$ has its required parity. [Katz on tame curves, differentials and expansions](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/padicpropMFMS.pdf)

This gives a residual cuspidal Hecke eigensystem. A maximal ideal of the integral cuspidal Hecke algebra lifts to characteristic zero by torsion-freeness and finiteness. The form is new at the full level by its primitive nebentypus. At 577 its eigenvalue is a unit congruent to one. Its residual semisimplification is $1\oplus\phi\omega^{296}$ by good-prime traces and Chebotarev. Non-dihedrality follows because a dihedral trace vanishes on a quadratic coset whereas the residual character $\phi\omega^{296}$ has order greater than two. The distinct Hodge–Tate weights exclude finite projective image. Thus the rational adjoint stays irreducible over finite extensions. At each tame conductor prime its conductor exponent equals that of its ramified determinant. The conductor sum bounds the determinant term at each ramification group, and the inertia codimension would be strictly larger if there were no fixed line. Thus there is an unramified line and a ramified character, with zero monodromy. Its normalized local parameters are pure. At 577 the ordinary flag and its high positive gap exclude cyclotomic links. Multiplication by powers of the Hasse invariant, followed by the same cuspidal lifting in the exact character, gives the required residual system at every weight $297+576u$. This verifies the rational Eisenstein mechanism on the progression relevant here. The auxiliary prime in a full source frame can have additional congruence requirements. The existence argument applies whenever its stated splitting conditions hold. The small primes below are arithmetic controls for the change of level. ## Compute the first digit it earns

Let $L_w(b)$ be the discrete logarithm to the chosen generator, reduced modulo 577. Write $C_w=-B_{297,\phi\theta}/(2\cdot297)$. The entries are its coefficients in $1,\pi,\pi^2,\pi^3$ modulo $\pi^4$. This quotient has characteristic 577, since $577$ has $\pi$-adic valuation 576.

The last prime also satisfies $w\equiv1\bmod1792\cdot577$. To reproduce the calculation, expand $\theta(b)=(1+\pi)^{L_w(b)}$ in the Bernoulli sum at conductor $Dw$. For $b=1,\ldots,w-1$ the residue lifts are $b+wi$, $0\le i<D$. The coefficient kernel is $P(s)=D^{296}B_{297}(s/D)\bmod577$, since $w\equiv1\bmod577$. Choose $y\equiv b w^{-1}\bmod D$, $y\equiv b\bmod577$. The inner sum is the length-$D$ sum of $\phi(y+i)P(y+i)$ in a periodic table of length $577D$. Weight each such sum by $\binom{L_w(b)}j$ to get coefficient $j$, with the constant factor $-1/(2k_0)$. The Python verifier uses partial sums. An independent C++ calculation visits the original residue pairs. It builds $P$ from $P(s+D)-P(s)=297s^{296}$, since an arbitrary additive constant cancels against the primitive character. The two methods agree at all three C++ control primes. [Six-prime verifier](marked-directions-577/auxiliary_constant.py) · [Direct sum](marked-directions-577/auxiliary_constant.cpp)

At every prime in the table one digit is optimal for matching **all positive Fourier coefficients** by a cusp form at this exact character and weight. If all the positive coefficients agreed beyond valuation $1/576$, subtract the Eisenstein expansion. Over a joint coefficient DVR the first nonzero reduction after dividing by the valuation of its constant would be a modular form with a nonzero constant $q$-expansion. Its 576th power would have the same expansion as a scalar times $H^{297}$. At a simple supersingular zero of $H$ this would force $576\mid297$. More explicitly, in the quotient just beyond the constant's valuation the characteristic is already 577. Vanishing of the entire expansion in each preceding residue layer permits division by the uniformizer, by the expansion principle for the line bundle. The same argument works at $k=297+576u$ using $H^u E_{297}^{1,\phi\theta}$. Positive divisor coefficients are identical at this precision because the coefficient ring has characteristic 577. A cusp section matching modulo $\pi$ exists by the lifting argument above. An individual characteristic-zero **eigenform** obtained by lifting the residual eigencharacter may have further ramification in its coefficients. The source asks only for positive valuation on the residual characteristic-polynomial congruences. ## The disappearing inertia is measurable

At a generator of inertia at $w$, the adjoint eigenvalues are $\xi,1,\xi^{-1}$ with $\xi$ primitive of order 577. The trace difference from three has exact valuation $2/576=1/288$ because $\xi+\xi^{-1}-2=(\xi-1)^2/\xi$. The 288 distinct inverse-pair conjugates satisfy
$$\prod_{s=1}^{288}(2-\xi^s-\xi^{-s})=\Phi_{577}(1)=577.$$
On $G_L$ the auxiliary character disappears, so this particular local inertia reading becomes three. The ramification does **not** disappear from the field generated by all adjoint traces. In fact an absolutely irreducible adjoint which stays irreducible on a finite normal subgroup has the same trace field on both groups. Any local coefficient automorphism fixing the restricted traces gives an isomorphic restriction. By Schur's lemma its extension differs globally by a character $\delta$ trivial on that subgroup. Determinant one gives $\delta^3=1$. Self-duality gives a self-twist by $\delta^2$, and an intertwiner for this self-twist restricts to a scalar. Hence $\delta^2=1$ and $\delta=1$. Thus the restricted trace field still contains $\mathbb Q_{577}(\xi+\xi^{-1})$, a totally ramified extension of degree 288. This proves a field statement. It does not transfer the integral depth of the erased inertia reading to a particular Frobenius trace.

An extension of this very same restricted irreducible adjoint back to $G_K$ also differs from its given extension by a scalar character. A scalar twist cannot remove its non-scalar order-577 inertia at the split prime $w$. The desired marked point over $K$ is unramified there. More generally it cannot even become self-dual on an abelian finite extension if it is strongly irreducible: an isomorphism to its dual on that subgroup would make it essentially self-dual on $G_K$. The determinant and the residual multiset $\{1,z,z^{-1}\}$ of order 576 exclude a nontrivial cubic self-twist as in the [fixed-level exclusion](2026-11-24-the-rank-three-obligation-at-577.md).

The source gives the seed a different job. It produces one point over $L$ and passes that point to an ideal-specialization hypothesis. A later propagation theorem is responsible for reaching the other points. The rational adjoint calculation verifies the initial congruence and rules out descent of the identical seed to the required point. It does not independently establish that propagation. The directions of the two source implications matter here. The point-to-ideal statement takes automorphy as an explicit premise. The seed already has that premise. Applying the point-to-ideal statement to the unknown representation would assume the conclusion under test.
