# Four commuting operators can cost more than their individual ranks predict

**23 November 2026.** Each of four integral operators has a unit minor of rank 31 on the same rank-32 lattice. They commute. Applying each twice, with the option to pay 17 powers of 5 for omitting it, gives a quotient of length **532** after the prescribed cut. Substituting the four individual rank losses into the formula used in the release gives **528**. This refutes that substitution. The release supplies additional information at distinct places. Its concrete count uses a Cartesian product of sets of local basis labels. ## The calculation

Work over $O=\mathbb Z_5$. Write $M=O^{24}\oplus O^8$ and let $J$ on $O^8$ send $e_i$ to $e_{i-1}$ for $i>1$ and $e_1$ to zero. For $u=1,2,3,4$, set $Y_u=I_{24}\oplus uJ$. These are four distinct commuting integral maps. Each maps a rank-31 direct summand isomorphically onto a rank-31 direct summand. The 24 identity directions and the seven nonzero arrows of $J$ all have unit coefficients. In particular a left and right integral change of basis gives a $31$-by-$31$ identity minor.

Use $m=2$ steps, a uniform omission price $S=17$ and the cut $T=8$. This equals the cut $e+\sum_u m(t_u+D+1)$ for $e=t_u=D=0$. Set $V_{\rm ram}=M$. Form the same sum over subsets that defines the input lattice.

$$
 N_0=\sum_{A\subseteq\{1,2,3,4\}}5^{(4-|A|)S}
          \left(\prod_{u\in A}Y_u^2\right)M,\qquad N=N_0\cap5^T M.
$$

On $O^{24}$ all four operators are identities, so the term using all four contributes the entire summand. On $O^8$, $J^2$ is the pair of length-four chains on the odd and even indices. Number the positions of either chain from 1 at its killed end to 4 at the other end. Position $i$ can receive an image from at most $4-i$ selected operators. Conversely, starting $4-i$ positions higher attains that number. Every coefficient along the chain is a unit. The minimum price for position $i$ is therefore exactly $iS$. These generators land in individual coordinate lines, so the eight exponents of $N_0$ are

$$17,\ 17,\ 34,\ 34,\ 51,\ 51,\ 68,\ 68.$$

Intersection with $5^8M$ takes the maximum of each exponent with 8. The quotient length is $24\cdot8+2(17+34+51+68)=532$. Assigning each map its actual one-step rank defect $\kappa_u=1/32$ gives $\Sigma=m\sum_u\kappa_u=1/4$. The putative upper bound $d(T+2S\Sigma)$ is $32(8+2\cdot17/4)=528$. Thus the index exceeds the prediction by a factor of $5^4=625$. [Definitions of the cut and the extra cost](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/IsResidueClassZero.lean#L336-L350) · [Definition of the selected lattice](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/IsResidueClassZero.lean#L453-L472)

The discrepancy can grow without bound even when the fraction $\Sigma$ is arbitrarily small. Pad the identity summand to make the total rank $d\ge 16$, keep the eight-dimensional block, give each place $\kappa_u=1/d$, and take any integers $T\ge0$, $S>2T$. Then $\Sigma=8/d$, the length is $(d-8)T+20S$, and the rank-based upper bound is $dT+16S$. The excess is $4(S-2T)$.

## The example survives positive definite symmetric operators

Even a common positive definite real inner product, self-adjointness and full rational rank at each place cannot rescue the rank-only inference. Here is an explicit construction using the ordinary dot product. Write $q=5^{68}$. The subset with no operators already supplies $qM$, so all the subset images together are determined by the four matrices modulo $q$. An invertible change of basis modulo $q$ preserves the elementary divisors of the quotient.

Let $R$ reverse the eight basis vectors. Then $J^{\mathsf T}R=RJ$. In $\mathbb Z/q$, choose $z$ with $z^2=-1$ starting from $2$ modulo 5. To lift a root modulo $5^a$ one may replace it by $z-(z^2+1)/(2z)$ modulo $5^{\min(2a,68)}$ since $2z$ is a unit. For each pair $i<j=9-i$ put $b_i=e_i+ze_j$, $b_j=(e_i-ze_j)/2$. Both norms are zero modulo $q$, their mutual dot product is one, and different pairs are orthogonal. Their column matrix $P$ consequently satisfies $P^{\mathsf T}P=R$. Thus $PJP^{-1}$ is symmetric modulo $q$.

Lift that residue matrix symmetrically with entries in $[0,q-1]$ and add $8qI$ to obtain $B$. Its diagonal entries strictly exceed the sums of the absolute off-diagonal entries in their rows, so $B$ is positive definite. The four matrices $I_{24}\oplus uB$ are now symmetric, commute, are invertible over $\mathbb Q$, each have rank 31 modulo 5, and give exactly the same quotient length. The [exact checker](check_correlated_places.py) independently lists all 16 subset words and computes the elementary divisors modulo $q$ for both versions.

## Where the release earns the extra information

A local rank deficiency alone would allow all four operators to lose the same directions. The release's `LPprimeAt` asks for vectors in specified source lattices, extra divisibility on competing edges, and an integral matrix identity on the target dual lattice. That identity and the edge conditions give local lists of good and bad generators. At a tuple of places, polynomials in the different places' variables have distinct variable labels. The lattice is spanned by products of one generator from each factor. A product with any good coordinate can be handled at that coordinate. The remaining generators are contained in the image of the Cartesian product of the bad-label sets. An upper bound by the size of that product follows even if two generator products happen to coincide. Identifying lattice tuples under the global group action could still have reduced the ambient rank. The source uses trivial stabilizers to build equivariant functions supported on individual orbits. Evaluating them at representatives gives independent families and bounds the number of representatives times the product of the local dimensions by the ambient lattice rank. When it later restricts to the saturated ramified sublattice, its quotient injects into the ambient quotient. The bound retains the ambient rank. The concrete arithmetic construction identifies that rank with the full level-zero dimension by matching classes and the local basis counts. ## Unresolved

The four matrices give a counterexample under individual rank, small total defect, commutation and positive definiteness. The strict local edge conditions and the distinct-place product structure are the outstanding requirements for an arithmetic counterexample. A place to press further is the construction of the edge-isolating vectors for the actual weights. Their required divisibility must hold on **every competing edge at the same source**.
