# The marked point and the two tangent signs

**24 November 2026.** Chris asked whether the characteristic-zero trace still carries the class supplied by the arithmetic. At $q=2179$ the trace estimate has enough room. In the actual marked coordinates one can be more precise. The [weight-two adjoint](2026-11-24-one-adjoint-at-2179.md) gives a point of the marked deformation problem. Its lower-root tangent line and the line carrying the order-25 Selmer class have opposite signs under complex conjugation. A single matrix entry distinguishes the lines even though both have zero trace.

## Put the adjoint in the prescribed frame

Write $\eta=\chi_{-7}$, $\psi=\chi_{-2179}$, $\Psi=\psi\varepsilon$. The two residual characters of $W_f$ are $1,\eta\Psi$. The local coefficient field is $\mathbb Q_5$. The representation can be realized there, since its character is defined there and complex conjugation has two distinct rational eigenvalues, each of multiplicity one. Equivalently the corresponding simple central algebra of degree two has a nontrivial idempotent, so it is split. Choose a stable lattice and a basis in the two eigenspaces of complex conjugation $c$. The two residual characters take values $+1,-1$ at $c$. A composition line of the residual representation must be one of the two eigenlines. If its extension is nonsplit, one of the two off-diagonal entries lies in $5\mathbb Z_5$ for every group element. Passing to a totally ramified quadratic coefficient extension with uniformizer $\pi$ and scaling one basis vector by $\pi$ in the appropriate direction makes **both** off-diagonal entries divisible by $\pi$. The residue field stays $\mathbb F_5$ and $W(c)=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$.

Use the ordered basis $(E_{21},H,-2E_{12})$ of traceless $2\times2$ matrices with $H=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$. Write $A(g)=\operatorname{Ad}^0(W(g))$, $D_c=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,-1)$ and let $J$ be the anti-diagonal identity matrix of size three. The trace form $\operatorname{tr}(XY)$ in this basis has anti-diagonal $(-2,2,-2)$. Thus $J$ is twice $D_c$ times the inverse Gram matrix and
$$J A(g)^{-t}J=D_c A(g)D_c.$$
With $\delta(g)=(1-\eta(g))/2$ the formula $r(g)=(A(g)D_c^{\delta(g)},\eta(g))$ gives a homomorphism to the release's semidirect product. Indeed its involution acts on every $A(g)$ by that conjugation and fixes $D_c$. We have $r(c)_{\rm mat}=1$, $r|_{G_K}=A|_{G_K}$, and $r(g)_{\rm mat}$ reduces to $\operatorname{diag}(\Psi(g),1,\Psi(g)^{-1})$ on **all** $g$. At $q$ and 7 the prime-to-5 inertia action factors through precisely the permitted quadratic quotient, and it is trivial at the other primes away from 5. This checks the killed set. For any allowed mark $g_1$ put $Z=r(g_1)_{\rm mat}-Jr(g_1)_{\rm mat}^tJ$. This is exactly the matrix in the normalizing condition because $r(c)_{\rm mat}=1$ and $\eta(g_1)=1$. It is skew-adjoint for $J$. Its three residual eigenvalues are $z,0,-z$ for $z=\bar\Psi(g_1)-\bar\Psi(g_1)^{-1}$, a unit. Hensel's lemma separates the three integral eigenlines. Skew-adjointness pairs the opposite lines perfectly. Choose lifts of the three standard vectors in their respective eigenlines. Scale the last one to pair to one with the first. Scale the middle one by the square root of its inverse norm, congruent to one since 2 is a unit. The resulting change of basis is a $J$-isometry congruent to the identity. It diagonalizes $Z$ and preserves $r(c)_{\rm mat}=1$. In particular both specified off-diagonal entries vanish. Because the three residues are distinct the eigenlines have a prescribed order.

## Does it lie on a component through the base point at 5?

The source defines `It` by saturating the ordinary equations by a discriminant, taking a radical, selecting minimal primes through the base lift, and intersecting those primes. An ordinary point by itself only checks the first part. The following integral family puts the actual local point and the base lift in the same prime of that saturation. [Ordinary generators and discriminant](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PadicHodgeTheory/IsInertiaInfiniteCyclotomic.lean#L90-L104) · [Selected components](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PadicHodgeTheory/IsInertiaInfiniteCyclotomic.lean#L110-L135) · [Universal property and box condition](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/UnitaryThreeGroups/GRepresents.lean#L73-L105)

Locally $\psi=1$, and $\eta$ is unramified quadratic at 5. In an ordinary basis, ordered with the stable line second, $W$ is lower triangular with quotient character $\nu$, subcharacter $\eta\varepsilon\nu^{-1}$ and $\nu$ unramified, congruent to one. The change from the chosen global basis can be taken congruent to the identity. In fact the ordinary stable line reduces to the second line, which is singled out by tame inertia. Write $a=\nu(\mathrm{Fr}_5)-1$. Over $R=\mathcal O'[[T]]$ take the unramified character $\nu_T(\mathrm{Fr}_5)=1+T$. The local extension cocycle in the chosen ordinary basis is $\pi b$, where $b$ takes values in $\mathcal O'(\eta\varepsilon\nu^{-2})$. This division is integral because the lattice has split residual representation.

The cocycle $b$ lifts to one with coefficient module $R(\eta\varepsilon\nu_T^{-2})$ specializing at $T=a$. To see surjectivity on classes, local Tate duality gives $H^2(\mathbb Q_5,\mathbb F_5(\bar\eta\omega))=H^0(\mathbb Q_5,\mathbb F_5(\bar\eta))^\vee=0$. Filter finite local coefficient quotients by their maximal ideals. All successive factors have this residual character, so their second cohomology vanishes. Passing to the compact inverse limit is exact here by finiteness of the finite-level cohomology groups. The exact sequence for multiplication by $T-a$ now gives surjectivity on $H^1$. Lifting the single scalar in a coboundary arranges equality of the specialized cocycles.

Over the domain $\mathcal O'[[T,S]]$ form the lower-triangular two-dimensional representation with those diagonal characters and extension cocycle $S b_T$. It recovers the base diagonal at $(0,0)$ and the actual ordinary representation at $(a,\pi)$. Apply the adjoint and the same $D_c^{\delta(g)}$ construction. To recover the final marked basis at the latter point, interpolate the required fixed change of basis $P\equiv I\bmod\pi$ by $I+S(P-I)/\pi$. This is invertible over the power series ring and equals the identity at the origin. Conjugate in the semidirect product. On the subgroup `HL` this is usual matrix conjugation and preserves the following tests.

On inertia in `HL`, the three successive diagonal characters are $\varepsilon,1,\varepsilon^{-1}$ in the required order. Each rightmost shifted factor in the ordered product of three kills its successive quotient. Thus both sets of ordinary equations vanish identically. Let $c_a$ denote the product of the two eigenvalue differences at the chosen infinite-cyclotomic inertia element. Each $c_a$ is nonzero, since cyclotomic has infinite order there. Multiplying the two complementary shifts and taking trace against the source's Frobenius lift gives $c_a d_a$, with $d_a$ the corresponding diagonal Frobenius character. This follows by triangularity, or by the three characteristic-zero spectral projectors. That lift has positive residue degree $k$ and belongs to `HL`. Its diagonal is
$$(d_0,d_1,d_2)=\big(\varepsilon(\mathrm{fr})(1+T)^{-2k},1,\varepsilon(\mathrm{fr})^{-1}(1+T)^{2k}\big).$$
Hence each difference $d_a-\varepsilon(\mathrm{fr})d_{a+1}$ used for $a=0,1$ is nonzero as a power series. The discriminant `del` is nonzero in the domain even though it vanishes at the base point. At the actual ordinary point $v_5(\nu(\mathrm{Fr}_5)-1)=3$. In particular $v_5(\nu^{2k}-1)=3+v_5(k)$ for the indicated positive residue degree, another check that the specializing family leaves the discriminant locus. The definitions of the selected inertia and Frobenius elements require exactly the infinite-order and positive-degree properties just used. [Elements, shifts and discriminant](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PadicHodgeTheory/IsInertiaInfiniteCyclotomic.lean#L72-L104)

By local universality this family gives a homomorphism from the framed universal ring. Its prime kernel contains the saturation and its radical. Choose a minimal prime of that radical inside this kernel. Evaluation at $(0,0)$ is the unique base-point homomorphism, followed by the injective coefficient extension to $\mathcal O'$. This minimal prime is therefore one of `primesAt0`. Its containment in the kernels at both specializations yields the required containment for `It`. All rings in this application are complete local coefficient algebras with the **same** residue field. This is a direct local membership argument for the adjoint at 2179. With the integral global lift, normalization and killed set checked above, its marked characteristic-zero point follows from the defining universal property.

## The sign the scalar trace cannot see

In these coordinates the release puts a Selmer value $u$ in $u(E_{21}-E_{32})$ in the $3\times3$ matrices. For the rational adjoint the differential in the corresponding lower-root position is $\operatorname{ad}(E_{12})$. Apply it to the three displayed basis vectors of $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ to get $H,-2E_{12},0$. Its matrix is **$E_{21}+E_{32}$**. At the base lift conjugation by $c$ acts by $X\mapsto-JX^tJ$. It fixes the difference and negates the sum. Since two is invertible modulo 25, their intersection is zero. On $G_K$ the lines have the same character $\Psi^{-1}$. Over $\mathbb Q$ the second line carries $\eta\Psi^{-1}$. Equivalently the restrictions of the corresponding cohomology classes lie in opposite eigenspaces for $c$ when compared in $H^1(K,A)$. An $\eta$-twist of $W$ cannot change its adjoint or remove the sign. [Source matrix and its action](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/UnitaryThreeGroups/ShaInjGen.lean#L101-L130) · [Injection of actual classes](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/UnitaryThreeGroups/ShaInjGen.lean#L225-L260)

This disproves the proposed identification of the two tangent lines for an actual arithmetic input. The released lower bound retains the off-diagonal entry in an integral cotangent module. The upper bound estimates a length by a sum of trace valuations over distinct points. Those are statements about sizes. Neither asserts that an individual point's derivative must equal the injected cocycle. The sign calculation and the successful trace test consequently coexist.
