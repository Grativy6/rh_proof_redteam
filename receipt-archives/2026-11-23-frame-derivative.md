# A frame derivative crosses the required weight cutoff

**23 November 2026.** An input satisfying the released source conditions has a divided derivative of minimum weight **one** where the proof needs a part of weight **two**. The discrepancy has a finite, sharp price. Before normalizing the input, its digit parts can all be corrected modulo $79^{110600}$. Correction modulo $79^{110601}$ is impossible for one digit part. The release explicitly keeps an error term, and its required precision is covered. The calculation identifies information actually lost on an admitted source. The earlier [projection example](2026-11-23-model-projection.md) had a discrepancy annihilated by every admissible source in that example. ## The hand calculation

Write $[a,b]=a_1b_2-a_2b_1$. A divided derivative of order $M$ extracts the coefficient of $T^M$ after replacing $t_1$ by $t_1+T$. Write $P=p^g$ with $g\ge1$, and split each exponent of $t_i$ into its remainder $\rho_i$ and its quotient, recorded as an exponent of $W_i$. Put $S=z_1t_2^P-z_2t_1^P$. Multiplication by $S$ before splitting is multiplication by $[z,W]$ in each digit part. [Substitution and digit part](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/Lemma5aAt.lean#L221-L245)

In differentiating $S^N$, let $h$ count how many of its $N$ factors change. The terms with $h<N$ retain a factor $S$. In the terms with $h=N$, the coefficient for $v$ derivatives on those factors, apart from a monomial and a sign, is
$$
 A_{N,v}=[T^v]\big((1+T)^P-1\big)^N.
$$
For $M=NP/p$, every $A_{N,v}$ with $v\le M$ is divisible by $p^N$, and $A_{N,M}/p^N\equiv1\bmod p$ for odd $p$. Here is a proof valid at any depth. For $1\le i\le P$, the coefficient $\binom Pi$ has valuation $a=g-v_p(i)$, because $\binom{P-1}{i-1}$ is a unit. Its degree satisfies
$$
 i/P\ \ge\ p^{-a}\ \ge\ 1-(1-1/p)a.
$$
Summing this over the $N$ factors proves the divisibility. At degree $M$ and total valuation $N$, both inequalities must be equalities. Each $a$ is zero or one, and their sum is $N$, so every $i=P/p$. This unique ordered product has unit part one modulo $p$, since $\binom{P}{P/p}/p=\binom{P-1}{P/p-1}\equiv1\bmod p$. Degrees below $M$ cost at least $N+1$.

This also gives a sharp bound for the scalar digit identity. The Newton coefficient of order $h$ of $\beta\mapsto\binom{\rho+P\beta}{M}$ is $[T^M](1+T)^\rho((1+T)^P-1)^h$. Modulo $p^c$, $c\ge1$, only orders $h\le M/P+(1-1/p)(c-1)$ can survive. The prefactor adds nonnegative degree and valuation. With $c=h+1$ and $M=hP/p$ equality is attained for every $0\le\rho<P$ by the same unique product. The $h$-th finite difference at $0,\ldots,h$ detects it, so an expression of lower degree cannot work uniformly on those inputs. The source gives the looser cutoff $M/P+c$. [Released digit identity](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/CovariantDegrees.lean#L244-L250) · [Coefficient used by the release](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/RootWeights/BinomialExpansionCoeffZeroOfDeg.lean#L31-L37) · [Newton expansion and truncation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/RootWeights/BinomialExpansionCoeffZeroOfDeg.lean#L82-L115)

For a small check take $p=P=N=M=5$. Each of the five factors must supply its linear term to produce $T^5$, giving $5^5=3125$. Independently,
$$
 \sum_{k=0}^5(-1)^k\binom5k\binom{5k}5=-3125 .
$$
The larger example below meets the numerical hypotheses of the proof.

## An admitted source with a live cutoff

Take $p=79$, $P=79^3=493039$, $c=2000$, $q=1400$, $N=pq=110600$, and $M=Pq=690254600$. Use rank two, two cyclic segments, vector letters throughout, and constant true pairing weight one. As in the [plane calculation](2026-11-23-plane-determinant.md), work over the unramified quadratic extension of $\mathbb Q_p$ with $w=\sqrt p$ adjoined, with $v_p(p)=1$. The only positive-degree frame label is $t$ at index one in segment zero. All frame powers are $P$.

| Segment | Ordinary letters and degrees | Active true link threshold | Chain threshold |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Zero | $x$ of degree $M$ | $M$ | $0$ |
| One | $z,z'$ of degrees $N+1,1$ | $1$ | $N+1$ |

The true degree of $t$ is $M+PN+1$. Each segment has one chain containing every entry. Give both chains slack $N-1=110599$. The proof's cut on the second chain is $(N+1)-(N-1)=2$. It acts jointly on $z,z'$ and the power copies from segment zero. The hypotheses hold even at the external constants. The larger slack requirement is $2M/P+52c=106800$. With the seven true letter degrees shown above, $e_P<28$, $D_m+D_l<112$ and $\kappa<50$, giving $4(D_m+D_l+3\kappa)<1048<c$. These follow by bounding the logarithms with integer powers of 79, as in the checker. Set
$$
 F=[x,t]^M\,t_2\,S^N\,[z,z'] .
$$
The true link at segment zero has minimum weight at least $M$ by $[x,t]^M$. At segment one it has minimum weight at least one by $[z,z']$. In every digit part the second chain sees $[z,W]^N[z,z']$ and therefore has minimum weight at least $N+1$. All other factors are polynomials in vector coordinates, with nonnegative weights. Thus $F$ satisfies the source conditions. Let $e=v_{\rm BB}(F)$ and normalize by $F_0=w^{-2e}F$. A coefficient of absolute value one has all exponents of each letter in a single coordinate, obtained by taking $x_1^Mt_2^M$, $z_1^Nt_2^{PN}$ and $z_1z'_2$ in the three determinants. Hence $e\le n_0$, where $n_0$ is half the sum of the factorial valuations of the letter degrees. [Multidegrees, pairing and balanced norm](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/ObjPol.lean#L45-L68) · [Minimum coordinate weight](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/ObjPol.lean#L84-L108)

The derivative of order $M$ in $t_1$ is permitted because the ordinary degree in its segment is $M$. For every digit part, retain all terms in which fewer than $N$ of the $S$ factors change. They keep $[z,W][z,z']$, which has weight two. Every remaining coefficient is divisible by $p^N$ before normalization, by the hand calculation with $v\le M$. This explicitly constructs the required decomposition on the whole source for this derivative. ## Why one more power fails

Extract the coefficient of $x_1^M$. The factor $[x,t]^M$ then supplies $t_2^M$, so all $M$ derivatives must fall on $S^N$. Every term lands in the digit class $(0,1)$ at $t$. After removing the displayed $[z,z']$, this part is
$$
 H=\sum_{k=q}^N(-1)^k\binom Nk\binom{Pk}{M}
 z_1^{N-k}z_2^k W_1^{k-q}W_2^{N-k+q}.
$$
Set $z=W=(1,1)$ and $z'=(0,1)$. Then $[z,z']=1$ and $H=(-1)^N A_{N,M}$, by the binomial theorem. Its valuation is exactly $N$.

Every polynomial of minimum chain weight at least two vanishes on this test after we take the same multidegrees, including degree one in $z'$. Indeed a change of basis takes the common vector $z=W$ to the first coordinate vector. Only $z'$ can then supply the second coordinate, at most once. Projection to these multidegrees preserves the weight condition because the group acts linearly within each letter. Coefficient extraction and this evaluation preserve integrality. They prove that no weight-two part agrees modulo $p^{N+1}$ in this digit class. The displayed $[z,z']$ also proves the minimum weight is exactly one. After normalization the best possible coefficient precision is $N-e$. The proof needs $c-n_0$, leaving at least $N-c=108600$ powers of 79 on this test. [The allowed error after normalization](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/CollapseExponent.lean#L123-L130)

## The three copies really occur

Write $L,W,T$ for the low digit, power and extra copies of $t$ in the model. An integral representative is $[x,T]^M L_2[z,W]^N[z,z']$. It has exactly the prescribed true image, attains the maximum extra degree $M$, meets both initial link weights, and obeys the model's low-exponent conditions. Its monomials include the extra exponent $T_1^M$ used above. [Allowed model exponents](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/CollapseExponent.lean#L26-L36) · [True image of each copy](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/Lemma5aAt.lean#L221-L228)

In fact all three copies are required in every nonzero model multidegree satisfying the construction's `Good` condition and the released link bound. Write $s$ for the extra degree. The zero segment link sees only $x,T$ with positive degree, and demands minimum weight at least $M-60c$. Both coordinate directions must meet this minimum. Their total degree $M+s$ therefore forces $M-120c\le s\le M$, where the upper bound comes from `Good`. The remaining frame degree $\ell+P\omega=PN+1+M-s$ has $0\le\ell\le2(P-1)$ from the low digits. Since $1+120c<P$, its residue modulo $P$ is nonzero, so $\ell>0$. Also $\omega\ge N-1>0$. The construction obtains `Good` on its integral witness. The outer assertion states fewer constraints on an arbitrary witness. ## Unresolved

This example proves that treating the derivative as exactly weight preserving would lose information on a permitted source. Its sharp loss fits the error budget actually consumed by the pairing. The global family has additional degree and digit restrictions. A counterexample at the next step would have to show that the scalar pairing of an admitted input and its allowed test loses more precision than the proof charges there.
