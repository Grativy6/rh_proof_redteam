# An unramified determinant class forces a full denominator in every retained direction

**23 November 2026 · Hearthline, under Chris Pang’s continuing audit.** Let $F=E^+$ be the real subfield of the host, $T$ its norm-one torus, and $U_T$ its maximal compact finite-idele subgroup. Put
$C=T(F)\backslash T(\mathbb A_f)/U_T$ and $N=|C|$. In the actual fixed larger level, cutting a retained theta preimage to **one determinant class** and projecting back gives exactly $1/N$ times that preimage. At each prime $p\mid N$ the later local Hecke congruence ideal also costs at least $v_p(N)$ at every point in its distinguished set $Z$, with $v_p(p)=1$. The paper includes the class number of $E$ in the corresponding allowance.

Use the large trivial-character indefinite stages and the level and metric ties in the [fixed-level calculation](2026-11-23-fixed-level-theta-congruences.md). For the spectral argument take as inputs the release's raw range, normal definite Hecke operators, downward eigenform relation, existence of Satake parameters and its unramified theta correspondence. The deductions below test their combined arithmetic consequences. They do not certify those upstream theorems independently.

## What remains after the class-number factor

There is an exact test for the residual ideal. Work over a splitting DVR $O'$ for the character values, using the local group $B$ above. The group acts on the entire occupied point set $X$ in its residual component by the unramified determinant twists. The translates of $Z$ are disjoint. Put $Z'=BZ$. For a function $s:Z\to O'$ and $a\in\widehat B$, define $s_a$ on $X$ by $s_a(\chi z)=a(\chi)s(z)$ on $Z'$ and zero elsewhere. In the extended algebra $T'=O'\otimes_O T$ one has

$$
 \mathrm{ccAt}(T',Z,x)
 = |B|\,\{s(x):s:Z\to O',\ s_a\in T'\ \text{for every }a\in\widehat B\}.
$$

Indeed the algebra is generated by homogeneous Hecke operators under the $B$-action. Every polynomial in these operators can be grouped by its character degree. Thus its Fourier components are integral elements of the same algebra, even at $p$. For a function supported on $Z$, those components are exactly the extensions $s_a$, with $s=f|_Z/|B|$, after choosing the compatible translation sign. Conversely $\sum_a s_a$ is supported on $Z$ and equals $|B|s$ there by character orthogonality. The value sets are ideals since the algebra contains the constants. Finite flat extension preserves the kernel defining the support and the evaluated ideal. This identifies a concrete remaining test on the original generators. [Support and evaluation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L24-L30) · [Homogeneous generators](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L144-L150)

Even a free twist action can leave an extra factor. Let $O=\mathbb Z_3[\zeta_3]$, $u=1-\zeta_3$, $A=\{(a,b)\in O^2:a-b\in u^3O\}$ and $T=A[t]/(t^3-1)$. There are two three-point orbits. ## How large is the factor on the actual hosts?

A fractional ideal arising from a norm-one idele has opposite valuations at conjugate split primes and zero valuations elsewhere. It has the form $I/\bar I$. Sending $I$ to its class in $C$ through this ratio gives a surjection $\mathrm{Cl}(E)\to C$. Its kernel consists of classes with a conjugation-invariant ideal representative. Given $I/\bar I=(a)$ with $a\bar a=1$, write $a=b/\bar b$ and use the invariant ideal $I/(b)$. Thus $N$ divides $h(E)$.

On the trivial-character hosts the extension $E/\mathbb Q$ is abelian of $2$-power degree and contains $K_q=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-q})$ and $K_d=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$. Both quadratic discriminants are negative primes. Their class groups have odd order. Indeed an inverse-fixed ideal class in such an imaginary quadratic field is represented by an invariant ideal because a generator of $I/\bar I$ has positive rational norm one and the preceding Hilbert 90 argument applies. Invariant ideals are generated by rational ideals and the single ramified prime, which is principal. Extending ideals from either field to $E$ is injective on odd class groups, since composing with the ideal norm raises classes to a power of two. Their images are inverted by complex conjugation and hence meet the kernel of $\mathrm{Cl}(E)\to C$ trivially. An automorphism fixing one quadratic field and conjugating the other shows that the two odd images are independent, also in $C$. In particular **$h(K_q)h(K_d)\mid N$**. [The host subgroup, discriminants and window congruences](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L19-L37) · [Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, ideal factorization and class groups](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ant.html)

There is an elementary growing lower bound independent of class-number asymptotics. Put $B_0=Y^{\varepsilon/5}$ for the window parameter in the source. Every prime $\ell\le B_0$ splits in $K_d$, by $d\equiv7\bmod16$ at $2$ and $d\equiv-1\bmod\ell$ at the odd primes. For each such $\ell$ choose neither prime above it, one half, or the other, and multiply the chosen ideals. If two products $I,J$ represent the same class, $I\bar J=(a)$ with norm at most $(\prod_{\ell\le B_0}\ell)^2<d/4$ at large windows. The inequality follows already from $\sum_{\ell\le B_0}\log\ell\le B_0\log B_0$ and the displayed lower bound on $\log d$. A nonrational integer of $K_d$ has norm at least $d/4$, so $a$ must be rational. Equality of the two conjugate exponents at each prime forces the choices to be identical. This exhibits $3^{\pi(B_0)}$ distinct classes. For a small check, at discriminant $-23$ a prime above $2$ has order three. The element $(3+\sqrt{-23})/2$ has norm eight and generates its cube up to conjugation. ## Unresolved

The release explicitly adds $v_p(h(E))$ in its off-type pointwise bound for `vcc` and in its generator-prime bound. Its separate bound for $\eta_{\rm loc}$ includes $v_p(|G|)$ at the host primes.
