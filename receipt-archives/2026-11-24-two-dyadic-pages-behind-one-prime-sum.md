# Two dyadic pages behind one prime sum

**24 November 2026.** Holding the quadratic primes fixed, the [four-character calculation](2026-11-24-taking-rhofall-apart-at-the-four-characters.md) gives exactly the same prime sum at two different depths of the actual cyclotomic tower. Chris’s request to rebuild `rhOfAll` led to two checks. There is a concrete scale at which both depths satisfy the published page inequalities. At a separate, earlier transition, an actual strict Selmer class detects a difference that the prime sum cannot see.

## Same scale, two fields

Let $B=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-q},\sqrt{-d})$ and $E_e=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{2^{e+2}})^+B$ for the trivial-character host. The fixed-unit condition in `IsChiField` is the intersection of the kernels of the two Legendre characters with the condition $a=\pm1\bmod 2^{e+2}$. Its fixed field is exactly $E_e$. The odd quadratic discriminants are coprime, and the only real quadratic subfield of $B$ is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{qd})$. Its odd ramification excludes it from the real cyclotomic $2$-tower. Thus $[E_e:\mathbb Q]=2^{e+2}$ and $[E_{e+1}:E_e]=2$. The quadratic quotient measured in the signed row uses only subfields of $B$. [Host definition and page](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L19-L47) · [The same tower in the earlier class calculation](2026-11-24-a-class-inside-the-zeta-windows.md)

Take $\epsilon=1/55$, use either [certified prime pair](2026-11-24-prime-pair-readouts.md#two-actual-congruence-windows) at $Y=2^{40}$, and put $u=2^{55}$ inside $[Y,Y^2]$. Then
$$u^{2+8\epsilon}=2^{118},\qquad u^{2+8\epsilon}\le 2^e\le 2u^{2+8\epsilon}.$$
Both $e=118$ and $119$ qualify. Their field degrees are $2^{120}$ and $2^{121}$. Even their root discriminants differ. Among the even characters modulo $2^{e+2}$ there are $2^{k-1}$ of exact order $2^k$, with conductor $2^{k+2}$, for each $1\le k\le e$. Their combined conductor exponent is $\sum_{k=1}^e(k+2)2^{k-1}=(e+1)2^e-1$. Each occurs with all four odd-conductor characters $1,\chi_q,\chi_d,\chi_{qd}$. The conductor-discriminant formula gives
$$\operatorname{rd}(E_e)=2^{\,e+1-2^{-e}}\sqrt{qd},\qquad
\frac{\operatorname{rd}(E_{e+1})}{\operatorname{rd}(E_e)}=2^{\,1+2^{-(e+1)}}.$$
The signed row and all three auxiliary quadratic ramps at $u$ are identical in both cases. The two certified pairs and the two choices of page test the stated arithmetic predicates. The full late-window assertion has additional hypotheses. [Conductor-discriminant formula](https://people.reed.edu/~jerry/361/lectures/feqn.pdf) · [Exact powers of two](check_rhofall_reassembly.py)

## An arithmetic distinction behind the same row

For an odd prime $\ell$, put $A=(\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell)(-j)$. Write $S(K,A)$ for the classes in continuous $H^1(K,A)$ whose restrictions to decomposition groups above $\ell$ and to inertia groups away from $\ell$ vanish. For the untwisted module on these CM hosts, this describes the release’s strict group. For the next identity this absolute-Galois description also defines $S$ when the module has dyadic ramification. [Actual coefficient and localization kernel](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean#L48-L78)

Let $\eta$ be the quadratic character of $E_{e+1}/E_e$. Shapiro’s lemma identifies the cohomology upstairs with the cohomology downstairs for $A$ tensored with the two-element permutation module. Because $2$ is invertible on $\ell$-primary groups, the half-sum and half-difference split that module into $A$ and $A\otimes\eta$. Restriction to each decomposition or inertia group splits the two cosets into its orbits, with stabilizers the corresponding local subgroups upstairs. Applying the same cohomology identification orbit by orbit carries the local restriction kernels along. Consequently
$$S(E_{e+1},A)\ \simeq\ S(E_e,A)\ \oplus\ S(E_e,A\otimes\eta).$$
The second term describes the part on which the new quadratic involution acts by $-1$. This derivation uses the unramified condition at dyadic places as stated above, including for the twisted module. The argument applies to odd $\ell$. [Shapiro and restriction in group cohomology](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf)

There is an explicit nonzero second term at $e=5$, $\ell=577$, $j=296$. Our [independent class calculation](2026-11-24-the-577-class-on-paper.md) constructs an order-577 class over $\mathbb Q$ with coefficient $A\otimes\psi^{-1}$, where $\psi$ is even of conductor $256$, and $\psi(5)\equiv510\bmod577$ has order 64. On $E_6$ the character $\psi$ is trivial. Restriction preserves the class because corestriction back multiplies it by $[E_6:\mathbb Q]=256$, an invertible scalar mod 577. Its local conditions persist. On $E_5$ the remaining action of $\psi$ is exactly $\eta$. Indeed $5^{32}=129\bmod256$ fixes the conductor-128 layer, while $510^{32}=-1\bmod577$. A lift of the new involution therefore acts by minus one on the resulting untwisted class upstairs. Every untwisted class restricted from $E_5$ is invariant. The order-577 class supplies at least one additional unit of length whenever the lower length is finite. Complex conjugation is a different involution and acts with the plus sign on this class. [Original class and local conditions](2026-11-24-a-class-inside-the-zeta-windows.md) · [Finite class witness](fixed-character-577/fourier_witness.py) · [Divisor multipliers](fixed-character-577/bounds.py)

For $\ell=577$ there is an exact lower bound on the size of any new part. The cyclic group of $E_{e+1}/B$ has a generator $\tau$ of order $2^{e+1}$. On the minus part, $\tau^{2^e}=-1$. Each successive mod-577 layer is therefore a module for $\mathbb F_{577}[T]/(T^{2^e}+1)$. Every root of this polynomial has exact order $2^{e+1}$, so each irreducible factor has degree $\operatorname{ord}_{2^{e+1}}577$. Since $v_2(577-1)=6$ and squaring adds exactly one to that valuation, the degree for $e\ge5$ is $2^{e-5}$. Any positive finite length of the minus part must be a multiple of that number. At $e=5$ the minimum is 1. At $e=118$ the minimum is $2^{113}$. This gives a test for a proposed new class without presuming that one exists at the later page.

That particular class is already present at pages 118 and 119. It distinguishes 5 from 6. The root discriminant distinguishes 118 from 119. The size of the additional strict group at the later pair is open here. The known lower bounds at two different coefficient primes already [break the pointwise budget](2026-11-24-two-primes-break-one-window-budget.md) at explicit scales.

## Where the release joins the pages

The published `VWinAt` takes a page function $e(u)$ before producing a field-estimate window. Its supplier receives that very function. The consumer chooses one function and obtains $J,X_0$ for it. It picks a `PairGood` pair on the outer interval and applies the field assertion with those primes, obtaining an inner interval $[W,W^2]$. There is a real change of arguments. For $c\ge1$ put $k=c^{1/(2+8\epsilon)}$. The output is evaluated at $x$, with page $e(u)$ at $u=x/k$. [Supplier with the supplied function](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/CMFieldExponentialBounds/AdmissibleOfPageN.lean#L41-L49) · [Pair selection](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/WinPairs.lean#L5-L35) · [Rescaling and page](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/WinPairs.lean#L39-L80)

The fit can be checked directly. Once $W\ge k^3,k^{400},1$, every $x\in[kW,(kW)^{3/2}]$ has $W\le u\le x\le W^2$. The latter bound follows from $(kW)^3\le W^4$. Moreover $x^{2+8\epsilon}=c\,u^{2+8\epsilon}$ and $x^{400}=k^{400}u^{400}\le u^{401}$. The field estimate at $u$ therefore covers the twist cutoff at $x$, and its allowed upper bound increases from $\sqrt{\exp(u^\epsilon)}$ to $\sqrt{\exp(x^\epsilon)}$. One chosen prime pair works at both arguments. This checks the assembly conditional on the input field estimate and the good-pair assertion.

## Unresolved

The backward reconstruction leaves a particular arithmetic question. At odd coefficient primes, how large can $S(E_e,A\otimes\eta)$ be for a page actually used in a late good window, after summing the required twists? The invariant part is already visible one layer down. The signed prime measurement places no independent restriction on the extra part. Neither the explicit early class nor the increase in discriminant exceeds the released late bound.
