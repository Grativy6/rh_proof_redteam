# The model projection does not descend through the power substitution

**23 November 2026.** Two integral model monomials below satisfy the source's `GoodE` conditions and represent exactly the same polynomial in its true variables. The projection used in the proof sends the first to a polynomial with a unit coefficient and the second to zero, even after the diagonal corrections. The operator therefore does **not** define an operator on true polynomials by choice of representative. We checked whether the proof needs that stronger property. On this example every admitted source vector annihilates the discrepancy. ## One positive-degree frame label

Use $p=17$, $P=p^2=289$, $b=p^8$, one segment, and rank two. The three ordinary vector letters $x,z,u$ have degrees $b,b,1$. Of the two frame labels, the one with zero-based index 1 is called $t$ and has true degree $P$. The other has degree zero. Both frame power exponents are two, and all unit weights on the true side are one. Use the same local field as in the [determinant calculation](2026-11-23-plane-determinant.md).

Use one chain set containing every ordinary letter and both required frame entries. Set its threshold to $b$, its slack to $b-1$, the active link threshold to $b$, and $c=2000$. In the model the chain sees the ordinary letters and the power copies $W$ of the frame letters. The low digit copy $L$ and extra copy $T$ lie outside the chain. Under the substitution,
$$
 L_i\mapsto t_i,\qquad W_i\mapsto t_i^{P},\qquad T_i\mapsto t_i .
$$
The indexed model constructor really permits $T$ at this frame label. The bridge carries over the threshold and slack, so the actual projection cut is $\lceil b-(b-1)\rceil=1$. [The different copies](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/Lemma5aAt.lean#L221-L228) · [Membership in the chain and link](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/Lemma5aAt.lean#L246-L257) · [Bridge to the projected model](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/BrData.lean#L104-L125) · [Actual cut and projector](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/SubstitutedExponentApplyBlock.lean#L194-L215)

Here $e_P<40$ and $\kappa<100$. Thus $4(D_m+D_l+3\kappa)<1520<c$. The slack exceeds $2(2b+1)/P+26\cdot2c$, as required. The source class contains an explicit vector. Normalize $[x,z]^b u_1 t_1^{P}$ to balanced valuation zero by a power of $\sqrt{17}$, writing $[x,z]=x_1z_2-x_2z_1$. Only its zero digit class survives. Both that digit polynomial and the original true polynomial have minimum weight at least $b$ on the required sets because of $[x,z]^b$. These are full local signed-chain data. Their occurrence in the global family is unverified. ## Same true word, different outputs

Compare
$$
 Z_A=x_1^b z_1^b u_1 W_2,\qquad
 Z_B=x_1^b z_1^b u_1 L_2^{P-1}T_2 .
$$
They both map to $x_1^b z_1^b u_1 t_2^P$. In the second word the low exponent is strictly below $P$, the extra copy has degree one, and the available ordinary degree is $2b+1$. Both words have the required true degrees.

The source constructs, in each model multidegree, the Fock-orthogonal projection onto the subspace with minimum chain weight at least one. In $Z_B$ every tested variable uses coordinate one. On this weight space no nonzero polynomial can have minimum coordinate weight one. For example, specializing the tested rows to multiples of a common vector detects that monomial. The projection kills $Z_B$. The outside variables retain their exponents. [Projection and orthogonality](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/LiftBoundOfHypotheses.lean#L163-L177) · [Projection on the intersection](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/ProjKeepsLetter.lean#L56-L69) · [Multidegree and individual outside exponents](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/SubstitutedExponentApplyBlock.lean#L229-L243)

For $Z_A$, the four tested letters have degrees $d=(b,b,1,1)$ and total $N=2b+2$. Let $v_i$ put one occurrence of the second coordinate in letter $i$, all other occurrences being in coordinate one. In particular $Z_A=v_4$. A combination $\sum c_i v_i$ has minimum weight at least one precisely when $\sum c_i=0$. This is the constant-term test for scaling one coordinate after a generic change of basis. Each $v_i$ takes the same nonzero value on generic proportional rows. The subspace is stable under diagonal changes, so the orthogonal projection can be computed within this weight space. The squared Fock weights of $v_i$ are $\prod_j d_j!/d_i$. The orthogonal complement of the hyperplane is consequently spanned by $\sum_i d_i v_i$, and
$$
 P_{\rm chain}Z_A=v_4-\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^4 d_i v_i .
$$
The coefficient of $v_4$ is $(N-1)/N$, a unit at 17. After substitution its monomial still occurs only once. The other terms have $t_1^P$ in place of $t_2^P$.

The full operator `Ω` applies diagonal multipliers before and after this projection. On these input and output exponents the low digits obey their bounds. Both multipliers approximate units to positive precision, hence are units. They cannot remove the nonzero coefficient of the first word or revive the second. The image of $\Omega(Z_A-Z_B)$ in true variables therefore has a unit coefficient, while the image of $Z_A-Z_B$ itself is zero. The common nonzero power of $p$ used subsequently to clear denominators preserves the distinction. ## Test the discrepancy against every admitted vector

All projected true monomials in the discrepancy have zero low digit at $t$. In that digit class, they have exactly one occurrence of the second coordinate among the tested letters. For the term with $t_2^P$ this is the one power-copy variable $W_2$. For every other term it is a single ordinary second coordinate. Yet membership in `XX` requires minimum chain weight at least $b$ in the **same zero digit part**. Specializing the generic torus to the coordinate torus shows that a monomial with second-coordinate degree one has zero coefficient in any such source. With fixed digits the quotient-exponent map is injective. Consequently every one of these coefficients also vanishes in the true source polynomial.

The true pairing is diagonal in those monomials. The discrepancy is exactly invisible to every admitted source in this family, including the normalized vector above. The release allows the two true outputs to differ and controls their effect on this pairing. It takes a single eligible model witness into the next step.
