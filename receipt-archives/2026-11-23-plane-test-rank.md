# How many independent plane tests does the projection erase?

**23 November 2026.** The [positive pairing sent to zero](2026-11-23-scalar-projection.md) belongs to a large family of integral tests satisfying the same incoming link conditions. In its concrete example, at least **1,884,893,997** independent directions in that family are killed exactly. An explicit family of at least **1,764,168,004** independent directions survives even after reduction to the residue field. At fixed prime $p\ge13$, the fraction of independent directions surviving in the true polynomial space tends to **$4/p^2$** in the local family below. The corresponding slice of admitted source polynomials is just one line. Its detecting test survives. ## Which space is counted?

Use the same two-segment setup and write $[a,b]=a_1b_2-a_2b_1$. Set $P=p$, $M=N$ and assume $N$ is even and a multiple of $p$. Write $s$ for the chain slack, $h=N+1-s$ for the active projection cutoff, and put $b=12c$. For the rank bounds suppose $0\le s-b\le s+b<N$. Over the local fraction field consider the test space
$$
 \mathcal T=\big\{[x,T]^M L_2 [z,z']\,U(z,W):
 U\text{ has degree }N\text{ in each of }z,W\big\}.
$$
It has dimension $(N+1)^2$. The integral tests in it meet the degree, low-digit and incoming link requirements even at the exact link thresholds. The scalar test erased in the previous calculation uses $U=(z_1+z_2)^N(W_1+W_2)^N$. Its companion uses $U=[z,W]^N$. [Substitution and links](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/Lemma5aAt.lean#L221-L257) · [Allowed model exponents](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/CollapseExponent.lean#L26-L36)

For $0\le j\le N$, put $D=N-j$. A subspace of dimension $2D+1$ is spanned by
$$
 [z,W]^j u(z)^D u(W)^D,\qquad u(a)=u_1a_1+u_2a_2 .
$$
To count it, expand in $u_1,u_2$. Before the determinant factor, the coefficients of these $2D+1$ powers restrict at $z=W$ to distinct nonzero monomials times $\binom{2D}{k}$. Over the characteristic-zero field, taking all $u$ spans the same space as taking these coefficients. These subspaces for different $j$ form a direct sum. In any relation divide by the smallest occurring power of $[z,W]$ and set $z=W$ to isolate that term. Their dimensions add to $(N+1)^2$. The span for $j\ge j_0$ is precisely the multiples of $[z,W]^{j_0}$ in this bidegree, by inclusion and dimension.

## A rank bound that survives the true substitution

Write $\Omega=D_-\Pi D_+$ as in the preceding calculation. Both diagonal corrections lower the chain weight by at most $b$. Every generator of the $j$-th subspace, with the common factor from $\mathcal T$ included, pairs to zero against every polynomial of minimum chain weight at least $j+2$. To see this, use rational directions $u$ and take an orthogonal change of coordinates up to a scalar sending $u$ to the first coordinate. Exactly $j+1$ second coordinates can then occur, one per determinant. Rational directions already span. [Cost and range of the projection](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/CollapseExponent.lean#L103-L121) · [Fock orthogonality](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/LiftBoundOfHypotheses.lean#L163-L179)

If $j+1<h-b$, the argument applies to $D_+H$ for every $H$ in the range of $\Pi$. Hence $\Omega$ kills this entire subspace. Only $j\ge N-s-b$ can contribute to rational rank. In the other direction, for $j\ge N-s+b$ the input has weight at least $h+b$. Then $\Pi D_+$ acts as $D_+$ on it. On these test monomials $D_-D_+$ is diagonal and congruent to the identity modulo $p^c$. The product multiplies every allowed coordinate by a unit. [Inverse-unit congruences](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/CollapseExponent.lean#L103-L109) · [Actual composite](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/OmegaValue.lean#L108-L119)

Summing the dimensions and applying the true substitution $\phi$ gives
$$
 (s-b+1)^2\ \le\ \operatorname{rank}(\phi\Omega|_{\mathcal T})\
 \le\ (s+b+1)^2.
$$
The lower bound also counts integral tests with independent true reductions. Choose as their $U$ the product $[z,W]^{N-s+b}$ times each monomial of residual bidegree $(s-b,s-b)$. Modulo the uniformizer, $\phi$ sends those residual monomials to distinct monomials by $W_i\mapsto t_i^p$. Their common true polynomial factor is nonzero in the residue polynomial ring. Multiplication by it preserves independence. Since $\Omega$ agrees with the identity modulo $p^c$ on these inputs, their true reductions survive together. This comparison is before the common scalar used later for denominator clearing. [Later scalar clearing](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/MonomialOrderSegments/TestBoundOfRhoAndNormSets.lean#L97-L115)

At $p=79$, $N=79000$, $s=54001$ and $c=1000$, the total rank is $6,241,158,001$. The two bounds for the surviving rank are $42,002^2$ and $66,002^2$. The full subspace generated by shared directions $u$ alone has dimension $158001$ and is killed.

Saturating that last subspace cannot recover a shallower pairing on the explicit source. In the expansion in $u_1,u_2$ only the coefficient of $u_1^Nu_2^N$ pairs with that source. The other coefficients have different joint coordinate weights. Equivalently, after contracting the two common determinants as in the [scalar calculation](2026-11-23-scalar-projection.md#the-values-before-projection), every matched term contributes $(u_1u_2)^N$. The central coefficient is primitive because its terms with all $z$ coordinates first and all $W$ coordinates second, or the reverse, have coefficient one. The common polynomial factor stays primitive by reduction. Supports of different joint weights are disjoint, so making an integral linear combination inside their rational span leaves its central coefficient integral. Every pairing on this saturated subspace still has valuation at least $81024$.

## Limit and downstream count

Fix any prime $p\ge13$, let $N=M=2p^m$, $m\ge1$, and take $c=1000(m+1)$, $s=\lceil 2(N+2)/p\rceil+52c$. All source size hypotheses hold. In the notation of the [scalar calculation](2026-11-23-scalar-projection.md), $e_P<4(m+3)$, $D_m+D_l<16(m+3)$ and $\kappa<6m+30$, so $4(D_m+D_l+3\kappa)<c$. The choice of $s$ supplies the required slack. For sufficiently large $m$ both rank bounds apply. Now $s/N\to2/p$ and $b/N\to0$, giving the stated fraction $4/p^2$. At $p=79$ the limiting proportion killed exceeds $99.93\%$. This is a fixed-rank, local asymptotic. [Size and weight hypotheses](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/Lemma5aAt.lean#L259-L294)

For the corresponding slice of true sources $F_U=[x,t]^M t_2[z,z']\,U(z,t^{[p]})$, where $t^{[p]}=(t_1^p,t_2^p)$, the true degrees allow the same $(N+1)^2$ parameters. The source chain condition removes all but one. Take the $x_1^M$ coefficient of its $(0,1)$ digit part. It equals $W_2^{M/p}[z,z']U(z,W)$. Under the generic coordinate test the first factor has minimum weight zero and the second has weight one. Their minimum weights add under multiplication because the leading Laurent coefficients multiply in an integral domain. The cutoff $N+1$ therefore requires minimum weight at least $N$ for $U$. Indeed if $j$ is its largest power of $[z,W]$, the residual factor is nonzero somewhere with $z,W$ collinear. It has minimum weight zero, so $U$ has minimum weight exactly $j$. This forces $U$ to be a scalar multiple of $[z,W]^N$. This line meets every source condition, and its explicit companion $Y$ detects it. The no-carry identity for normalization continues to hold in this family since $M+pN+1$ has base-$p$ digits $1,2,2$ at distinct positions. [Different cuts on the source and test](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/Lemma5aAt.lean#L282-L294)

There is also a numerical reason the local percentage alone cannot contradict the per-edge bound. In that bound the target count on one edge is divided by $p^{n'r(k-r)}$ with $k=m_{\rm amb}+1$, the ambient dimension. Its hypotheses give $n'\ge2$, $r\ge2$ and $k-r\ge2r$, hence $n'r(k-r)\ge16$. The two displayed efficiency factors multiply to at most one at the constants used. The second factor lies in $[0,1]$ by the degree budget, and the first is one minus a nonnegative term. Even a surviving fraction $4/p^2$ exceeds $p^{-16}$. The proof later adds counts across different edges using tests that are divisible by $p^{D+1}$ on the other edges while their selected pairing divides $p^D$. Such divisibility prevents cancellation in a sum containing a unit coefficient on the selected edge. ## Unresolved

The dimension of this incoming **test** space would overstate the number of admitted source directions by a factor of $(N+1)^2$. The global edge estimate asks for detectors for every primitive combination of a separately selected source family and sums the sizes of those families. Its passage from this condition to simultaneous independent tests uses integral Smith factors. A global failure would require a count or valuation on those selected sources. The local fraction here alone supplies neither.
