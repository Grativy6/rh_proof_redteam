## Count the states directly

Let $F$ be a nonarchimedean local field with integer ring $\mathcal O$, uniformizer $\varpi$ and residue size $q=\ell^f$. In rank two take the smooth, unnormalized induction of the trivial upper triangular character. Its vectors are smooth functions on $\mathbf P^1(F)$. In the release's local subspace with trivial unit characters and lower depth $m=b+1\ge1$, the fixed subgroup is $K_0(\varpi^m)$. Indeed upper integral unipotents, diagonal units and lower unipotents with entry in $\varpi^m\mathcal O$ generate this subgroup by triangular elimination. The operator $U$ is the sum of the $q$ right translates by $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\varpi&r\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ for residue representatives $r$. [Group and prefix diagonals](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HeckeAlgebras/TcAnti.lean#L90-L104) · [Coset sum](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HeckeAlgebras/ContinuousTorusCharacter.lean#L116-L124) · [Generalized eigenspace being counted](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/LinkedUnramifiedChains/TeichmullerParts/AbsIrr.lean#L54-L66)

For a primitive bottom row $(c,d)$ label the orbit by $s=\min(v(c),m)$, taking $s=m$ if $c=0$. These are precisely the $m+1$ orbits. If $c$ is a unit, upper translations connect all possible $d/c$. Otherwise $d$ is a unit. Diagonal units connect ratios $c/d$ of the same valuation below $m$, and lower translations by $\varpi^m\mathcal O$ connect the last orbit. Write $f_s$ for a function's values. The translated row is $(\varpi c,rc+d)$. When $s=0$ there is just one $r$ for which both entries become divisible by $\varpi$. After dividing them by $\varpi$, the first entry is a unit. At the other $q-1$ residues the new label is 1. When $s>0$ the second entry stays a unit, giving label $\min(s+1,m)$. Therefore
$$ (Uf)_0=f_0+(q-1)f_1,\qquad
   (Uf)_s=q f_{\min(s+1,m)}\quad(1\le s\le m). $$

**Integral normal form.** On the lattice of integer-valued fixed functions, $U$ is integrally conjugate to
$$ [1]\ \oplus\ qJ_{m-1}\ \oplus\ [q], $$
where $J_d$ is the nilpotent shift with ones on its superdiagonal and the zero-size block is omitted.

**Proof.** Set $\Delta_s=f_s-f_{s+1}$ for $0\le s<m$. Keep $f_m$ and replace $\Delta_0$ by
$$ L(f)=\sum_{s=0}^{m-1}q^s\Delta_s. $$
Both changes of coordinates have determinant $\pm1$. The displayed operator gives $L(Uf)=L(f)$, $(Uf)_m=q f_m$, and the coordinates $\Delta_1,\ldots,\Delta_{m-1}$ shift to $q\Delta_2,\ldots,q\Delta_{m-1},0$. At $m=1$ the difference $\Delta_0$ itself is fixed. The invariant function carried by the first coordinate is the indicator of $s=0$.

There is exactly one ordinary direction at every depth. Modulo $\ell^r$, $r\ge1$, $U^k$ equals the ordinary projector precisely when $k\ge\lceil r/f\rceil$. The final scalar block forces sharpness. On the Steinberg quotient by constants that block drops out, so the precise cutoff there is $\min(m-1,\lceil r/f\rceil)$. The other $m-1$ directions can form an arbitrarily long Jordan chain in characteristic zero, and the operator records exactly how quickly its successive steps vanish at finite precision.

## A genuine repeated-root failure

In an unramified normalized principal series with inducing characters $\chi_1,\chi_2$, write $x=\sqrt q\,\chi_1(\varpi)$ and $y=\sqrt q\,\chi_2(\varpi)$. The same row reduction, with its upper triangular character factor, gives
$$ (Uf)_0=y f_0+\tfrac{q-1}{q}x f_1,\qquad
(Uf)_s=x f_{\min(s+1,m)}\quad(s\ge1). $$
For a row remaining primitive the triangular diagonal valuations are $(1,0)$, contributing $x/q$. Dividing the exceptional row by $\varpi$ gives valuations $(0,1)$ and contributes $y$. At $m=1$ the columns for $(f_0,f_1)$ give $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}y&(1-1/q)x\\0&x\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. The second prefix operator is the scalar $xy/q$ since its diagonal matrix is central.

Taking $x=y=1$ gives an ordinary-looking first eigenvalue on an irreducible smooth principal series (the inducing character ratio is one). Its eigenspace has dimension one and its **generalized** eigenspace has dimension two. Its second prefix has value $1/q$. At weight zero the release requires both normalized values to be $\ell$-adic units. This example fails exactly the second test. In the trivial unnormalized induction above, $x=q$ and $y=1$. Its Steinberg quotient keeps the ordinary value one. ## Why the comparison survives in higher rank

At a place over $\ell$ with ramification index $e$ and residue degree $f$, use $v_\ell(\ell)=1$. Write $h_{\tau,i}$ for the increasing-order algebraic weights (ties allowed) at each of the embeddings inducing this place, with $1\le i\le n$. The source divides the $j$th raw prefix reading $\mu_j$ by
$$ a_1\cdots a_j,\qquad a_i=\prod_{\tau\mid v}\tau(\varpi)^{h_{\tau,i}} $$
after transporting through the fixed coefficient-field isomorphism. For an ordinary packet all $u_j=\mu_j/(a_1\cdots a_j)$ are units. Put $\mu_0=u_0=1$. The roots in the release's normalization are recovered from successive prefixes as $\alpha_i=q^{i-1}\mu_i/\mu_{i-1}$. Thus
$$s_i=v_\ell(\alpha_i)=f(i-1)+e^{-1}\sum_{\tau\mid v}h_{\tau,i}.$$
In particular $s_{i+1}-s_i\ge f>0$. The product in the source ranges over continuous embeddings. Each fixes $\mathbf Q_\ell$ by density of $\mathbf Q$, and the finite separable local field has exactly $ef$ such embeddings. Each sends $\varpi$ to an element of valuation $1/e$. It holds before asking what representation realizes the packet. [Weights](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HeckeAlgebras/Distinct.lean#L58-L66) · [Normalization of the actual operators](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HeckeAlgebras/CoefficientRepTrivialOnUnramified.lean#L216-L225) · [Release's slope calculation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/LinkedUnramifiedChains/TeichmullerParts/SubalgebraIntegrality/NormCardLt.lean#L233-L241)

For any other permutation $\sigma$ of these roots, the excess valuation of the product of the first $n-1$ normalized readings is
$$\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^j(s_{\sigma(i)}-s_i)
=\sum_{\substack{a<b\\\sigma^{-1}(a)>\sigma^{-1}(b)}}(s_b-s_a).$$
Adjacent transpositions prove the identity: sorting a single adjacent inversion removes its gap from both sides. The smallest positive excess is the smallest neighboring gap, attained by interchanging that pair only. Equal products for two unordered subsets, such as $1\cdot q^3=q\cdot q^2$, therefore cannot create a second ordinary ordering.

To compare this calculation with **generalized** eigenspaces, set $N_0=U_n(\mathcal O)$ for the upper unipotents, $t_j=\operatorname{diag}(\varpi I_j,I_{n-j})$, and $z=t_1\cdots t_{n-1}$. On $N_0$-invariants the coset sums multiply for these contracting diagonals. In the Jacquet quotient by all $nv-v$, $n\in U_n(F)$, $U_{t_j}$ acts by $q^{j(n-j)}$ times $t_j$ before Jacquet normalization. There are exactly $j(n-j)$ free residue entries in its rectangular upper-right block.

The map into this quotient is injective on a joint generalized space with all $\mu_j\ne0$. If $v$ vanishes in the quotient, write it as a finite sum of differences $n_i w_i-w_i$. Some $z^{-k}N_0 z^k$ contains all the $n_i$, so averaging over this compact group kills $v$. For $N_0$-fixed $v$ this average, translated by $z^k$ and multiplied by the index, is $U_z^k v$. Each $U_{t_j}$ is injective on its nonzero generalized primary space, and the operators commute there. This forces $v=0$. The finite average is valid for smooth vectors. [Actual injectivity step](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/ExistsEig.lean#L218-L263)

For an irreducible smooth representation with a nonzero Borel Jacquet module, take a character quotient of that finite-length torus module. Frobenius reciprocity places the representation in a principal series. Exactness of Jacquet restriction and the Bernstein–Zelevinsky geometric lemma bound each primary multiplicity by the number of permutations giving that torus character. The filtration has one one-dimensional graded piece for each permutation. Its normalized prefix values are $q^{j(n-j)/2}\prod_{i\le j}\chi_{\sigma(i)}(\varpi)$. Multiplication by $q^{i-1}$ in the successive ratios gives $\alpha_i=q^{(n-1)/2}\chi_{\sigma(i)}(\varpi)$. Distinct recovered roots leave at most one permutation. A joint generalized space consequently has dimension at most one, even if the induction is reducible. In the released count, a separate nonzero global summand supplies the lower bound. ## Test the two operators on full flags

For the untwisted rank-three induction, the Iwahori-fixed space has six coordinates. A coordinate records the pivot of the bottom row in a full flag, followed by the additional pivot in its bottom plane and the remaining column. In lexicographic order, the joint diagonal readings $(U_{t_1},U_{t_2})$ are
$$ (1,1),\ (1,q),\ (q,1),\ (q^2,q),\ (q,q^2),\ (q^2,q^2). $$
One can recover them directly by multiplying each permutation matrix on the right by $n(X)t_j$ as $X$ runs over the $q^2$ residue rectangles. Reduce the primitive bottom row and the primitive vector of its bottom two-row minors. The resulting transition matrices are triangular. The open-cell indicator has eigenvalue one for both operators. Its image in the Steinberg quotient is nonzero: the alternating sum on the six cells vanishes on both partial-flag pullbacks and takes value one on that indicator (choose sign by the displayed order). The full induced space and the Steinberg quotient each have exactly one ordinary line. The rank calculation is entirely compatible with nonzero monodromy on Steinberg.

The [direct matrix check](../receipt-archives/check_ordinary_congruence_levels.py) enumerates 3,397 primitive rows at 19 levels in rank two and computes both six-by-six matrices at $q=2,3,5,7,11$. It tests the integral coordinate identity and sharp cutoffs as well as the rank-three quotient. It also compares the two sides of the inversion identity through rank seven. Run `python -B check_ordinary_congruence_levels.py` in the receipt archive.
