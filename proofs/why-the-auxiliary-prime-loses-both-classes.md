# Why the auxiliary prime loses both classes

**25 November 2026.** Christopher Daniel Pang asked where a reading disappears and what the original arithmetic still carries. His May shadow-lattice paper and PAL/APCI supplied that question. Hearthline derived the ray-class bound and the independent construction below. Alpöge and Claude supplied the auxiliary field, its quantifiers and the two cohomology conditions under audit. The arithmetic inputs are classical ray class theory, finite groups and cyclotomic fields. The release chooses a prime $w$ to obtain two global classes. **At the layer it actually builds, both classes restrict to zero on the entire decomposition group at $w$. They remain nonzero globally.** The proof below covers every witness satisfying the seed congruence. The construction of the rank-three component uses global nonvanishing and a condition at places above $\ell$. A local reading at $w$ plays no part in that use. ## How deep must the field be?

Write $k$ for the finite residue field of odd characteristic $\ell$, $\omega$ for the residual cyclotomic character, and $E$ for the common fixed field of the kernels of $\eta,\bar\Psi,\omega$. Its abelian degree $D$ is prime to $\ell$. It contains both $\mathbf Q(\mu_\ell)$ and the imaginary quadratic field cut out by $\eta$. The latter is unramified at $\ell$, so those two fields have trivial intersection. Thus $D\ge 2(\ell-1)$. The witness $w$ splits completely in $E$ and $r=v_\ell(w-1)\ge1$. Let $T_n$ be the degree-$\ell^n$ real subfield of $\mathbf Q(\mu_{\ell^{n+1}})$ and let $C_w$ be the degree-$\ell$ subfield of $\mathbf Q(\mu_w)$. Write $L_n^+$ for the totally real field fixed by the release’s subgroup $U_n$. It contains $C_wT_n$. Put $V_n=E L_n^+$, an abelian extension of $\mathbf Q$. Here the plus field and the character-trivializing field have different jobs. The bound $C_0$ covers the dimension of the unramified additive characters of $G_{V_n}$ **at every $n$**, for fixed $w$. The source then sets $m=3C_0+7$. **Claim.** The actual rank bound satisfies
$$
C_0\ \ge\ \frac D2\,\ell^{r-1}-1 .
$$

**Proof.** Set $B=ET_{r-1}$. Its degree is $D\ell^{r-1}$, since the two degrees are coprime. The prime $w$ splits completely in $B$. Complex conjugation $c$ pairs the primes above $w$, giving $g=D\ell^{r-1}/2$ pairs. In the ray class sequence of $B$ for the product of all those primes, the $\ell$-part of the residue-unit group is $(C_{\ell^r})^{2g}$. Its minus part, where $c$ acts by inversion, is $(C_{\ell^r})^g$. Taking the minus part is exact since 2 is invertible on these groups.

The image of global units removes at most one generator from that minus part. Indeed, for each unit $u$ the unit $u/c(u)$ has absolute value one at every complex embedding. It is a root of unity, as follows by bounding the integer coefficients of the polynomials of all its powers. The roots of unity in $B$ form a cyclic group. Applying the minus projector $(1-c)/2$ to the residue image of the units therefore gives a cyclic group. The minus ray class group contains a finite $\ell$-group of rank at least $g-1$. Containment gives the same rank lower bound for the ambient group by looking at elements killed by $\ell$. Hence there are at least $g-1$ independent minus ray characters $h:G_B\to\mathbf F_\ell$. [Ray class exact sequence](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.785/2016fa/LectureNotes21.pdf)

Restrict them to $G_{V_{r-1}}$. Away from $w$ they are unramified already. At $w$, all residue-characteristic-$w$ wild inertia maps to zero. The maximal exponent-$\ell$ quotient of tame inertia is cyclic of order $\ell$. The totally ramified field $C_w$ removes precisely that quotient. Every restricted character is therefore unramified. Restriction is injective on the entire minus space. For $t\in G_B$ the commutator $[c,t]=ctc^{-1}t^{-1}$ fixes every abelian extension of $\mathbf Q$, and $h([c,t])=-2h(t)$. Any nonzero linear combination survives on one such commutator. This proves the claimed bound at the single layer $r-1$. The same characters survive on every later abelian layer.

As $D\ge2(\ell-1)$, the bound gives $C_0\ge\ell^{r-1}\ge r$. In $T_m$ the residue degree at $w$ is
$$
 f_m(w)=\ell^{\max(0,m+1-r)} .
$$
Indeed $v_\ell(w^{\ell^j}-1)=r+j$ by the binomial theorem. Thus $\ell\mid f_m(w)$ at the selected $m$. Any residual twisted cocycle from the source is an additive homomorphism on $D_w$, since both coefficient characters are trivial there. After restriction to $C_w$ it is unramified, so further unramified base change multiplies its Frobenius value by $f_m(w)$. This kills the last possible local reading. Enlarging to $L_m^+$ preserves zero. The argument uses the uniform bound at the earlier layer before it uses the chosen layer. ## Why both global classes still exist

Here is a separate ray class construction for the same two characters. It lets us check global existence without assuming the released cohomology assertion. Put $\kappa_a=\eta\bar\Psi^{-a}$ for $a=1,2$. Each is odd, and neither is $\omega$. Equality with $\omega$ would force $\eta=1$ on $\ker\omega\cap\ker\bar\Psi$, contradicting the input. Each is nontrivial on the decomposition group at $\ell$, since the input gives an element there with $\eta=1$ and $\bar\Psi^2\ne1$. The characters are distinct for the same reason. [Character hypotheses](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/FrobeniusSplitPrimeDatum.lean#L26-L33) · [The two required cocycles](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/FrameEx.lean#L147-L156)

Set $\Delta=\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbf Q)$. At level zero the residue-unit $\ell$-group for the modulus $w$ consists of a copy of $C_{\ell^r}$ at each place of $E$ above $w$, permuted regularly by $\Delta$. Identify each residue field with $\mathbf F_w$ using the rational integers. To isolate the $\kappa_a$ part, extend scalars to the unramified $\ell$-adic coefficient ring with residue field $k$ and apply the projector $|\Delta|^{-1}\sum_{\delta\in\Delta}\widetilde\kappa_a(\delta)^{-1}\delta$, using the Teichmüller lift. The resulting module is one copy of the coefficient ring modulo $\ell^r$. The global units have zero image in this component. Their minus image comes from roots of unity, and the $\ell$-power roots in $E$ are exactly $\mu_\ell$ since $\ell\nmid D$. These have character $\omega$.

The $\kappa_a$ component of the ray group consequently contains that nonzero module. It has a nonzero quotient modulo $\ell$, giving an equivariant ray character $h_a:G_E\to k$ with $h_a(\delta t\delta^{-1})=\kappa_a(\delta)h_a(t)$. This argument allows a ray character to come from a pre-existing class group. It needs its global value to be nonzero.

For an explicit extension to a cocycle on $G_{\mathbf Q}$, choose representatives $s_\delta$ of $\Delta$. For $u\in G_{\mathbf Q}$, with image $\bar u$ in $\Delta$, put
$$
 y_a(u)=|\Delta|^{-1}\sum_{\delta\in\Delta}\kappa_a(\delta)\,
 h_a(s_\delta^{-1}u\,s_{\bar u^{-1}\delta}) .
$$
Multiplying the terms for $u,v$ and reindexing gives $y_a(uv)=y_a(u)+\kappa_a(u)y_a(v)$. On $G_E$ it equals $h_a$. At $\ell$ the character $h_a$ is unramified. Conjugation within the local decomposition group fixes unramified Frobenius. Since some element has $\kappa_a\ne1$, its value on that Frobenius must be zero. The remaining local cocycle factors through a group of order prime to $\ell$ and is a coboundary by averaging. At other primes away from $w$, $h_a$ is unramified. More explicitly, on inertia elements in $\ker\eta\cap\ker\bar\Psi$ away from $\ell$, $\omega$ is also trivial, so those elements are already in $G_E$. The preceding tame calculation settles $w$.

Complex conjugation multiplies each $h_a$ by $-1$. The commutator calculation again proves nonvanishing on $G_{V_m}$ and hence in cohomology on $G_{L_m^+}$. On $G_{V_m}$ every coboundary for these characters is zero. Even the two restricted homomorphisms are independent over $k$. Conjugate a proposed relation by an element on which $\kappa_1$ and $\kappa_2$ differ and subtract one multiple of the original relation. Each coefficient must vanish. This independently supplies both strict classes with the required local properties. The downstream calculation takes a nonzero class over the residue field and varies it along a polynomial path in the characteristic-$\ell$ fiber. The two classes are used in the component argument at this precision. The missing Frobenius reading at $w$ is real. The commutator proof identifies where the information remains that this step needs. The other immediate rank-three use takes density in the same fiber to rule out a zero-dimensional space of odd generators in a completed presentation. The rank-two use constructs a point of its special fiber.
