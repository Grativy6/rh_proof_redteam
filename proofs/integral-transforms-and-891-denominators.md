Its roots of unity are $\mu_4$, and the product of the two CM embeddings is one on them. The discriminant is even, $d$ is prime and $d\equiv7\bmod16$. ## The missing power at the CM point

Let $\tau=(1+\sqrt{-839})/2$, take a representative idele $y$ that is one above 3, and write $L_y$ in coordinates $a+b\tau$ over $F$. At every place above 3 the coefficient lattice is $O_F^2$. The relative quadratic order $O_F[\tau]$ has index 839 in $O_E$, the conductor is away from 3, and the chosen idele is a unit there. Every integral cusp transformation preserves this local equality.

Let $M_y(z)$ be the normalized holomorphic diagonal series for this lattice, of weight $2k$. At any cusp let $I$ be its ideal of first coordinates with second coordinate zero. Then $NI$ is a rational 3-adic unit. The constant of $M_y$ is $v_{C'}/(3356^{k/2}(NI)^k)$ for a real ideal class $C'$. All its nonconstant coefficients are integral at 3. The Poisson calculation gives them as finite sums of roots of unity times $\pm t^{k-1}/(3356^{k/2}(NI)^k)$ with $t$ a positive integer. It works for nonprincipal $I$ because the constant term is the *partial* ideal zeta and the frequency factor uses an integral ideal norm. [Classwise constant and Fourier terms](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/BlockTheory/DivisorBase/OmegaFactorization.lean#L88-L109) · [All-cusps integrality argument](../receipt-archives/2026-11-23-toric-cm-denominators.md#from-every-cusp-to-the-cm-point)

The form $E_4^{k/2}$ has constant one and nonconstant coefficients divisible by 3. Hence $(3M_y-\lambda_k E_4^{k/2})/3$ has 3-integral coefficients at every cusp. Raise it to the twelfth power and divide by $\Delta^{2k}$. ## What the full character transform sees

Choose representatives in cosets of the local $C_9$ by multiplying fixed idele representatives by conductor units at 37. The other finite weights are common 3-adic units times a character of $C_9$ within each coset. In the sum of the displayed leading terms, the character sum over $C_9$ is zero or nine. Both remove the denominator three, including for the trivial character. The sum of the remaining terms is integral. Thus **all 891 normalized character sums and their twelfth powers are 3-integral**, simultaneously.

At $s=4$ these complex sums are nonzero. Reindexing by integral finite ideles modulo the order units gives the Hecke Euler series in its absolute-convergence region. Global order units are precisely the real units. The ratio $u/\bar u\in\mu_4$ reduces to one at 37 only for the identity. At the conductor a nontrivial residue character kills the deep unit cosets. An unramified character gives the local correcting polynomial $1-(A+B)X+37ABX^2$, where $|A|=|B|=1$ and $X=37^{-4}$. It is nonzero since $2X+37X^2<1$. All the other Euler factors form a nonzero absolutely convergent product.
