## Same-level products and a change of phase

At a fixed modulus the release’s orbit is a union of tuples of residue classes. The same linear transformation selects the tuple of residues. Once they are selected, each coordinate can be any lift of its residue. For a block product the sum over lifts is therefore the product of the corresponding class sums. This explains directly why swapping tied terms within a class cannot affect this calculation. The finite orbit sum still counts each distinct tuple of classes once. [Orbit definition](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/IsUnimodMod.lean#L21-L26) · [Invariant polynomial in the actual class sums](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/ApproxOrbitSumsOfGaloisClassSums.lean#L353-L371)

The geometric phase $U(\kappa)$ is constrained to be a root of unity and to make the corrected targets transform together over the base. At the interface one can multiply every $U(\kappa)$ at one level by a common root of unity in the integral base. It is fixed by the deck transformations, has norm one at every trait, and a product over $j$ factors acquires its $j$th power. The integral orbit polynomial can be scaled by exactly that base unit. All the named same-level conditions survive. For example, with base seventh roots at $p=2$, a primitive $\zeta_7$ satisfies $|\zeta_7-1|_2=1$. Applying this change at just one of two adjacent levels changes the sum of the fine targets in the zero class by the **full norm** of the original coarse target. The torus example has a unit target there. A common torus frame instead gives exact additivity. Thus raw equality across levels requires a phase choice shared between the levels, even at a fixed lattice vector. ## An algebraic-closure limit test

Fix $m_j=7^{j+1}$ for $j\ge1$ and a primitive $m_j$th root of unity $\eta_j$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_2$. In its completion $\mathbb C_2$ put
$$\alpha=1+\sum_{j\ge1}2^{m_j^2}\eta_j.$$
This element is outside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_2$. If it belonged to a finite extension $K/\mathbb Q_2$, subtract the previously recovered digits, divide by $2^{m_j^2}$ and reduce. This reveals the residue of $\eta_j$. Its unique prime-to-2 Teichmüller lift would belong to $K$, so the induction continues. The finite residue field of $K$ cannot contain elements of unbounded order $7^{j+1}$.

On $\mathbb Z$ start with coefficients $2^{v^2}$. Add $\alpha-1$ to the coefficient at 0 and subtract $2^{m_j^2}\eta_j$ at each $v=m_j$. Every individual norm is still exactly $2^{-v^2}$, since each $1-\eta_j$ is a unit. At modulus $m_i$ the added and subtracted contributions for $j\ge i$ cancel in the zero class. The same class sums are obtained by making **only the finitely many changes $j<i$**. Thus every finite level has an algebraic realization in one finite extension of $\mathbb Q_2$, and its targets add correctly to every coarser level. Any common coefficient system satisfying those same bounds must have its coefficient at zero equal to $\alpha$. Indeed all other terms of the zero class have norm at most $2^{-m_i^2}$. This refutes the promotion from these numerical hypotheses alone to one algebraic system at every level. [Integer check of the cancellations](../receipt-archives/check_theta_descent_bounds.py)

There is a specific symmetry this example fails. Over $K_0=\mathbb Q_2(\mu_7)$, unramified Frobenius acts on these roots as $\eta\mapsto\eta^8$. At $i\ge2$ it changes the zero-class sum by a quantity of norm $2^{-49^2}$, coming from $\eta_1$ of order 49. Every permitted linear relabelling fixes the zero class. The geometric equivariance clause therefore rules out using this example as the release’s class table with that base evaluation. ## All the labels at one fixed trait

There is a stronger protection for the field of the *evaluated* class sums. Fix a classical trait and a positive-definite quadratic weight $Q$ on its nonzero lattice $L$, with a fixed positive scale. Choose a finite complete extension $K/\mathbb Q_p$ containing the image of the base stalk under that trait. This is possible in the arithmetic model since a finite-type affine neighborhood has finitely many generators with algebraic $p$-adic images, and specialization of any allowed denominator stays in the same field. The rank-zero case has only the zero label. [Trait and its integral embedding](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/ThetaSetupStructureMapComp.lean#L70-L80)

Here is the paper argument for each sufficiently deep $N$ in the finite Galois class-sum construction. Write $\varphi:B\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$ for the chosen extension of the base evaluation. The finite deck group $G$ has fixed ring the image of the base. For any $\tau\in G_K$, $\tau\varphi=\varphi\sigma$ for some $\sigma\in G$. Indeed for each $b$ the polynomial $\prod_{\sigma\in G}(X-\sigma b)$ has base coefficients. If $\tau\varphi$ differed from every $\varphi\sigma$, an integer linear combination of finitely many distinguishing elements would distinguish it from all of them at once. This contradicts the orbit polynomial for that combination. Isometry of $\tau$ makes $\sigma$ preserve the kernel of residue evaluation, the designated maximal ideal. The source therefore supplies a linear relabelling congruent to the identity modulo $N_1$, fixing the $\kappa$ index. At the reference trait $k$ is the identity. At another fixed trait conjugate the relabelling by $k$ modulo $N$, where `IsUnimodMod` makes it invertible. [Fixed ring and trace setup](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/ApproxOrbitSumsOfGaloisClassSums.lean#L353-L365) · [Evaluation and action](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/ClassSums.lean#L64-L73) · [Invertibility modulo the level](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/IsUnimodMod.lean#L19-L26)

Let $\lambda_1=\min_{v\ne0}\sqrt{Q(v)}$. Choose $R$ containing a lattice basis and all sums of distinct basis vectors in the ball $Q\le R$. Assume $N\lambda_1>3\sqrt R$. Reduction mod $N$ is injective on that ball. Each of its points is the unique minimum in its residue class. Its largest measured absolute value over $\kappa$ therefore equals $\exp(-cQ(v))$ for the trait’s scale $c>0$ by attainment for its leading term, the strict ultrametric inequality, and the actual class-sum relation. Every other class has smaller upper bound than $\exp(-cR)$. Hence the relabelling permutes these short labels preserving their lengths.

Lift the images of the basis vectors and pairwise sums back to the short ball. Congruence respects addition. The difference between the two lifts for a pairwise sum has length at most $3\sqrt R$, so congruence mod $N$ forces actual equality. Polarization recovers the Gram matrix. The lifted basis map is an integral isometry with determinant $\pm1$, congruent to the identity modulo $N_1$. The isometry group is finite by positive definiteness. At $N_1\ge3$ no nonidentity finite-order integral matrix has this congruence. To see it, take a power of prime order and write it $I+\ell^aC$ with $C$ nonzero mod $\ell$ for an odd $\ell\mid N_1$, or use $\ell=2,a\ge2$ if $4\mid N_1$. In the prime-order power, a degree-one binomial term has strictly smaller valuation than all higher terms if the order is $\ell$. For prime order different from $\ell$ it already survives mod $\ell^{a+1}$. This contradicts that power being the identity.

The relabelling on all of $L/NL$ is consequently the identity. All evaluated class values at this fixed trait and depth are fixed by $G_K$ and lie in $K$. For the $E_8\oplus E_8$ example one may take $R=4,\lambda_1=\sqrt2$ before multiplying by the common scale. In a separate finite replay only the identity among 27 determinant-one maps modulo 9 fixing level 3 preserves the short labels of the binary form $x^2+xy+y^2$.
