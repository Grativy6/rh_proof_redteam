## Stronger information on a point coming from the ring

A solution to the scalar trace inequality can be tested numerically at one prime. A point the proof actually constructs satisfies more. Its representation $r$ is integral, agrees entrywise with the prescribed diagonal reduction, and kills every prime-to-five inertia element in the residual kernel, by the definition of the deformation problem. On $G_K$ the residual multiset is $\{\bar\Psi,1,\bar\Psi^{-1}\}$. Type II would give a characteristic-zero rank-two representation $W$ over $\mathbb Q$ whose restriction to $G_K$ is the corner block. The weight-three newform is attached to $W\varepsilon_5^{-1}$ in the release’s geometric Frobenius convention. Its determinant before that Tate twist is finite order, and its trace at complex conjugation in the extended corner is zero. Put $W'=W\otimes\psi$. For every prime $r\ne5,7,q$, inertia is inside $G_K$, and the entire residual representation is trivial on it. Thus $W'$ is unramified there, including at 2 and the extra host prime $d$. At $q$ inertia is also inside $G_K$ and its image factors through $\{1,-1\}$ by the same killed-kernel condition. It therefore acts by $-I$ on the corner. Twisting by $\psi$ removes its ramification at $q$.

At 7 the subgroup $I_7\cap G_K$ acts trivially. Thus $W'$ has inertia of order at most two there and no monodromy. For the classical conductor at 5 one must check monodromy. The attached representation of a classical newform is potentially semistable. ## The single remaining classical form fails

The possible form lies in $S_3(\Gamma_1(7))$. The effective modular group has index 24, six cusps and no elliptic stabilizers. Its genus is zero. All cusps are regular, so the Hodge line bundle has degree $24/12=2$. Weight-three cusp forms are sections of its cube vanishing at the six cusps. That line bundle has degree zero on a genus-zero curve, giving dimension one. The normalized form in it is
$$\eta_{\mathrm{Ded}}(z)^3\eta_{\mathrm{Ded}}(7z)^3
=Q\prod_{n\ge1}(1-Q^n)^3(1-Q^{7n})^3,\qquad Q=e^{2\pi iz}.$$
The eta-product transformation criterion gives weight 3 and character $\chi_{-7}$, and its orders at 0 and infinity for $\Gamma_0(7)$ are both one. Multiplying this product through degree 11 gives $a_5=0$ and $a_{11}=-6$. An independent newspace computation gives the same result. [Integer series calculation](../receipt-archives/check_odd_selmer_precision.py) · [PARI newspace calculation](../receipt-archives/check_odd_class_fields.gp) · [LMFDB 7.3.b.a](https://www.lmfdb.org/ModularForm/GL2/Q/holomorphic/7/3/b/a/)

The prime 11 splits in $K$. After twisting off $\psi$, the two residual corner eigenvalues there are $11$ and $11^{-1}$. Undoing the Tate normalization for the classical coefficient gives $a_{11}\equiv 11^2+1\equiv2\bmod5$. The actual coefficient is $-6\equiv4\bmod5$. Equivalently its proposed rank-three trace is $1-6/11$ for $q=571$, and its difference from $133/11$ is $-128/11$, a 5-adic unit. The sole form also has complex multiplication by $K$, which makes its restriction to $G_K$ reducible, and $a_5=0$ exhibits its failure of ordinarity. Each check is consistent with the same exclusion.

A nearby way to fool the scalar test illustrates why the ring matters. At a good rational prime $p$ inert in $K$ the same level-seven form has $a_p=0$ and determinant $-p^2$ on geometric Frobenius. After normalization, Frobenius squared is the identity on this two-dimensional representation. At the place over $p$ its rank-three trace is 3, and the target is $1+p^2+p^{-2}$ since $\psi(p^2)=1$. Their difference is $-(p^2-1)^2/p^2$. Thus $p=19$ gives valuation 2 and primes $p\equiv-1\bmod5^b$ inert in $K$ give at least $2b$. An arbitrarily accurate trace at such a prime can come from the very form that fails at 11. In particular this comparison using the level-seven form does not certify its membership in the released set of admissible points.
