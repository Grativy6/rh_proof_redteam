# The class lifts and loses its inertia symmetry

**25 November 2026.** Christopher Daniel Pang asked whether a residual trace leaves a further trace of its own. His shadow-lattice paper, PAL and APCI motivated following one arithmetic class to the next digit. Hearthline derived the commutator obstruction and the class-group tower below. Alpöge and Claude supply the original coefficient, seed congruences and the characteristic at which their component argument uses the class. The proof uses classical class field theory, Chevalley’s ambiguous class number formula and the abelian relative class number formula. **Result.** In a family of full seed-compatible frames, the very class produced by the release has unramified lifts over the enlarged field to every precision $5^s$ with $s\le m+1$, where $m$ is its chosen layer. Every lift even to $25$ loses invariance under the order-five inertia action used to pass back towards $\mathbf Q$. The defect forces four linearly independent classes in a specified part of the residual class group. At the infinite layer there is a compatible $5$-adic lift. The release uses its class at the residual precision. ## The actual input and its forced ray character

Use the trivial outer character, $\ell=5$, $\psi=\chi_{-d}$ and $j=1$ in a sufficiently late admissible zeta window. The long-root coefficient is $\kappa=\chi_{-35}$, with field $K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-35})$. Write $T_n$ for the real cyclotomic layer of degree $5^n$ at 5, and $C_w$ for the degree-five subfield of $\mathbf Q(\mu_w)$. Choose a split witness $w$ with $v_5(w-1)=1$, away from ramification in the two already fixed auxiliary fields. Such witnesses exist for **each** host. The release’s seed modulus $Q$ here is prime to 5. Impose $w\equiv1$ modulo the least common multiple of $Q$ and the prime-to-5 character conductors, and $w\equiv6\bmod25$. The Chinese remainder theorem and Dirichlet’s theorem supply infinitely many choices. The construction in the source works with every witness meeting its conditions. The underlying cocycle is forced up to nonzero scalar and coboundary. The source constructs a nonzero cocycle on $G_{\mathbf Q}$ whose restriction to $G_K$ is unramified away from $w$. Restriction to $G_K$ is nonzero because the quotient has order two and 2 is invertible. Conjugation by the nonidentity automorphism of $K$ negates this character. Wild inertia at $w$ has zero image. The class number of $K$ is 2, its units are $\{\pm1\}$, and $w$ splits there. The minus 5-part of the ray group modulo the two primes over $w$ is therefore $C_5$. ## A commutator that has exactly order five after taking coinvariants

Let $V$ be any of the character-trivializing abelian fields in this family containing $KC_w$, with ramification index five at $w$. This includes the final field $V_m$ of the previous paper for the witnesses just chosen. Let $A$ be its 5-primary ideal class group, equivalently its maximal unramified abelian Galois quotient of 5-power order. Conjugation by $\tau$ induces an operator $T$ on $A$. Write $c$ for complex conjugation and $x$ for the image of $[c,\tau]=c\tau c^{-1}\tau^{-1}$ in $A$. Both $\tau^5$ and $c\tau^5c^{-1}$ lie in upstairs inertia and map to zero. The word identity for $[c,\tau^5]$ therefore gives
$$
 (1+T+T^2+T^3+T^4)x=0 .
$$
The global class restricted to $A$ is $T$-invariant and has $y(x)=-2$ in $\mathbf F_5$. This follows from the cocycle identity, $\kappa(c)=-1$ and $\kappa(\tau)=1$. Hence $x$ has **exact order five** in $A/(T-1)A$. A $T$-invariant unramified lift to $\mathbf Z/25$ would have to send $x$ to a multiple of 5, which would reduce to zero. This obstruction applies even to a lift chosen entirely upstairs.

Suppose an arbitrary unramified lift $H:A\to\mathbf Z/25$ exists. Put $U=T-1$. Since $H(Uz)=0\bmod5$, define $d(z)=H(Uz)/5\bmod5$. Expanding the word identity yields
$$
 0=H\big((5+10U+10U^2+5U^3+U^4)x\big),\qquad
 d(U^3x)=-y(x)\ne0 .
$$
In $A/5A$ we have $U^4x=0$ and $U^3x\ne0$. Consequently $x,Ux,U^2x,U^3x$ are independent there. The span is a single length-four Jordan block for $T$. Below we prove that the lift exists for the actual family, so these four directions are present at the selected layer.

## The class group at each layer

Let $B_n=KT_n$, $P_n=C_wT_n$, $M_n=KC_wT_n$ and $A_n=\mathrm{Cl}(M_n)[5^\infty]$. The prime $w$ is inert in every $T_n$ because $v_5(w-1)=1$. It therefore has exactly two primes above it in $B_n$. The 5-class groups of $B_n$ and $P_n$ are trivial. Here are the details.

For a cyclic degree-five extension $Y/X$, the ambiguous class number formula gives the fixed class number as $h_X\prod_v e_v$ divided by $5$ and a unit index. If $5\nmid h_X$ and precisely one finite prime ramifies with index five, the fixed subgroup has trivial 5-part. A nonzero finite 5-group under an action of order five has nonzero fixed points, by counting orbits. Thus $5\nmid h_Y$. Apply this inductively to $T_n$ and to $KT_n$ at the unique prime above 5 in each tower. The quadratic field $K$ is ramified at 5. The new cyclotomic layers are totally ramified there. Apply it to $P_n/T_n$ at the single prime above $w$. These extensions have odd degree, so no infinite prime contributes. [Chevalley’s formula and proof, Lemmermeyer](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.1071)

For $M_n/B_n$ there are two ramified primes, both of index five. The same formula gives $|A_n^T|\le5$. The ray class remains nonzero on $G_{M_n}$ by the commutator calculation, so $|A_n^T|=5$. The norm from $M_n$ down to $B_n$ kills the 5-part, hence $1+T+\cdots+T^4$ annihilates all of $A_n$. Thus $A_n$ is a finite module over the discrete valuation ring $R=\mathbf Z_5[\zeta_5]$, with $T$ acting by $\zeta_5$ and uniformizer $\pi=\zeta_5-1$. Each nonzero cyclic summand contributes a factor of 5 to $|\ker(T-1)|$. There is exactly one summand, with length $e_n$.
$$
 A_n\simeq R/\pi^{e_n},\qquad e_n=v_5(h_{M_n})\ge1 .
$$
The element $x$ above generates this $R$-module since its image under the nonzero $T$-invariant residual character is nonzero.

## At least four new factors of five per layer

For $n\ge1$ choose a Dirichlet character $\beta_n$ of order $5^n$ and conductor $5^{n+1}$, and a character $\xi$ of order 5 and conductor $w$. The new odd characters of $M_n$ form five Galois orbits represented by $\theta_a=\chi_{-35}\beta_n\xi^a$ for $0\le a<5$. The new exponent of $\beta_n$ runs through the units modulo $5^n$. Conjugating it to 1 leaves one representative for each $a$. Each representative has value field $\mathbf Q(\mu_{5^n})$. With primitive conductor $f$, put
$$
 B(\theta)=\frac1f\sum_{t=1}^f t\,\theta(t).
$$
The abelian relative class number formula gives $e_n-e_{n-1}$ as the sum of the five valuations $v_5(N_{\mathbf Q(\mu_{5^n})/\mathbf Q} B(\theta_a))$. The real class numbers have zero 5-valuation by the preceding argument. The Hasse unit indices and factors of 2 are 5-units, and $M_n$ has no fifth roots of unity because its degree is twice a power of five. [Relative class number formula, Hirabayashi, §3](https://cm.osu.cz/sites/default/files/contents/22-2/cm022-2014-2_133-140.pdf)

One can check the required divisibilities directly in these finite sums. With $\pi_n=\zeta_{5^n}-1$, the factor $2-\theta(2)^{-1}$ is a $\pi_n$-unit because $\chi_{-35}(2)=-1$. For any of the odd conductors in use, permuting residues by multiplication by 2 gives
$$
 (2-\theta(2)^{-1})B(\theta)=\sum_{t=1}^f \theta(t)\lfloor 2t/f\rfloor .
$$
This proves integrality at 5, including for the imprimitive character with the same modulus. For $a\ne0$, compare $\theta_a$ with $\chi_{-35}\beta_n$ inflated to the same modulus $7\cdot5^{n+1}w$ and extended by zero at multiples of $w$. Their values agree modulo $\pi_n$, as do the smoothed sums. Removing the multiples of $w$ multiplies $B(\chi_{-35}\beta_n)$ by $1-(\chi_{-35}\beta_n)(w)$. This factor is zero modulo $\pi_n$ because $w$ splits in $K$. Equivalently, this identity follows by summing complete residue blocks and subtracting the multiples of $w$. Hence four of the five norms are divisible by 5 and the fifth has nonnegative valuation. We obtain
$$
 e_n\ge e_0+4n\ge1+4n .
$$

At $w=11$, $e_0=1$. The five exact norms for $n=1$ are $256,403280,27280,43280,4400$. Their 5-valuations sum to 5, so $e_1=6$ and $A_1$ has additive group $C_{25}^2\times C_5^2$. The same finite sums give $e_2=10$. The replay checks the first two increments for 11 and 211 using integer multiplication determinants. The formula proves the bound for every layer. ## Lifting and the action that every lift loses

Since $5$ is a unit times $\pi^4$ in $R$, the subgroup of $A_n$ killed by $5^n$ lies in $\pi A_n$. Every $T$-invariant character to $\mathbf F_5$ vanishes on $\pi A_n$. For a finite abelian 5-group, a character to $\mathbf F_5$ lifts to $\mathbf Z/5^{n+1}$ precisely when it vanishes on the subgroup killed by $5^n$. This follows on each cyclic summand by lifting the value of a generator. Apply it to the marked character of $A_n$. These lifts also vanish on Frobenius at every prime above 5. Indeed complex conjugation acts by $-1$ on $A_n$ since the norm to the real subfield $P_n$ kills it. It fixes each prime above 5 since $K$ is ramified there and the real local field has odd ramification index. The class of any such prime is therefore zero in $A_n$.

At $n=m$ restrict to the release’s field $V_m\supset M_m$. The reduction is still nonzero on the same commutator. To descend from $V_m$ to the release’s real field $L_m^+$ at this precision, project the lift onto the quadratic character $\kappa$ by averaging over $\operatorname{Gal}(V_m/L_m^+)$. Its order is prime to 5. This preserves the residual class, unramifiedness and splitting at 5. The coset-average formula in the [first paper](why-the-auxiliary-prime-loses-both-classes.md) then gives the twisted cocycle. The actual coefficient $\eta\Psi^{-2}$ agrees with this quadratic lift on $G_{L_m^+}$ modulo $5^{m+1}$ because $\psi^2=1$ and $T_m$ kills the 5-primary part of the cyclotomic character at that precision. The inertia action $T$ belongs to the further descent towards $\mathbf Q$. Its coinvariant obstruction persists at $V_m$.

There is also a compatible limit. The class field norm maps carry $x_{n+1}$ to $x_n$ by functoriality of reciprocity on the same absolute commutator. They are $R$-linear and send a generator to a generator. Thus $\varprojlim A_n\simeq R$, with $1$ identified with this compatible generator. On the absolute group of $M_\infty=\bigcup_n M_n$ the resulting map onto $R$ is surjective. The commutator and its inertia conjugates give all the $R/\pi^{e_n}$ at each level, and the image is compact. An additive $\mathbf Z_5$-linear functional on $R$ reducing to the marked character provides a continuous unramified 5-adic lift, split at 5. Every $T$-invariant functional on $R$ kills $\pi R$ and hence has zero image in $\mathbf Z_5$.

The exact obstruction to invariant lifting already occurs at the second digit, even though arbitrarily many digits exist when the field is enlarged. The release’s component step only needs a nonzero residual class and varies it within the characteristic-5 fiber. The solvable automorphy descent takes a characteristic-zero $r$ already defined on $G_{\mathbf Q}$ and assumes irreducibility and automorphy of its restriction. Its input includes the descent action on $r$. Our obstruction concerns extending the particular residual class with that action. The remaining question is how the argument establishes the restricted automorphy for the marked $r$.
