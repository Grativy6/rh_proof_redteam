# Two determinant tests at a component intersection

**Corpus and derivation.** Christopher Daniel Pang’s PAL/APCI question asks which information survives a change of observation. It led here to a comparison of a chosen tangent witness with the fibre of the algebra that carries it. Developed in Pang’s PAL research program with Hearthline, 25 November 2026. [PAL atlas](../corpus/PAL_v2.4-M_Mathematical_Realization_Atlas.docx) · [APCI](../corpus/APCI_Declared_Interface_v1.0.0.docx)

**Proof inputs and the release.** Alpöge and Claude provide the local and patched determinant constructions, dimension thresholds and arithmetic hypotheses. Hearthline derived the three-component example, the one-coefficient counterexample to the weakened lemma and the classification of a single linear hyperplane. The syzygy calculation uses classical Koszul relations.

## What point the global argument uses

In rank three, let $S.A$ be the entry algebra and $S.B$ its weight-zero base. The source calls a prime eligible (`TI`) if some upper, some lower, and some odd-weight off-diagonal entries escape it. Its good locus is cut out by the image of the modular ideal $M$. The core `BB` is the fibre above the maximal ideal of $S.B$ with at least one lower entry nonzero. These definitions allow the actual intersection point used in the proof to lie in residue characteristic $\ell$. [Core](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/LeAdicClosure.lean#L201-L225) · [Eligible and good loci](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/CurveDVRSpecialization/PropagationTheoremGL3.lean#L33-L70)

The core's connectivity has a concrete explanation. On this fibre all weight-zero products involving off-diagonal entries vanish. A nonzero entry of either weight sign forbids entries of the opposite sign, since suitable positive powers of one of each would give a nonzero weight-zero product in the residue field. The lower-triangular data in rank three have two coordinates $(y,a)$ with weights one and two. Matrix multiplication gives a cross term quadratic in $y$ in the equation for $a$. If $b$ is a nonzero allowed pure long-root cocycle, then
$$(y,a)\ \longmapsto\ \big(Ty,\ T^2a+(1-T)b\big)$$
respects the equations over the polynomial ring. Its endpoints are $(y,a)$ and $(0,b)$. The local splitting parameters transform with the same weights. The generic point stays in the core since it specializes to a point with a surviving lower entry. The family and both endpoints share that generization. The frame supplies the nonzero pure long-root class and a separate short-root class used for density away from the even locus. These are arithmetic hypotheses on the selected auxiliary field. [Cocycle and local equations](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/StrSMul.lean#L13-L23) · [Polynomial operation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/StrSMul.lean#L40-L55) · [Actual generic point and endpoints](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/ExistsNonzeroLowCocycle.lean#L144-L192) · [Frame hypotheses](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/ContinuousCoboundaries2.lean#L185-L209)

There are finitely many irreducible components. Separate the eligible ones into those contained in the good locus and the rest. Their two closed unions cover the core using density of the selected points. Each union meets the core if both classes exist. Connectedness gives an intersection in the core. The fibre is of finite type over a field, so the nonempty locally closed intersection contains a closed point after allowing its finite residue-field extension. The source uses that closed point on both unions and invokes its **local** propagation result. The argument requires more than connectedness of a union of components. [Closed-point argument](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/FibreClosed.lean#L45-L58) · [Two closed unions](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/CurveDVRSpecialization/GlobProp.lean#L12-L45)

## The determinant in the first local ring

Write $y=(y_1,\ldots,y_g)^t$ for the odd formal variables in the local power-series presentation and $G=My$ for the odd relations. The source imposes one additional equation $\det M=0$. The adjugate identity shows that every prime at which some $y_i$ survives already contains $\det M$. The desired intersection dimension for successive components is $D=3d+1$. [Presentation and dimension](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/Integral.lean#L71-L101) · [Choice of determinant and adjugate](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/ChainedOfComap.lean#L85-L118)

**The determinant is independent of the factorization.** If $My=M'y=G$ in a formal power-series ring in the coordinate variables $y_i$ over any commutative coefficient ring, then $\det M-\det M'\in(G_1,\ldots,G_g)$. In particular the extra ideal in the presented ring is independent of the chosen matrix for these same ordered relations.

**Proof.** Replace one row at a time. Its difference $v$ satisfies $v\cdot y=0$. Such syzygies are generated by $y_i e_j-y_j e_i$: reduce modulo $y_g$, use induction for the first $g-1$ coordinates, then cancel the nonzerodivisor $y_g$ on the remaining relation. Equivalently, $v$ is the contraction of a two-form by the covector $y$. Wedge that two-form with the other $g-1$ rows. The result is zero in exterior degree $g+1$. Contracting by $y$ expresses the determinant with row $v$ as a sum of multiples of the dot products of the other rows with $y$. Those dot products are the corresponding $G_j$ throughout the replacements. For $g=1$ cancellation gives equality directly. [Koszul relations for a regular sequence](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/062D)

**A component with only an infinitesimal transverse direction.** Take a complete discrete valuation ring $O$ of odd residue characteristic and $D\ge2$. Use even variables $a,d$ and $D-2$ spectators, odd variables $b,c$, and relations
$$G_1=b^2c,\qquad G_2=db+ac.$$
Two coefficient matrices have first rows $(bc,0)$ and $(0,b^2)$, with the same second row $(d,a)$. Their determinants $abc,-db^2$ differ by $bG_2$. The three minimal primes, before and after adding the determinant, are
$$P_1=(a,b),\qquad P_E=(b,c),\qquad P_2=(c,d).$$
Indeed a prime containing $b^2c$ contains $b$ or $c$. The second equation then forces one of these three coordinate ideals, all prime and pairwise incomparable. Each component has dimension $D+1$. The intersections with $P_E$ have dimension $D$. The direct intersection of $P_1$ and $P_2$ has dimension $D-1$. The middle component is indispensable for a chain with the release’s specified intersection dimension in this presentation.

At the generic point of $P_E$, $a,d$ are units. Substitute $c=-db/a$. The first ring gives $b^3=0$ and the determinant adds $b^2=0$. The tangent $b=\epsilon$ is nonzero modulo $\epsilon^2$. It has no extension to a reduced field-valued branch through that generic point with $b\ne0$. Even over the base of the whole middle component the possible linear readings satisfy $d\,db+a\,dc=0$, where $db,dc$ here denote variations. They are precisely $(db,dc)=h(a,-d)$ since $a,d$ are coprime. Every such integral reading specializes to zero at $a=d=0$, where the fibre itself has two independent variations.

In the released argument `Thick` asks for a **representation** on this even component. Taking one off-diagonal column of a derivation gives an honest extension: the cocycle law is linear on a single off-diagonal block since two such matrices multiply to zero. Nonsplitting is tested under **injective** maps from the component domain to fields. At the next stage this witnesses failure of semisimplicity of the Cayley–Hamilton algebra over the generic field. The edge condition then puts that prime below the kernels of its two endpoints. The definition does not require the chosen column to give a nonsplit specialization at both endpoints. [Extension and nonsplitting](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/LeAdicClosure.lean#L255-L267) · [How the derivation gives the representation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/FrobeniusSmoothVectors/ThickOfDer.lean#L95-L121) · [Generic algebra test](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/CurveDVRSpecialization/ThickOf.lean#L170-L195) · [Exact edge hypotheses](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/WeilGroupCharacters/UniversalOrdinaryDeterminantRing.lean#L280-L298)

There is also a direct check in the completed block algebra used at such an endpoint. It has full matrix blocks over $B$ on the diagonal and entries in a finite $B$-module $N$ in both opposite rectangles. Its scalar algebra has the direct-sum decomposition $B\oplus N$, with products of two odd entries in $B$. At a prime take the residue field $K$ and put $V=N\otimes_B K$. Tensor the direct-sum coordinates of the block algebra itself. If $V=0$, the fibre is the product of the two diagonal matrix algebras. If $V\ne0$ and $V V=0$, the rectangles form its nonzero square-zero radical. For blocks of sizes 2 and 1 this radical has dimension $4\dim_K V$. Finally, if $uv\ne0$ for some $u,v\in V$, associativity $(uv)w=u(vw)$ makes every $w$ a scalar multiple of $u$. A generator $t$ then satisfies $t^2=c\ne0$. Rescale one of the two rectangles by $c$ to identify the fibre with the full matrix algebra. These three cases use the multiplication as well as the vector-space dimension. In particular the support of the finite module $N$ is exactly the union of the full-matrix and nonsemisimple cases in this model. If the generic fibre of a component has a radical, $N^2$ and $\operatorname{Ann}_B N$ both lie in its prime, hence also in all its specializations. The radical can survive there even when a particular map out of $N$ specializes to zero. [Completed block presentation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PatchingModules/ClosureConjugation/GrAlg.lean#L13-L29) · [Annihilator used by the source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PatchingModules/ClosureConjugation/GrThick.lean#L77-L85)

## The separate coefficient test in the patched ring

At a two-plus-one point, let $B$ be the base, $A=B\oplus N$ its finite algebra with odd part $N$, and $I_{\rm red}=N^2\subset B$ the ideal generated by odd products. The later lemma `presOut1` uses $g$ generators of $N$ and $D_0$ formal even coordinates. It requires **all $g^2$ coefficient rows** for the linear parts of its relation matrix to be independent in the formal coordinate space. Under the rest of its hypotheses, a large stable prime $Z$ must be contained in every tested prime $Q$. [Exact lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PatchingModules/SelmerScaling/CotangentIndependentOfResidues.lean#L74-L83) · [Application to component propagation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PatchingModules/AutomorphicDetection/GalOut.lean#L16-L37)

**One missing coefficient suffices to break the weakened lemma.** Take $g=2$, $D_0\ge4$, even variables $u,v,w$ and $D_0-3$ spectators, odd variables $b,c$. Set
$$A=O[[u,v,w,\text{spectators},b,c]]/(ub+vc,\ wc),\qquad B=A^{(b,c)\mapsto(-b,-c)}.$$
The odd part is generated by $b,c$ over $B$, and $I_{\rm red}=(b^2,bc,c^2)$. Averaging over the involution splits $A=B\oplus N$. The even algebra is generated as a complete algebra by the even variables and the three odd products, so it is a complete Noetherian local ring with the same residue field as $O$. This also proves the lemma's maximal-ideal generation condition. Its relation matrix is
$$\begin{pmatrix}u&v\\0&w\end{pmatrix}.$$
Exactly three of its four entries are independent to first order. The three minimal primes of $A$ are $(b,c)$, $(u,c)$ and $(w,ub+vc)$. For the last one, the initial polynomial $ub+vc$ after setting $w=0$ is irreducible even over the residue field: as a linear polynomial in $u$, its coefficients $b,vc$ are coprime. The associated graded quotient by this principal initial form is a domain, hence so is the complete local quotient. The list follows by splitting the equation $wc=0$.

Contract $(u,c)$ and $(w,ub+vc)$ to $B$, calling the results $Z,Q$. Both quotients have dimension $D_0+1$ by integrality, both primes miss $I_{\rm red}$, and $u\in Z\setminus Q$. This violates the conclusion $Z\subseteq Q$ while meeting its dimension and stable-locus tests. There is no fourth linear coefficient in disguise. Modulo the uniformizer and terms of higher degree, the rows of relations on $b,c$ lie in the span of $(u,v),(0,w)$. All their entries use just $u,v,w$. The even cotangent space modulo odd products and the uniformizer already has dimension $D_0$, so a spanning list of $D_0$ even coordinates has no spare slot.

**Which single missing equation matters?** In the homogeneous model let a nonzero matrix $C$ specify the missing direction, meaning that the variable $g$-by-$g$ matrices $M$ range over the hyperplane $\sum C_{ij}M_{ij}=0$. Over an algebraically closed field and for $g\ge2$, the reduced incidence set $My=0,\det M=0$ on that hyperplane is irreducible except when $g=2$ and $\operatorname{rank}C=1$.

For fixed $y\ne0$, the hyperplane condition is redundant on $My=0$ precisely when each row of $C$ is proportional to $y^t$. This is impossible for $\operatorname{rank}C\ge2$. Otherwise it happens on a single line of $y$’s. Away from that line the fibres form a vector bundle of rank $g(g-1)-1$ over an irreducible base. Over the exceptional punctured line the total dimension is $1+g(g-1)$. For $g\ge3$ this is too small to be another component: the whole section over $y\ne0$ is a nonzero hypersurface in the smooth $g^2$-dimensional bundle of pairs $My=0$. For $g=2$ a rank-one constraint is equivalent to one zero matrix entry. The two moving components are exactly the ones exhibited above. Every point with $y=0,\det M=0$ is in their collective closure, by keeping $M$ fixed and scaling any nonzero kernel vector to zero. This last classification is for the stated linear incidence model.

## What an actual arithmetic counterexample must defeat

In the release the $g^2$ rows live in the **formal** coordinates. Their evaluated images may be dependent. For a surjection $k^{D_0}\to T$ onto a space of dimension $d_T$, a given list of $g^2$ vectors of rank $r$ in $T$ has independent lifts exactly when $g^2-r\le D_0-d_T$. This follows by sending a basis of the relations of the list into independent kernel directions and lifting a complement. The example above has no such spare direction.

The source’s more precise arithmetic bound is $\dim W+g^2\le D_0$, where $W$ consists of the even tangent classes that lift separately in every indexed coordinate. The construction uses the dual of the constrained tangent space and pads its spanning list when the bound leaves room. Its $g^2$ first-order classes are indexed by two positions: the class $\kappa_i$ and the coefficient slot $\epsilon_j$ in which it is placed. To see why this counts ordered pairs, project a putative polarized coboundary to its constant part. After the independent even classes are removed, this part centralizes both full matrix blocks and is skew under transpose. It is block scalar and zero since 2 is invertible. In each remaining slot $j$, the relation would make a linear combination of the independent $\kappa_i$ a coboundary. All coefficients vanish, column by column. The **upper** arithmetic bound on that first-order space is still needed. ## Unresolved

These examples locate a one-coefficient failure in a weakening of a necessary arithmetic lemma, and an unavoidable even component in the earlier determinant construction. Realizing the deficient matrix at an actual patched arithmetic point would require showing that its Selmer bound fails with the release's auxiliary-prime hypotheses. The [trace calculation on the cyclotomic subgroup](the-trace-kernel-on-the-cyclotomic-subgroup.md) tests one of those hypotheses independently.
